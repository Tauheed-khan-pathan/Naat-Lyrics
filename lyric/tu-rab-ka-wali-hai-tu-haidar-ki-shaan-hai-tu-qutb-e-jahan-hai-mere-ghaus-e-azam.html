Tu Rab Ka Wali Hai Tu Haidar Ki Shaan Hai | Tu Qutb-e-Jahan Hai Mere Ghaus-e-Azam

Manqabat • Shayar: Rizwan Ahmad Mahbubi
Naat Khawan: Sandali Ahmad
Tu rab ka wali hai, tu haidar ki shaan hai Tera rutba ala, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Tu qutb-e-jahaan hai, tu sayyad ki zeenat Tera darja baala, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Tu rab ka wali hai, tu haidar ki shaan hai Tera rutba ala, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Ba-fazl-e-khuda murda zinda kiya hai Jo bekas the un ko sahaara diya hai Karaamat mein ala, fazeelat mein ala 'inaayat mein ala, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Tu rab ka wali hai, tu haidar ki shaan hai Tera rutba ala, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Qalndar ho abdaal ya auliya ho Zamaane ka koi bara rehnuma ho Tera hai qadam sab ki gardan pe, shaaha ! 'ajab rutba tera, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Tu rab ka wali hai, tu haidar ki shaan hai Tera rutba ala, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Mere dil ki hasrat mita deejiye na Zara rukh se parda hata deejiye na Mere dil ko raahat 'ata keejiye na Dikha kar ke jalwa, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Tu rab ka wali hai, tu haidar ki shaan hai Tera rutba ala, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Sataaya gaya hoon, rulaaya gaya hoon Zamaane ke zaalim se tang a gaya hoon Hoon majboor main ab 'ata keeje mujh ko Sheh-e-din ka sadqa, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Tu rab ka wali hai, tu haidar ki shaan hai Tera rutba ala, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Tu ne'mat Khuda ki, tu jalwa Nabi ka Teri zaat bartar, tu hai sab se behtar Tera rang-e-jaudat wafa ka samndar Tu dariya sakha ka, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Tu rab ka wali hai, tu haidar ki shaan hai Tera rutba ala, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Sada de raha hai ye rizwaan Ahmad Karam ki nazar ho ghareebon pe har shab Adab se yahi iltija hai sabhi ka Bana do naseeba, mere Ghaus-e-Azam ! Tu rab ka wali hai, tu haidar ki shaan hai Tera rutba ala, mere Ghaus-e-Azam !
