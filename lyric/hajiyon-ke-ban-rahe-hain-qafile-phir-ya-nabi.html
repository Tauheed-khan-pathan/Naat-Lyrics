Hajiyon Ke Ban Rahe Hain Qafile Phir Ya Nabi

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Haji Muhammad Mushtaq Attari
Hajiyon ke ban rahe hain qafile phir, ya nabi ! Phir nazar mein phir gaye haj ke manazir, ya nabi ! Kar rahe hain jane wale haj ki ab tayyariyan Rah na jaoon main kahin kar do karam phir, ya nabi ! Aah ! palle zar nahin, rakht-e-safar sarwar ! nahin Tum bula lo tum bulane par ho qadir, ya nabi ! Kis-qadar tha khush, mujhe jab pesh aaya tha safar Mujh ko ab ki bar bhi bulwaiye phir, ya nabi ! Dil mera ghamgeen hai aur jan bhi hai muztarib Murshidi ka wasita bulwaiye phir, ya nabi ! Gham ke badal chha rahe hain, aah !  mere qalb par Haziri ki do ijazat mujh ko tum phir, ya nabi ! Gunbad-e-khazra ke jalwe dekhne kab aaoonga Kab tak ab tarpaoge tum mujh ko aakhir, ya nabi ! Aap hi asbab, aaqa ! phir muhayya kijiye Phir dikha dije madine ke manazir, ya nabi ! Kis tarah taskin doonga main dil-e-ghamgeen ko Rah gaya gar haziri se main jo qasir, ya nabi ! Mujh pe kya guzregi, aaqa ! is baras gar rah gaya Mera hal-e-dil to hai sab tum pe zahir, ya nabi ! Aah ! tayba se agar main door rah kar mar gaya Rooh bhi ranjoor hogi kis-qadar phir, ya nabi ! Misl-e-sabiq is baras bhi kijiye nazar-e-karam Main guzishta sal aaya tha bil-aakhir, ya nabi ! Jo madine ke liye rahte hain, aaqa ! be-qarar Wo bhi ho jayein tere rauze pe hazir, ya nabi ! Chak sina, chak dil, soz-e-jigar aur chashm-e-tar Dijiye mujh ko tufail-e-abd-e-qadir, ya nabi ! Bil-yaqin quraan azamat par tumhari hai gawah Shahid-o-qasim ho tum tayyib ho tahir, ya nabi ! Tum ko ilm-e-ghaib maula ne ata farma diya Kar diya har ja pe hazir aur nazir, ya nabi ! Istiqamat din par mujh ko ata farmaiye Ya rasoolallah ! bara-e-aal-e-yasir, ya nabi ! Aap sab nabiyon mein afazal aur main badkar aah ! Aasiyon mein hoon yagana aur nadir, ya nabi ! Aasi-o-badkar ke apane khuda-e-pak se Bakhshawa do sab saghair aur kabair, ya nabi ! Bal bhi bika na mera to koi kar payega Kyoon ki mere tum ho hami aur nasir, ya nabi ! Tishnagan-e-did ko ho did ki sharbat ata Az-tufail-e-ghaus-e-aa'zam abd-e-qadir, ya nabi ! Aah ! mera kya banega gar na haj par ja saka Ho karam attar par ho jaye hazir, ya nabi !
