Ajmer Bulaya Mujhe Ajmer Bulaya

Manqabat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Zaid Attari
Ajmer bulaya mujhe ajmer bulaya Ajmer bula kar mujhe mehman banaya Ho shukr ada kaise ki mujh papi ko, khwaja ! Ajmer bula kar mujhe darbar dikhaya Sultan-e-madina ki mohabbat ka bhikari Ban kar main, shaha ! aap ke darbar mein aaya Duniya ki hukoomat do na daulat do na sarwat Har chiz mili jam-e-mohabbat jo pilaya Qadmon se laga lo mujhe qadmon se laga lo Khwaja ! hai zamane ne bara mujh ko sataya Dooba abhi dooba mujhe lillah sanbhalo Sailab gunahon ka bare zor se aaya Ho chashm-e-shifa ab to, shaha ! soo-e-marizan Isyan ke maraz ne hai bara zor dikhaya Sarkar-e-madina ka bana dijiye aashiq Ye arz liye, shah ! karachi se main aaya Ya khwaja ! karam kijiye, hon zulmatein kafoor Batil ne bare zor se sar apana uthaya Attar karam hi se tere jam ke khara hai Dushman ne girane ko bara zor lagaya
