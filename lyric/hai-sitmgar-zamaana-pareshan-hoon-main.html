Hai Sitmgar Zamaana Pareshan Hoon Main

Naat • Shayar: Mufti Abdullah bin Abbas
Naat Khawan: Furqaan Wazeer
Hai Sitmgar Zamaana, Pareshan Hoon Main Kamli Waale Ki Nagri Mein Ghar Chaahiye Jis Subh Haaziri Ja Ke Rauze Pe Ho Han Mujhe Ek Aisi Sahar Chaahiye Un Ki Yaadon Ka Markaz Hai Seena Mera Door Rah Kar To Mushkil Hai Jeena Mera Sabz-Gumbad Tale Ho Kabhi Haaziri Wo Gali Chaahiye, Wo Nagar Chaahiye Hai Sitmgar Zamaana, Pareshan Hoon Main Kamli Waale Ki Nagri Mein Ghar Chaahiye Hai Hasin Chaand Bhi Maanta Hoon Magar Us Pe Hain Chhaaiyan A Raha Hai Nazar Jo Hua Godi-e-Aamina Mein Tuloo' Bas Hamen Ek Wo Hi Qamar Chaahiye Hai Sitmgar Zamaana, Pareshan Hoon Main Kamli Waale Ki Nagri Mein Ghar Chaahiye Un Ki Yaaden Hasin-Tar Sataayen Bahut Dooriyon Ke Ye Lamhen Rulaayen Bahut Jis Se Mehboob Ko Ru-B-Ru Dekh Lun Mere Maula ! Mujhe Wo Nazar Chaahiye Hai Sitmgar Zamaana, Pareshan Hoon Main Kamli Waale Ki Nagri Mein Ghar Chaahiye Jaan De Dun Main, Aaqa ! Tere Naam Par Phir Karun Fakhr Main Apne Is Kaam Par Be-Khatar Kood Jaaun Bade Shauq Se Aatish-e-'Ishq Ka Ik Bahar Chaahiye Hai Sitmgar Zamaana, Pareshan Hoon Main Kamli Waale Ki Nagri Mein Ghar Chaahiye Pur-Fitan Daur Hai, Zulm Ka Zor Hai Kufr Ki Saazishen, Fisq Ka Shor Hai Ho Muyassar Riyaasat-e-Madina Hamen Aur Phir Se Hamen Ik 'Umar Chaahiye Hai Sitmgar Zamaana, Pareshan Hoon Main Kamli Waale Ki Nagri Mein Ghar Chaahiye Dekh Lun, Ibn-e-'Abbaas !
