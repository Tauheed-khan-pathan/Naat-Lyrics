Bhejo Na Mujhe Wapas Tum Shah-e-Ata Ho Kar

Naat • Shayar: Gulfam Raza Hassani
Naat Khawan: Gulfam Raza Hassani
bhejo n mujhe waapas tum shah-e-'ata ho kar mar jaaunga ro ro kar main tum se juda ho kar lagta tha n dil mera tasbeeh-o-'ibaadat mein qaidi tha, mere maula ! main qafs-e-zalaalat mein ud aaya tere dar par pinjre se riha ho kar mar jaaunga ro ro kar main tum se juda ho kar maana hai zamaane ki shaahi se ise behtar ban jaaun agar tere naukar ka jo main naukar kar loonga basar apni tukdon pe raza ho kar mar jaaunga ro ro kar main tum se juda ho kar hai chaand ko kya ! us ki auqaat dikha bhi do sarkaar mere ! parda chehre se hata bhi do ab aur n tadpaao tum noor-e-khuda ho kar mar jaaunga ro ro kar main tum se juda ho kar khole n agar ab bhi qismat ki mere taale haalaat pe hans hans ke bolenge jahaan waale maara hua phirta hai daata ka gada ho kar mar jaaunga ro ro kar main tum se juda ho kar tarkeeb to pyaari hai, gulfaam ! ye jeene ki khwaahish hai ki paakeeza sehra-e-madeena ki mitti mein main mil jaaun, ai kaash ! fana ho kar mar jaaunga ro ro kar main tum se juda ho kar
