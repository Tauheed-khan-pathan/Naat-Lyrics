Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain

Naat • Shayar: Muhammad Asim-ul-Qadri Muradabadi
Naat Khawan: Haafiz Taahir Qaadri
Shab Hai Me'raaj Ki, Kya Ada Hai ! Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Josh Par Rehmat-e-Kibriya Hai, Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Shab Hai Me'raaj Ki, Kya Ada Hai ! Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Bheeni Bheeni Hawa Chaar-Su Hai, Mehki Mehki Faza Chaar-Su Hai Aur Mausam Suhaana Hua Hai, Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Shab Hai Me'raaj Ki, Kya Ada Hai ! Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Rab Ko Me'raaj Mein Mustafa Ne Apne Maathe Ki Aankhon Se Dekha Ye Hadeeson Se Saabit Hua Hai, Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Shab Hai Me'raaj Ki, Kya Ada Hai ! Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Aaj Jibreel Ne Bhi Labon Se Pyaare Aaqa Ke Talwon Ko Chooma Allah-Allah, Kya Martaba Hai ! Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Shab Hai Me'raaj Ki, Kya Ada Hai ! Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Shaan Dekho Makeen-e-Madina La-Makan Rab Se Milne Chale Hain Ye Sharaf Aap Hi Ko Mila Hai, Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Shab Hai Me'raaj Ki, Kya Ada Hai ! Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Ambiya De Rahe Hain Salaami, Kar Rahe Hain Farishte Ghulaami Har Taraf Shor Salle-'Ala Hai, Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Shab Hai Me'raaj Ki, Kya Ada Hai ! Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Ja Ke Sidra Pe Jibreel Bole, Aage Jaaoonga To Par Jalenge Ya Nabi ! Ye Meri Intiha Hai, Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Shab Hai Me'raaj Ki, Kya Ada Hai ! Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Yun Hua 'Arsh Par Un Ka Jaana, Ek Hi Lamhe Mein Laut Aana Kitna Dil-Chasp Ye Waaqi'a Hai, Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Shab Hai Me'raaj Ki, Kya Ada Hai ! Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain 'Aasim-Ul-Qaadri ! Tere Lab Par Naghma Me'raaj Ka Jo Hai Aaya Ye Habeeb-e-Khuda Ki 'Ata Hai, Mustafa Aaj Doolha Bane Hain Shab Hai Me'raaj Ki, Kya Ada Hai !
