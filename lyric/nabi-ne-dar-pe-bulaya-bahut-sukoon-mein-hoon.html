Nabi Ne Dar Pe Bulaya Bahut Sukoon Mein Hoon

Naat • Shayar: Junaid Qasmi
Naat Khawan: Rao Ali Hasnain
madeena madeena ! madeena madeena ! chamak rahe hain sitaare, 'urooj par qismat nazar ke saamne un ka dayaar jo aaya nabi ne dar pe bulaaya, bahut sukoon mein hoon mera naseeb jagaaya, bahut sukoon mein hoon madeene ka safar hai aur main nam-deeda nam-deeda jabeen afsurda afsurda, qadam laghzeeda laghzeeda chala hoon ek mujrim ki tareh main jaanib-e-tayba nazar sharminda sharminda, badan larzeeda larzeeda udaasiyon mein guzarti thi zindagi pehle udaasiyon ko mitaaya, bahut sukoon mein hoon nabi ne dar pe bulaaya, bahut sukoon mein hoon madeene aane se pehle to be-qaraari thi madeene aaya to paaya, bahut sukoon mein hoon nabi ne dar pe bulaaya, bahut sukoon mein hoon kisi ne poochha ki kis haal mein ho ? kaise ho ? to haal dil ne sunaaya, bahut sukoon mein hoon nabi ne dar pe bulaaya, bahut sukoon mein hoon junaid-e-qaasmi ! dekha jo rauza-e-anawar qaraar rooh ko aaya, bahut sukoon mein hoon nabi ne dar pe bulaaya, bahut sukoon mein hoon madeena madeena ! madeena madeena !
