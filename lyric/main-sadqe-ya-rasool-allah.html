Main Sadqe Ya Rasool Allah

Naat • Shayar: Fatima Najeeb
Naat Khawan: Atif Aslam
nami-daanam kuja raftam, safar sadiyon se jaari hai mere aaqa ! mohabbat aap ki is dil pe taari hai koi samjhe, nahin mumkin, kisi ko kaise samjhaaun jo mere dil ki haalat hai, faqat aaqa ko batlaaun kisi ke bhi gumaan mein ho, nahin mumkin, nahin mumkin kisi ko bhi jahaan mein ho, nahin mumkin, nahin mumkin mujhe hai aap se jitni mohabbat, ya rasoolallaah ! mujhe hai aap se jitni mohabbat, ya rasoolallaah ! main sadqe, ya rasoolallaah ! main sadqe, ya rasoolallaah ! hue hain lafz bhi saakin, nameen aankhon mein thehri hai mohabbat aap ki zamazam ke kunwein se bhi gehri hai s'i karti hain ye aankhein teri galiyon, tere dar ki kabhi na'lain ko choomein, kabhi masjid ke mimbar ki kisi ke bhi gumaan mein ho, nahin mumkin, nahin mumkin kisi ko bhi jahaan mein ho, nahin mumkin, nahin mumkin mujhe hai aap se jitni mohabbat, ya rasoolallaah ! mujhe hai aap se jitni mohabbat, ya rasoolallaah ! main sadqe, ya rasoolallaah ! main sadqe, ya rasoolallaah ! ya rasoolallaah ! ya habeeballaah ! ya rasoolallaah ! ya habeeballaah !
