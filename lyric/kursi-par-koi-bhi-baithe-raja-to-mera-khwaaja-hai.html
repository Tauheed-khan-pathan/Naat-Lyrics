Kursi Par Koi Bhi Baithe Raja To Mera Khwaaja Hai

Manqabat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Unknown
kursi par koi bhi baithe, raaja to mera khwaaja hai mai.n ghada-e-khwaaja-e-chisht hu.n mujhe is ghadaaee pe naaz hai mera naaz khwaaja pe kyu.n na ho mera khwaja banda-nawaaz hai us ke karam ke sab hei.n bhikaari kya raaja mahaaraaja hai saare hind ka hai raaja, mera khwaaja mahaaraaja haidar ka laadla hai, wo zahra ka laal hai beshak mera mu'een muhammad ki aal hai deewaano.n ko kis baat ka aakhir malaal hai khwaaja ko apni praja ka pura khayaal hai saare hind ka hai raaja, mera khwaaja mahaaraaja har aankh chaahti hai ziyaarat mu'een ki har dil mei.n bas gayi hai mohabbat mu'een ki is sar-zamin-e-hind ke shaaho.n ne keh diya mehshar talak rahegi hukumat mu'een ki saare hind ka hai raaja, mera khwaaja mahaaraaja ham gharibo.n ki sadaao.n ne bulaaya hai tujhe hind ka shaah muhammad ne banaaya hai tujhe kaise aaye-ga koi harf hukumat pe teri panjatan paak ne kursi par bithaaya hai tujhe saare hind ka hai raaja, mera khwaaja mahaaraaja pyaara hai hasanain ka beshak nabi ki aal hai sanjar wala peer mera saiyeeda ka laal hai mast mastaan hai har haal mei.n khush haal chishti-e-daaman ko jo pakda wo maalaamaal hai saare hind ka hai raaja, mera khwaaja mahaaraaja tu dar-ba-dar ki thokarei.n ek baar khaake dekh milta hai kya kisi se zara aazmaake dekh tu jin se mil raha hai ye saare ghulam hei.n raaja ko dekhna hai to ajmer jaake dekh kursi par koi bhi baithe, raaja to mera khwaaja hai saare hind ka hai raaja, mera khwaaja mahaaraaja dar-e-khwaaja pe sawaali ko khada rehne do sar nadaamat se juka hai to juka rehne do mujhko mil jaaye ga sadka mai.nchala jaaunga kaasa-e-dil mera qadmo.n mei.n pada rehne do khud hi farmaaeinge mujrim pe wo rahmat ki nazar mujhko khwaaja ki adaalat mei.n pada rehne do kursi par koi bhi baithe, raaja to mera khwaaja hai saare hind ka hai raaja, mera khwaaja mahaaraaja
