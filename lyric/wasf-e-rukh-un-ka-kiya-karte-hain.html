Wasf-e-Rukh Un Ka Kiya Karte Hain

Naat • Shayar: Ala Hazrat
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Wasf-e-rukh un ka kiya karte hain Sharh-e-Wal-Shams Wa Duha karte hain Un ki hum madh o sana karte hain Jin ko Mahmud kiya karte hain Mah-e-shaq gashta ki surat dekho Kanp kar mehr ki raj'at dekho Mustafa pyare ki qudrat dekho Kaise i'jaz huwa karte hain Tu hai khursheed-e-risalat pyare Chhup gaye teri ziya mein tare Anbiya aur hain sab mah pare Tujh se hi noor liya karte hain Ae bala be-khiradi-e-kuffar Rakhte hain aise ke haq mein inkar Ke gawahi ho gar us ko darkar Be-zaban bol utha karte hain Apne maula ki hai bas shan-e-azeem Janwar bhi karein jin ki ta'zeem Sang karte hain adab se tasleem Ped sajde mein gira karte hain Rif'at-e-zikr hai tera hissa Donon alam mein hai tera charcha Murgh-e-firdaus pas az hamd-e-khuda Teri hi madh o sana karte hain Ungliyan payin woh pyari pyari Jin se dariya-e-karam hain jari Josh par aati hai jab gham-khwari Tishna seerab huwa karte hain Han yahin karti hain chiriyan faryad Han yahin chahti hai hirni dad Isi dar par shutran-e-nashad Gilah-e-ranj o ina karte hain Aasteen rahmat-e-alam ulte Kamar-e-pak pe daman bandhe Girne walon ko koojah-e-dozakh se Saaf alag kheench liya karte hain Jab saba aati hai taiba se idhar Khilkhila padti hain kaliyan yaksar Phool jama se nikal kar bahir Rukh-e-rangin ki sana karte hain Tu hai woh badshah-e-kaun o makan Ke malak haft falak ke har an Tere maula se shah-e-arsh eywan Teri daulat ki dua karte hain Jis ke jalwe se uhud hai taban Ma'dan-e-noor hai is ka daman Hum bhi is chand pe ho kar qurban Dil-e-sangin ki jila karte hain Kyun na zeeba ho tujhe tajwari Tere hi dam ki hai sab jalwa-gari Malak o jinn o bashar hur o pari Jaan sab tujh pe fida karte hain Toot padti hain balayein jin par Jin ko milta nahin koi yawar Har taraf se woh pur arman phir kar Un ke daman mein chhupa karte hain Lab par aajata hai jab naam-e-Muhammad Munh mein ghul jata hai shahd-e-nayab Wajd mein ho ke hum ae jaan betab Apne lab choom liya karte hain Lab pe kis munh se gham-e-ulfat layein Kya bala dil hai alam jis ka sunayein Hum to in ke kaf-e-pa par mit jayein Un ke dar par jo mita karte hain Apne dil ka hai unhein se aaram Saunpe hain apne unhein ko sab kaam Law lagi hai ke ab is dar ke
