Mere Maula Teri Baat Hi Aur Hai

Hamd • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Rizwan Khan
Donon Aalam ka Malik hai Tu Aye Khuda Mere Maula! Teri baat hi aur hai Hamd Teri kare Koi Insan kya Mere Maula!
