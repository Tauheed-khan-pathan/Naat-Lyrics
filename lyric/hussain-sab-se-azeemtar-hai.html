Hussain Sab Se Azeemtar Hai

Manqabat • Shayar: Shauq Faridi
Naat Khawan: Sahil Raza Qadri, Muhammad Firdos Qadri
Hai mera Maula ya Husain Hai tera Maula ya Husain Aaqa ka pyaara ya Husain Hai sab ka Maula ya Husain Husain ka zikr 'arsh par hai Husain sab se 'azeem-tar hai Ye hukm-e-sultaan-e-behar-o-bar hai Husain sab se 'azeem-tar hai Janaab-e-maula Hasan biraadar Janaab-e-zehra hai jis ki maadar Janaab-e-maula 'ali pidar hai Husain sab se 'azeem-tar hai Jalaal dekha Husain ka jab Pukaar uttha yazeedi lashkar Janaab-e-haidar ka ye pisar hai Husain sab se 'azeem-tar hai Zubaan pe quraaan ki hai tilaawat Ye dekh kar hai jahaan mein hairat Ki nok-e-neza pe rakkha sar hai Husain sab se 'azeem-tar hai Jo baaghi deen-e-Rasool ka hai Jo baaghi ibn-e-batool ka hai Wo aaj bhi phirta dar-ba-dar hai Husain sab se 'azeem-tar hai Faqat Khuda ki Raza ki khaatir Nabi ke din ki baqa ki khaatir Luta diya jis ne apna ghar hai Husain sab se 'azeem-tar hai Chala jo maidaan mein le ke khanjar To tharthraaya yazeedi lashkar Wo sher-e-haidar hai, wo nidar hai Husain sab se 'azeem-tar hai Hua hai hairaan jahaan saara Jo ek kaase mein saagar aaya Karam ye us ka hi Khwaja par hai Husain sab se 'azeem-tar hai Jahaan se, shauq-e-fareedi ! Wallaah Yazeed ki nasl mit chuki hai Hamaara Maula magar amar hai Husain sab se 'azeem-tar hai
