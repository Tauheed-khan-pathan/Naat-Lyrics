Mere Aaqa Madine Bula Lijiye

Naat • Shayar: Aalam
Naat Khawan: Hafiz Tahir Qadri
Qismat Meri Chamka Do, Ya Shah-e-Madina, Bigdi Meri Bana Do, Ya Shah-e-Madina Mere Aaqa! Madine Bula Lijiye, Sahara Chahiye Sarkar Zindagi Ke Liye Tadap Raha Hoon Madine Ki Haazri Ke Liye, Taiba Ke Jaane Wale, Jaa Kar Bade Adab Se Mera Bhi Qissa-e-Gham Kehna Shah-e-Arab Se, Kehna Ke Shah-e-Aalam, Ek Ranj-o-Gham Ka Mara Dono Jahan Mein Jiska Hain Aap Hi Sahara, Haalat-e-Pur-Alam Se Is Dam Guzar Raha Hai Aur Kampte Labon Se Fariyad Kar Raha Hai, Baar-e-Gunah Apna Hai Dosh Par Uthaye Koi Nahi Hai Aisa Jo Poochne Ko Aaye, Bhula Hua Musafir, Manzil Ko Dhundhta Hai Taarikiyon Mein Maah-e-Kamil Ko Dhundhta Hai, Seene Mein Hai Andhera, Dil Hai Siyah Khana Yeh Hai Meri Kahani Sarkar Ko Sunana, Kehna Mere Nabi Se, Mehrum Hoon Khushi Se Sar Par Ek Abr-e-Gham Hai, Ashkon Se Aankh Nam Hai, Paamal-e-Zindagi Hoon, Sarkar Ummati Hoon Ummat Ke Rehnuma Ho, Kuch Arz-e-Haal Sun Lo, Fariyad Kar Raha Hoon, Main Dil-Figar Kab Se Mera Bhi Qissa-e-Gham Kehna Shah-e-Arab Se, Huzoor Aisa Koi Intezam Ho Jaye Salam Ke Liye Hazir Ghulam Ho Jaye, Sahara Chahiye Sarkar Zindagi Ke Liye Tadap Raha Hoon Madine Ki Haazri Ke Liye, Mera Dil Tadap Raha Hai, Mera Jal Raha Hai Seena Ke Dawa Wahin Milegi, Mujhe Le Chalo Madina, Nahi Maal-o-Zar To Kya Hai, Main Ghareeb Hoon Yahi Na Mere Ishq Mujh Ko Le Chal, Tu Hi Janib-e-Madina, Aaqa Na Toot Jaye Ye Dil Ka Aabgeena Ab Ke Baras Bhi Maula Reh Jaoon Main Kahin Na, Dil Ro Raha Hai Jinka, Aansoo Chalak Rahe Hain Un Aashiqon Ka Sadqa Bulwaiye Madina, Mere Aaqa! Madine Bula Lijiye Madine Jaoon Phir Aaoon Dobara Phir Jaoon, Ke Zindagi Meri Yunhi Tamaam Ho Jaye
