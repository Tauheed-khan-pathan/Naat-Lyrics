Madad Karo Fizza

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Mir Hassan Mir
madad karo fizza madad karo fizza shikasta pehlu se mere ye dar hata do khudara madad karo fizza tumhari zehmatein jaldi se ab tamaam karun main jaate jaate jahan se unhe salaam karun wo dekho aa gaye baba utha do de ke sahara madad karo fizza gira hai toot ke aangan mein goshwara mera hai kaan zakhmi sitamgar ne is tarha maara ali na dekhe khudara chupa do zakhm ye mera madad karo fizza chalo madine mein ek ek ghar pe jayenge ghadeer kya hai unhe yaad hum dilayenge fidak to chin gaya mera ye haq na cheene ali ka madad karo fizza mareez e maut ke jaisi naheef lagti hoo mai aaj tum se zyada zaeef lagti hoo laga hai jab se tamacha mujhe nazar nahi aata madad karo fizza sahara de ke mujhe le chalo khudara wahan wo rassi baandh ke hyder ko le gaye hai jahan na ab ho dayr khudara hai mera maula akela madad karo fizza ye baat soch ke dil gham se paara paara hai hassan ke saamne zaalim ne mujhko maara hai bata do kaise karungi bhala mai saamna uska madad karo fizza mai baal khol ke arsh e ulaa hila dungi abul hassan ko na choda to bad'dua dungi dua ke waaste mera ye haath utha do khudara madad karo fizza hui ye keh ke takallum khamosh wo bibi mai apne laal se rukhsat na ho sakungi kabhi lita do jaanib e qibla hussain aata hi hoga madad karo fizza
