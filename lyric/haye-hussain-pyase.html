Haye Hussain Pyase

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Hadi Ali Khan Muslim
haye hussain pyase hussain jaane payambar dilbare zehra apna bhara ghar tu ne lutaya ibne ali ehsaan hai tera tu hai muhafiz kaaba e haq ka sajde mein sar tera kata haye hussain pyase hussain dhoond rahe hai ran mein sarwar bechaini se laasha e akbar khaate hai har gaam pa thokar kaun sahara dega bad kar aalam e ghurbat dasht e bala haye hussain pyase hussain zainab ki aankhon ke ujaale aun o mohamed ran ko sidhare jang hui aur khoon mein nahaye maqtal se jab aaye laashe karti hai zainab shukre khuda haye hussain pyase hussain byaah hui kuch dayr na guzri ran ko chali qasim ki sawari kubra baithi soch rahi thi ab ujhdegi maang hamari aa gaya dulha ka laasha haye hussain pyase hussain thaame kamar ye kehte thay sarwar dashte bala mein shaane kata kar qatl huwe abbas e dilawar de pursa mujhko meri khwahar hogaya zainab mai tanha haye hussain pyase hussain bain yehi karti thi sakina chod gaye kyun mujhko tanha badta hai jangal ka andhera aajao aajao baba deti hoo kab se tum ko sada haye hussain pyase hussain soti thi seene pa tumhare tum ne mere naaz uthaye shimr ne maare mujhko tamache khoon tapakta hai kaano se neend nahi aati baba haye hussain pyase hussain kaisi ghadi hai aale nabi par ghash mein pade hai abid e muztar keh de koi abbas se jaa kar cheen raha hai shimr e sitamgar binte ali ke sar se rida haye hussain pyase hussain zainab ke baazu mein rasan hai aur labon par bas ye sukhan hai laash teri be-gor o kafan hai aaj bahot majboor bhan hai sar pa nahi chadar bhaiya haye hussain pyase hussain hum pa ye ehsaan hai zainab ka jis ne diya majlis ka saleeqa karte raho matam pyason ka denge tumhe abbas sahara binte payambar dengi dua haye hussain pyase hussain chalke kuch itne aankhon ke saagar phaili kuch itni ashkon ki chaadar chashme falak hairaan hai is par soch rahe hai saare samandar kitne hai aakhir ashk e aza haye hussain pyase hussain zeest ka maqsad pyason ka gham hota rahe ta hashr ye matam le ke yunhi abbas ka parcham parwane shabbir ke har dam dete rahenge bas ye sada haye hussain pyase hussain kije rasheed is gham ki hifazat ashq e aza hai qalb ki khuwwat karte rahe har dam ye ibadat matam hai shabbir ki nusrat goonji faza mein bas ye sada haye hussain pyase hussain
