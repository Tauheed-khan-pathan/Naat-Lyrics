Hum Hain Iltaja Wale

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Syed Altaf Shah Kazmi
Hum hain Iltijaa waalay, Aap hain ata waalay, Bheek kuch ataa karna, aye meray sakha walay. Cher na hamein dunya, ham hain Mustafa (SAW) waalay, Sayyidul Wara waley, Shah e Do Sara waalay. Mein mareez e taiba hoon, us galli may lay jao, Haan wohi gali jis kay, zaray hay shifaa waalay. Khud kaha meray Rab (SWT) nay, bakhsh doun ga main sab ko, Par aain yaar kay dar par, yeh jithnay hain khata waalay. Mar to jayein gay lekin, ghair say na mangain gay, Aaqa aap kay bhikari to, hain bari anaa waalay. Waqt kay salaami bhi, bheek lenay aatay hain, Aray taajdaar aisay hain, yay khaak e naqsh e paa waalay. Maal o zar ki yeh Altaf, kuch nahinn hamain parwaa, Array jaan bhi lutatay hain, admi wafa waalay. Cheir na hamain dunya, ham hain Mustafa (SAW) waalay, Cheir na hamain dunya, ham hain Mustafa (SAW) waalay,
