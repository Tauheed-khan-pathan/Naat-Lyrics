Aye Zahra Ke Baba Sunen Iltija Madina Bula Lijiye

Naat • Shayar: Sayyed Shabahat Hussain
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Ali Faizi
Ae Zahra ke Baba! Sunen iltija Madina bula lijiye Kahin mar na jaae ghulaam aap ka Madina bula lijiye Sataati hai mujh ko, rulaati hai mujh ko Ye duniya bahut aazmaati hai mujh ko Hun duniya ki baaton se toota hua Madina bula lijiye Badi bekasi hai, badi be-qaraari Na kat jaae, Aaqa! Yunhi umr saari Kahan zindagaani ka hai kuchh pata Madina bula lijiye Ye ehsaas hai mujh ko, main hun kameena Huzoor! Aap chahein to aaun Madina Gunaahon ke daldal mein main hun phansa Madina bula lijiye Main dekhoon wo rauza, main dekhoon wo jaali Bula leeje mujh ko bhi, Sarkaar-e-Aali! Kahan jaae, Aaqa! Ye mangta bhala Madina bula lijiye Wo ramzaan tera, wo daalaan tera Wo ajwa, wo zamzam, ye mehmaan tera Tere dar pe iftaar ka wo maza Madina bula lijiye Jahan ke sabhi zarre shams-o-qamar hain Jahan pe Abu-Bakr-o-Usmaan, Umar hain Jahan jalwa-farma hain Hamza chacha Madina bula lijiye Na itna main maa aur baba ko chaahoon Tumhein jitna chaahoon, kisi ko na chaahoon Mere baal-bachche hon tum par fida Madina bula lijiye Hua hai jahan se jahan ye munawwar Jahan aae Jibreel Quraan le kar Mujhe dekhna hai wo Ghaar-e-Hira Madina bula lijiye Jise sab hain kehte Naqi Khan ka beta Wo Ahmad Raza hai Bareli mein leta Usi Aala Hazrat ka hai waasta Madina bula lijiye Ata ho Baqi mein ye Zahra ka sadqa Mujhe maut aae wahin kaash, Aaqa! Padha dein wahin par janaaza mera Madina bula lijiye Karam kar diya hai ye Khwaja piya ne Jo misre likhe hain Shabahat Miyan ne Karein darguzar jo hui ho khata Madina bula lijiye
