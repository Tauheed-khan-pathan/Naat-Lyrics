Khuda Ki Rehmat Baras Rahi Hai

Naat • Shayar: Zainul Abideen Kanpuri
Naat Khawan: Zainul Aabideen Kaanpuri
Khuda Ki Rehmat Baras Rahi Hai, Huzoor Tashreef La Rahe Hain Farishte Ghar Aamina Ka Ja Kar Khud Apne Haathon Saja Rahe Hain Nabi Ka Bachpan Tha Kya Salona Ki Chaand Tha Aap Ka Khilauna Kabhi Khilaune Ko Todte Hain, Kabhi Khilauna Bana Rahe Hain Dukaanen Bu-Jahal Ki Hain Jitni, Wo Ek Bhi Ab Nahin Khulengi The Jis Mein Jhoote Khuda Usi Mein Huzoor Taala Laga Rahe Hain Bashar Bashar Un Ko Kehne Waalo ! Bashar Ki Koi Misaal To Do Wo Doobe Sooraj Ko Samt-e-Maghrib Se Dekho Waapas Bula Rahe Hain Saba Ne Poochha, Aye Phool ! Tera Libaas Kitna Mahak Raha Hai To Phool Bola, Nabi Ke Talwon Ki Ham Bhi Khairaat Pa Rahe Hain Unhin Ka Kaa'ba, Unhin Ka Tayba, Unhin Ki Duniya, Unhin Ka 'Uqba Ye Sach Hai, Zainul !
