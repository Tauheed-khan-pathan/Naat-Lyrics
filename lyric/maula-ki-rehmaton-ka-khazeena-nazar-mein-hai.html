Maula Ki Rehmaton Ka Khazeena Nazar Mein Hai

Naat • Shayar: Hafiz Mazharuddin
Naat Khawan: Khalid Hasnain Khalid, Syed Hassan Ullah Hussaini
Maula Ki Rahmaton Ka Khazeena Nazar Mein Hai Salle-'Ala Ki Shehr-e-Madina Nazar Mein Hai Toofaan Nazar Mein Hai Na Safeena Nazar Mein Hai Tera Karam Hi, Shaah-e-Madina! Nazar Mein Hai Dekhe Koi Huzoor Ki Banda-Nawaaziyaan Mujh Sa Zaleel, Mujh Sa Kameena Nazar Mein Hai Husn-e-Azal Farogh-e-Abad Dekhta Hoon Main Rangeeni-e-Jamaal-e-Madina Nazar Mein Hai Ya Rab! Nawaaz Daulat-e-Soz-o-Gudaaz Se Bu-Zar Ka Dil, Bilaal Ka Seena Nazar Mein Hai Bint-e-Rasool! Teri Ghiza Yaad Hai Mujhe Naan-e-Sha'eer-o-Naan-e-Shabeena Nazar Mein Hai Jis Mein 'Arab Ka Mehr-e-Mubeen Jalwa-Gar Hua Sadiyon Ke Baad Bhi Wo Mahina Nazar Mein Hai Ab Meri Chashm-e-Shauq Pahunchti Hai 'Arsh Tak Me'raaj Jaane Waale Ka Zeena Nazar Mein Hai Banda-Nawaaz! Banda-Nawaazi Se Kaam Le Banda-Nawaaziyon Ka Qareena Nazar Mein Hai Dil Kya Hai? Ek Makhzan-e-Asraar-e-Mustafa Ye Ganj-e-Ma'rifat, Ye Khazeena Nazar Mein Hai Mazhar!
