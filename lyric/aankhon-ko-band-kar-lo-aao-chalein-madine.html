Aankhon Ko Band Kar Lo Aao Chalein Madine

Naat • Shayar: Unknown

Sambhal, nabi ka rauza qareeb-tar hai Jhuk jhuk ke dein salaami ashkon ke aabgeene Aankhon ko band kar lo, aao chale madine Khush-bakht meri aankhon! Ye shehr-e-mustafa hai Sultan-e-do-jahaan ka darbaar aa gaya hai Dehleez-e-mustafa se lipti hui duaa hai Har harf-e-iltija mein meri bhi iltija hai Dil mein chhupe hue hain, aaqa! Kai dafeene Aankhon ko band kar lo, aao chale madine Sajdo!
