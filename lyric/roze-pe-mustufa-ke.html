Roze Pe Mustufa Ke

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Nadeem Sarwar
roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai kunbe ko khoke zainab e nashaad aayi hai rokar pukari nana ke faryad laayi hoo mai chod kar hussain ko jangal mein aayi hoo nana hamare ghar ka sab asbaab lut gaya nana hamare sar pe na baakhi rahi rida nana hazaar tarha jafa ki layeeno ne qaime mein haye aag lagadi layeeno ne roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai nana ajeeb saanihe hum par guzar gaye ranj o alam ne ghayr liya ham jidhar gaye nana khuda ke ghar mein na hum ko amaan mili bhai ne shehr chod ke jangal ki raah li roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai nana shaheed hogaye bhai ke jaanisaar ek ek karke mar gaye ashaab e zeewiqaar nana bala ke ban mein qayamat ka ran pada bhai se bhai baap se beta bichad gaya roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai nana tumhari shakl ka farzand ek jo tha kha kar sina jigar pe wo mehroo bhi chal basa nana shakhi ke teer se asghar huwe shaheed aisa hua na hoga jahan mein sitam shadeed roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai nana zameen e karbobala laal hogayi qasim ki laash ghodon se pamaal hogayi nana kata ke shaano ko abbas mar gaye ghurbath mein bhai jaan ko be-aas kar gaye roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai nana hui aseer tumhari nawasiyan kaun o makaan mein chagayi gham ki udasiyan nana haram hussain ke bazaar mein gaye hum sar barehna kufe ke darbar mein gaye roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai nana tumhari aal gayi lut ke shaam mein pahunchi ali ki betiyan darbare aam mein nana aseer sahibe tatheer hogaye hum log shehre shaam mein tasheer hogaye roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai nana aseer baara thay aur ek thi rasan baandhe gaye thay misle gunahgar e khastatan nana hum apne haal pe rote thay haal e zaar nana hamare haal pe haste thay badshiaar roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai ek ek ka naam poochta tha baani e sitam nana batata jaata tha shimre jafa shishan ye zainabe hazeen hai wo kulsoom e dilfigaar ungli utha utha ke dikhata tha naabekaar roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai nana ajeeb haal hamara tha us ghadi ek bibi peeche doosri bibi ke chupti thi us waqt thar-thari si mere tan mein pad gayi sad haif hai zameen mein zainab na gad gayi roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai itne mein ek shaami ne nana ghazab kiya saydani ko kaneezi mein usne talab kiya hoti thi laak zillatein hum dekhte rahe has has ke taaziyane sitam jhaylte rahe roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai nana tabaah hoke hum aaye hai shaam se ek daaghe nou sakina ka laaye hai shaam se ab tak hai neel rassi ke shaano ko dekhlo nana hamare tan pe nishano ko dekhlo roze pe mustafa ke udaasi si chayi hai zainab jo
