Ali Ki Yaad Aaye Najaf Ki Yaad Aaye

Manqabat • Shayar: Shams Jafri
Khusha zamin-e-mu'alla, zahe-faza-e-najaf Riyaaz-e-khuld bhi hai shaaiq-e-hawa-e-najaf Mili angoothi bhi vaisi hi tha nagin jaisa Najaf bara-e-'ali tha, 'ali bara-e-najaf Mareez-e-'ishq-e-vila hoon ki subh-o-shaam mujhe 'ali ki yaad aaye, najaf ki yaad aaye Usi se rakhta hoon rishta har ek aan jise 'ali ki yaad aaye, najaf ki yaad aaye Mareez-e-'ishq-e-vila hoon ki subh-o-shaam mujhe 'ali ki yaad aaye, najaf ki yaad aaye Ya 'ali ! 'ali ya 'ali ! 'ali ya 'ali ! 'ali ya 'ali ! Izaafa hota hai mere maraz ki shiddat mein Tarap raha hoon main har dam najaf ki furqat mein Ye dharkanon ki sada aaye gaam-gaam mujhe 'ali ki yaad aaye, najaf ki yaad aaye Mareez-e-'ishq-e-vila hoon ki subh-o-shaam mujhe 'ali ki yaad aaye, najaf ki yaad aaye Bina 'ali ke mera zindagi mein ji na lage Khushi mile bhi koi to mujhe khushi na lage Mila hai saanson ki soorat 'ali ka naam mujhe 'ali ki yaad aaye, najaf ki yaad aaye Mareez-e-'ishq-e-vila hoon ki subh-o-shaam mujhe 'ali ki yaad aaye, najaf ki yaad aaye Ya 'ali ! 'ali ya 'ali ! 'ali ya 'ali ! 'ali ya 'ali ! Zamin pe khuld ka manzar mujhe dikha deeje Tarap raha hoon, najaf mein mujhe bula leeje Wagrna jeena nahin hai mere imaam ! Mujhe 'ali ki yaad aaye, najaf ki yaad aaye Mareez-e-'ishq-e-vila hoon ki subh-o-shaam mujhe 'ali ki yaad aaye, najaf ki yaad aaye Ye aarzu hai tamanna hai shams ki, Maula ! Qaseeda likhta rahun saari zindagi, Maula ! Pukaare saara zamana tera ghulaam mujhe 'ali ki yaad aaye, najaf ki yaad aaye Mareez-e-'ishq-e-vila hoon ki subh-o-shaam mujhe 'ali ki yaad aaye, najaf ki yaad aaye Ya 'ali ! 'ali ya 'ali !
