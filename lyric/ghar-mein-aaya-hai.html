Ghar Mein Aaya Hai

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Mir Hassan Mir
shiddat e sabr se mar jaye na maa akbar ki din madine mein wo taqdeer ne dikhlaya hai jisne maari thi kalaje mein sina akbar ke ban ke mehmaan wo sajjad ke ghar aaya hai ghar mein aaya hai qatil e akbar umme laila ko hogayi hai khabar sheh tadapte hai qabr ke andar maa tadapti hai qabr se baahar ghar mein aaya hai qatil e akbar umme laila ko hogayi hai khabar phir wohi kaifiyat hai laila ki haal tha jo jawan ke laashe par uske qadmon ki aahatein sun kar maa ne thaama hua hai apna jigar ghar mein aaya hai qatil e akbar umme laila ko hogayi hai khabar chehra karbobala ki jaanib hai pani haathon mein le ke roti hai gir raha hai nigaah mein maa ki ali akbar ki maut ka manzar ghar mein aaya hai qatil e akbar umme laila ko hogayi hai khabar umme laila sambhaal zainab ko kal ye shabbir de rahe thay sada aaj abid phupi se kehte hai maa ko meri sambhaliye jaa kar ghar mein aaya hai qatil e akbar umme laila ko hogayi hai khabar qabr e zahra se aa rahi hai sada is ne maara hai mere baba ko phir madine mein ek qayamat hai phir raha hai mazaar e paighambar ghar mein aaya hai qatil e akbar umme laila ko hogayi hai khabar ali akbar lipat ke qadmon se keh rahe honge apne baba se toot jaye na zabt ka bandhan hausla maa ko dijiye jaa kar ghar mein aaya hai qatil e akbar umme laila ko hogayi hai khabar sar e akbar utha ke naize par umme laila ko ye dikhaata tha usko kaise wo bhool sakti hai jis se khaaye thay shaam mein pathar ghar mein aaya hai qatil e akbar umme laila ko hogayi hai khabar ye kareemi se ruk nahi sakte qaatilon ko duayein dete hai is se pehle kye baksh de abid koi mukhtaar se kahe jaa kar ghar mein aaya hai qatil e akbar umme laila ko hogayi hai khabar tum ne sajjad mujhko pehchana sun ke qaatil se bole ye maula tu wo ibne e numair hi hai na jisne tukde kiya hamara jigar kal thay mehmaan hum tere zaalim aaj mehmaan tu hamara hai aur kuch chahiye bata tujhko abhi kehdun phupi se mai ja kar ghar mein aaya hai qatil e akbar umme laila ko hogayi hai khabar dil nahi lagta aaj ghar mein mera ay takallum ye maa ka nauha tha thaam kar koi mere baazu ko le chale fatema ki turbat par ghar mein aaya hai qatil e akbar umme laila ko hogayi hai khabar
