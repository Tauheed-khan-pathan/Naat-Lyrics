Qismat Se Guzar Jaye Ek Raat Madine Mein

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Zainul Abedin Kanpuri
Qismat Se Guzar Jaye Ek Raat Madine Mein Aaqa Ko Sunaunga Phir Naat Madine Mein Patthar Bhi Chamakte Hain, Sona Bhi Nikalta Hai Shehron Se Bhi Achchhe Hain Dehaat Madine Mein Allah Re Taabaani! Shaahon Ne Qadam Choome Dekhi Ye Ghulamon Ki Auqaat Madine Mein Ai Baad-e-Saba! Tujh Ko Itna Bhi Nahin Maaloom Batti Hai Nawaason Ki Khairaat Madine Mein Ai Maut! Abhi Ruk Jaa, Us Waqt Chali Aana Sarkaar Hon Aur Hum Hon Ek Saath Madine Mein Mehmaan-e-Muhammad Ko Zehmat Agar Hoti Hai Aate Hain Madad Karne Sau Haath Madine Mein
