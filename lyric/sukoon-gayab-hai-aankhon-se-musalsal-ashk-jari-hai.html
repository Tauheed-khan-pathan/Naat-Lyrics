Sukoon Gayab Hai Aankhon Se Musalsal Ashk Jari Hai

Naat • Shayar: Mufti Hafizullah Hafiz
Naat Khawan: Alvida Khatun, Sandali Ahmad
sukoon ghaayab hai aankhon se, musalasal ashk jaari hai tere jaane se, ai maah-e-mubaarak ! be-qaraari hai badi raunaq thi, ai maah-e-mukarram ! tere aane se judaai par teri tham si gai baad-e-bahaari hai nahin kuchh qadr-daani kar sake ham maah-e-ramzaan ki 'ibaadat mein n din guzre n dar kar shab guzaari hai gunehgaari ki laa'nat se hamein ab door hi rakhna tu 'aadat daal de, maula ! dilon mein neko-kaari ki ilaahi ! rehmaton se tu hamein maayoos mat karna bacha le laaj, ya rab ! tu hamaari sharmasaari ki nahin ham se ada haq ho saka kuchh maah-e-ramzaan ka m'aasi mein rahe lat-pat, gunaahon ki sawaari ki tamannaayen machal kar reh gain seenon mein, ai ramzaan ! kahaan tujh ko basa kar dil mein ham ne tujh se yaari ki ai maah-e-maghfirat ! kyun-kar hamein tu chhod jaata hai hamein rehmat ki chaadar mein tujhe phir se chhupaana hai teri aamad pe ham ne bhi khushi ke geet gaaye the khushaamad ham ne bhi ki thi 'ata-e-rabb-e-baari ki n koi fikr hai, thi zindagi shaadaab karne ki n kuchh ashk-e-nadaamat se chaman ki aab-yaari ki hafeez ! aao kahein ab alwida' ham maah-e-ramzaan ko mohabbat mein ijaazat hai kahaan kuchh aah-o-zaari ki
