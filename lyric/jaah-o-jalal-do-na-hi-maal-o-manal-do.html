Jaah-o-Jalal Do Na Hi Maal-o-Manal Do

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Owais Raza Qadri
Jaah-o-jalaal do na hi maal-o-manaal do Soz-e-bilaal bas meri jholi mein daal do Duniya ke saare gham mere dil se nikaal do Gham apna, ya habeeb! bara-e-bilaal do Hijr-o-firaaq to mila, do iztiraab bhi Betaab qalb az pa-e-soz-e-bilaal do Behti rahe jo har ghadi bas yaad mein, shaha! Wo chashm-e-ashkbaar pa-e-zul-jalaal do Seena madina ho mera, dil bhi madina ho Fikr-e-madina do mujhe madani khayaal do Har aan bas tasawwur-e-tayba mein gum rahun Aisa buland, shaah-e-madina! khayaal do Do dard sunnaton ka pa-e-shaah-e-karbala Ummat ke dil se lazzat-e-'isyaan nikaal do Khulq-e-'azeem se mujhe hissa 'ata karo Be-ja hansi ki khaslat-e-bad ko nikaal do Zikr-o-durood har ghadi wird-e-zabaan rahe Meri fuzool-goi ki 'aadat nikaal do Donon jahaan ki aafatein, maula! museebatein Sadqe mein mere ghaus ke, sarkaar! taal do 'Attar ko bula ke madine mein do baqee'a Do phoolon ke tufail hon poore suwaal do
