Ya Rab Meri Qismat Mein Ik Aisa Safar Likh De

Naat • Shayar: Mufti Kausar Roohani
Naat Khawan: Mufti Kausar Roohaani
Ya Rab ! Meri Qismat Mein Ik Aisa Safar Likh De Makke Mein, Madine Mein Meri Shaam-o-Sahar Likh De Main Shahar Madine Ke Har Koocha-Gali Ghoomun Phir Phir Ke Main Naqsh-e-Qadam-Paak-e-Nabi Choomun Mehboob Ke Qadmon Mein Ho Jaye Mera Ghar Likh De Makke Mein, Madine Mein Meri Shaam-o-Sahar Likh De Ya Rab ! Meri Qismat Mein Ik Aisa Safar Likh De Makke Mein, Madine Mein Meri Shaam-o-Sahar Likh De Jaanib Jo Madine Ki Jab Qaafile Chalte Hain Mera Dil Bhi Tadpta Hai, Aansu Nahin Rukte Hain Marne Se Qabl Is Dar Par Meri Ye Nazar Likh De Makke Mein, Madine Mein Meri Shaam-o-Sahar Likh De Ya Rab ! Meri Qismat Mein Ik Aisa Safar Likh De Makke Mein, Madine Mein Meri Shaam-o-Sahar Likh De Kal Roz-e-Qayaamat Mein Jab Karb Ki Haalat Ho Mere Sar Pe Bhi Pyaare Nabi Ki Nazar-e-Shafaa'at Ho Karta Hoon Mohabbat Un Se, Sang Un Ke Hashar Likh De Makke Mein, Madine Mein Meri Shaam-o-Sahar Likh De Ya Rab ! Meri Qismat Mein Ik Aisa Safar Likh De Makke Mein, Madine Mein Meri Shaam-o-Sahar Likh De Dooba Hai Gunaahon Mein Roohaani Bhi Sar-Ta-Qadam Shewa Hai Magar Tera, Aye Mere Maula ! Karam Haqdaar Nahin Hoon Phir Bhi Du'aayon Mein Asar Likh De Makke Mein, Madine Mein Meri Shaam-o-Sahar Likh De Ya Rab ! Meri Qismat Mein Ik Aisa Safar Likh De Makke Mein, Madine Mein Meri Shaam-o-Sahar Likh De Laaiba Fatima
