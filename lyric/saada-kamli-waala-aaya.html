Saada Kamli Waala Aaya

Naat • Shayar: Abdul Sattar Niazi
Naat Khawan: Milad Raza Qadri
Ral khushiyan yaar manao, Allah ne karam kamaya Sadda kamli waala aaya Aj hoiyaan chaar chuphere ohde karam diyan barsataan Hoya Noor da noori chaanan, sab mukiyaan kaliyaan rataan Tussi farshiyo farsh sajao, Allah vi arsh sajaya Sadda kamli waala aaya Chan chadheya khushiyan waala, do-jag da aaya waali Sir o te taaj shafaa't, nabiyan ton shaan niraali Do-jag te us da saaya, jihde jism da koi nain saaya Sadda kamli waala aaya Wah ! bhaag halima tere tainu milya kamli waala Teri jholi de wich aaya jehda do-jag da rakhwala Nain milya hor kise nu, tainu milya jo sarmaya Sadda kamli waala aaya Do-jag de andar hoiyaan ohde Noor diyan rushnaiyaan Farshaan di gal ik pasay paiyaan arshon paar duhaiyaan Sonh rab di ! do-jag andar ohda paaya kise nain paaya Sadda kamli waala aaya Mangda nain niyazi shaahi, nain mangda hor khazeena Main apni wich hayaati tak laan ik waar Madina Apne mahboob de sadqe ! kar poori aas khudaya !
