Tere Hote Janam Liya Hota

Naat • Shayar: Dr. Muhammad Tahir ul Qadri
Naat Khawan: Milad Raza Qadri, Muhammad Afzal Noshahi, Azmat Raza Bhagalpuri, Saqlain Rasheed
