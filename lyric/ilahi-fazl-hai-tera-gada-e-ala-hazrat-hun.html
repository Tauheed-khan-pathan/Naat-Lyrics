Ilahi Fazl Hai Tera Gada-e-Ala Hazrat Hun

Manqabat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Hasnain Attari
Ilahi! Fazl hai tera, gada-e-Aala Hazrat hoon Karam hai aap ka, Aaqa! Gada-e-Aala Hazrat hoon Waseela sab Sahaba ka, hai sadqa Aal-e-Zahra ka Karam hai Ghaus-o-Khwaja ka, gada-e-Aala Hazrat hoon Mili ummat Muhammad ki, ghulami Ghaus-e-Aazam ki Dar-e-Khwaja ka hoon mangta, gada-e-Aala Hazrat hoon Madine aa ke phir jalwa main dekhoon sabz-gumbad ka Raza ka, Ya Nabi! Sadqa, gada-e-Aala Hazrat hoon Khuda! Ahmad Raza Khan ke chaloon naqsh-e-qadam par main Ata taufeeq tu farma, gada-e-Aala Hazrat hoon Ilahi! Ye sa'aadat de, Madine mein shahadat de Waseela chaar yaaron ka, gada-e-Aala Hazrat hoon Khudaya! Ho karam aisa, baja doon un ki Sunnat ka Jahaan mein chaar-soo danka, gada-e-Aala Hazrat hoon Ameer-o-maaldaaron ki, jahaan ke tajdaaron ki Mujhe, Attar! Kya parwa, gada-e-Aala Hazrat hoon
