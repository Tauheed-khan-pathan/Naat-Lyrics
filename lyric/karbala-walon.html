hai tumhara hogaya saara zamana karbala waalon duayein deti hai tum sab ko zahra karbala waalon tumhara gham rahe zinda hamesha karbala waalon hum apne marne waalon ka bhi gham karte nahi is mein moharram hai tumhara aur rahega karbala waalon duayein deti hai tum sab ko zahra karbala waalon tumhara gham rahe zinda hamesha karbala waalon imam e asr se keh do kisi surat chale aaye sakina shaam mein ab tak hai tanha karbala waalon duayein deti hai tum sab ko zahra karbala waalon tumhara gham rahe zinda hamesha karbala waalon khuda par baat aa jaaye to sar pyara nahi karte sikhaya tumne ye sab ko saleekha karbala waalon duayein deti hai tum sab ko zahra karbala waalon tumhara gham rahe zinda hamesha karbala waalon tumhare naam ki majlis har ek shadi mein hoti hai khushi mein bhi tumhara gham hai taaza karbala waalon duayein deti hai tum sab ko zahra karbala waalon tumhara gham rahe zinda hamesha karbala waalon tumhara marte dam tak rasta takti rahi sughra madine tum nahi aaye dobara karbala waalon duayein deti hai tum sab ko zahra karbala waalon tumhara gham rahe zinda hamesha karbala waalon na jaane kitne din karti rahi hai dhoop sehra ki tumhare bekafan laashon pe saaya karbala waalon duayein deti hai tum sab ko zahra karbala waalon tumhara gham rahe zinda hamesha karbala waalon ali ki betiyon ke saath pathar tum ne khaye hai gaye naizon pe tum shaam o kufa karbala waalon duayein deti hai tum sab ko zahra karbala waalon tumhara gham rahe zinda hamesha karbala waalon kahin pani pilaane par takallum ko bhi rakh lena jahan mein aaoge jab tum dobara karbala waalon duayein deti hai tum sab ko zahra karbala waalon tumhara gham rahe zinda hamesha karbala waalon
