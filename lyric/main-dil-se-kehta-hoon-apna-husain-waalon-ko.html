Main Dil Se Kehta Hoon Apna Husain Waalon Ko

Manqabat • Shayar: Sohail Kalim Farooqi
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Azam Qadri
main dil se kehta hoon apna husain waalon ko salaam sirf hai mera husain waalon ko kisi yazeed ki chaalon mein aa nahin sakte nazar mein rakhta hai allah husain waalon ko sada ye aaj bhi karb-o-bala se aati hai ki sirf sajta hai kalma husain waalon ko mujhe yaqeen hai guzrenge pul-siraat se jab huzoor denge sahaara husain waalon ko huzoor ki kisi nisbat pe ungliyaan n utha tu jaanta nahin, beta ! husain waalon ko sakhi husain ki yaadon mein jab bhi rote hain du'aayen deti hai zehra husain waalon ko ye nisbaton ka samar hai ,suhail faarooqi ! ki pyaar karti hai duniya husain waalon ko khuda ke deen ki jab baat hogi, faarooqi ! karega yaad zamaana husain waalon ko
