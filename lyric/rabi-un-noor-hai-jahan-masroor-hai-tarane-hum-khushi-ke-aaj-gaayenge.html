Marhaba ! Marhaba ! Marhaba ! Saj Gai Har Gali, Sab Makaan Saj Gae 'Aashiqon Ki Chhaton Par Hai Jhande Lage Har Taraf Dhoom Hai, Har Taraf Hai Khushi Aaj Hai Har Taraf Noor Hi Noor Hi Rabi'unnoor Hai, Jahaan Masroor Hai Taraane Ham Khushi Ke Aaj Gaayenge Nabi Ji Aa Gae, Jahaan Mein Chha Gae Ki Dil Imaan Se Ab Jagmagaayenge Bi Haleema Ki Godi Ka Paala Aamina Ka Dulaara Rahmat-e-Do-Jahaan Ban Ke Aaya Le Ke Quraan Pyaara Imaam-Ul-Ambiya, Habeeb-e-Kibriya Jo Dushman Ko Bhi Seene Se Lagaayenge Nabi Ji Aa Gae, Jahaan Mein Chha Gae Ki Dil Imaan Se Ab Jagmagaayenge Aaye Sarkaar Aaye ! Mere Dildaar Aaye ! Mere Gam-Khwaar Aaye ! Hoor-O-Gilmaan Ne Gaaye Huaa Mausam Haseen, Mahak Uthi Zameen Ki Fazl-e-Rab Ke Ab Anwaar Chhaayenge Nabi Ji Aa Gae, Jahaan Mein Chha Gae Ki Dil Imaan Se Ab Jagmagaayenge
