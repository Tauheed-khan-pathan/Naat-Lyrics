Bigri Bhi Banayenge Jalwe Bhi Dikhayenge

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Hafiz Tahir Qadri
Bigri Bhi Banayenge Jalwe Bhi Dikhayenge, Ghabrao Na Deewano Sarkar Bulayenge Hum Masjid-e-Nabvi Ke Dekhenge Minaron Ko, Aur Gumbad-e-Khazra Ke Pur-Noor Nazaron Ko Hum Jaake Madine Phir Wapis Nahi Aayenge, Mil Jayengi Ta'beerein Ek Roz To Khwabon Ki Gir Jayengi Dewarein Sab Dekhna Raahon Ki, Hum Rauza-e-Aqdas Par Jab Aansoo Bahayenge Dil Ishq-e-Nabi Mein To Kuch Aur Tadapne Do, Is Deed Ki Atish Ko Kuch Aur Bhadakne Do Hum Tishnagi Ja Kar Zamzam Se Bujhayenge, Jab Hashr Ke Maidan Mein Ek Hashr Bayan Ho Ga Jab Faisla Ummat Ka Karne Ko Khuda Ho Ga, Ummat Ko Shahe Batha Kamli Mein Chupayenge Lillah Muhammad Se Rudaad Meri Kehna, Ye Pooch Ke Aaqa Se Ae Hajiyo Tum Aana Ishrat Ko Dar-e-Aqdas Kab Aap Bulayenge, Ghabrao Na Deewano Sarkar Bulayenge
