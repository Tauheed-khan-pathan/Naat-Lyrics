Ya Rasoolallah Ya Habiballah Ek Nazar-e-Karam Farma Jana

Naat • Shayar: Mohammad Shahab Ul Deen Saifi
Naat Khawan: Umer Munir Qadri
ya rasoolallaah ! ya habeeballaah ! ik nazar-e-karam farma jaana din raat tadapta hoon lillaah deedaar ka jaam pila jaana ya rasoolallaah ! ya habeeballaah ! ik nazar-e-karam farma jaana tere hijr mein dil bhi jalta hai aankhon se khoon ubalta hai kahin doob n jaaun toofaan mein bekas ko paar laga jaana ya rasoolallaah ! ya habeeballaah ! ik nazar-e-karam farma jaana jeena bhi saza hai bin tere marna bhi saza hai bin tere duniya ke ghamon se, mere nabi ! bismil ki jaan chhuda jaana ya rasoolallaah ! ya habeeballaah ! ik nazar-e-karam farma jaana jis waqt naza' ka 'aalam ho taqdeer ka chehra bareham ho us waqt apne deewaane ko apna deedaar kara jaana ya rasoolallaah ! ya habeeballaah ! ik nazar-e-karam farma jaana meri har koshish naakaam hui raah takte 'umr tamaam hui jab mar jaaun to turbat mein ik apni jhalak dikhla jaana ya rasoolallaah ! ya habeeballaah ! ik nazar-e-karam farma jaana ai mere yaaro gham-khwaaro ! ik meri naseehat likh lena mujhe jab dafna ke jaane lago sarkaar ki naa't suna jaana ya rasoolallaah ! ya habeeballaah ! ik nazar-e-karam farma jaana teri naa't kahaan, meri baat kahaan laachaar shahaab ke bas mein nahin ye phool hain sirf 'aqeedat ke inhein kar manzoor piya jaana ! ya rasoolallaah ! ya habeeballaah ! ik nazar-e-karam farma jaana
