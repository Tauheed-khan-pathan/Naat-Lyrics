Be-Adaban Na Sar Adab Di Gae Adab Thin Wanje Hoo

Hamd • Shayar: Sultan Bahu
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Be-Adaban Na Sar Adab Di Gae Adab Thin Wanje Hoo Jehde Hon Mitti De Bhande Qadi Na Thiwan Kanje Hoo Jehde Mudh Qadim De Khede Kadin Na Honde Ranjhe Hoo Jain Huzur Na Mangya Ahu Dohin Jahanin Wanje Hoo
