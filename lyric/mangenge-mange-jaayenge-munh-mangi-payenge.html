Mangenge Mange Jaayenge Munh Mangi Payenge

Naat • Shayar: Ala Hazrat
Naat Khawan: Unknown
mangenge mange jaayenge munh mangi paayenge sarkar mein na 'la' hai na haajat 'agar' ki hai uff be-hayaiyan ke ye munh aur tere huzur haan tu kareem hai teri khoo darguzar ki hai tuj se chhupaun munh to karun kis ke samne kya aur bhi kisi se tawakko nazar ki hai kaabe ka naam tak na liya tayba hi kaha puchha tha mujhse jisne ke nohḏat kidhar ki hai uske tufail hajj bhi khuda ne kara diye asale muraad haazri us paak dar ki hai sarkar ham ganwaron mein tarz-e-adab kahan hamko to bas tameez yahi bhik bhar ki hai itna ajab bulandi-e-jannat pe kis liye dekha nahin ke bhik ye kis unche ghar ki hai mangta ka haath uthte hi daata ki dayn thi doori qubool-o-arz mein bas haath bhar ki hai sharbat na dein, na dein, to kare baat lutf se ye shahed ho to fir kise parwah shakar ki hai
