Allah Muaf Kar De

Dua, Durood, Tasbih • Shayar: Shabbir Abu Talib
Naat Khawan: Shabbir Abu Talib
allah ! mu'aaf kar de maula ! tu mu'aaf kar de jab mushkilon ne ghera, tu ne bacha liya hai duniya ne jab giraaya, tu ne utha liya hai mere kareem ! mujh ko yoon hi sanbhaal rakhna bigda jo kaam mera, tu ne bana diya hai allah ! mu'aaf kar de maula ! tu mu'aaf kar de 'isyaan ka shor har pal, din-raat hai musalasal mujh ko bacha le, maula ! mere saamne hai daladal rasta n koi rehbar, haami n koi yaawar pyaasa hoon bhej de ab tu rehmaton ke baadal allah ! mu'aaf kar de maula ! tu mu'aaf kar de ramzaan ka waseela, jholi ko meri bhar de jo hon muraadein meri, tu sab ko poori kar de shabbeer tere dar pe ban ke sawaali aaya kar darguzar khataayen, tauba qubool kar de allah ! mu'aaf kar de maula ! tu mu'aaf kar de
