Aaj Karbal Main Shabir Mehman Hai

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Aaj karbal main Shabir mehman hai, dil ko arman hai Hota qatal-e-shah deen ka saman hai, dil ko arman hai Deen Nabwi ko zalim mitany lagay, haq chupanay lagay Kaaba e-deen ko mil kay giranay lagay, haq chupanay lagay Bagh Zahra ka ab hota veeran hai, dil ko arman hai Dosto ban gai zulam ki raat hai, Gham main saadat hai Har taraf shah pay teeron ki barsat hai, gham main saadat hai Hai narghay main fakhr-e-Suleman hai, dil ko arman hai Laash Akbar ki jab shah uthanay lagay, khoon rulanay lagay Parh ka Sughra ka khat yoon sunanay lagay, khoon rulanay lagay Ghar main sughra akaili parishan hai, dil ko arman hai Sar ko sajda main rakh kay yeh shah nain kaha, aay mera badshah Kyon satatay hain mujh ko yeh ahlay jafa, aay meray badshah Kia yehi mera nana ka farman hai, dil ko arman hai Shah nay aada ko Asghar dikha kar kaha, pani deejo zara Hay piyasa karo reham behar-e-khuda, pani deejo zara Kia khatawar nanha sa nadan hai, dil ko arman hai Ki kahoon kaisay Asghar nain pani piya, howay kaisay ada Teer lay kar wahan baitha tha hurmala, howay kaisay ada Lash Asghar hoa khoon main ghaltan hai, dil ko arman hai Naz shah ki sinan par sawari hoi, giria zari hoi Sham ahl-e-haram ki tayyari hoi, giria zari hoi Aaj Zainab hoi sar say uryan hai, dil ko arman hai
