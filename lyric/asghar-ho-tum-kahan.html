Asghar Ho Tum Kahan

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Mir Hassan Mir
ay ali asghar maqtal may baithi khaak par nauha kuna hai maa asghar ho tum kahan jhoola jala to jal gaye armaan bhi mere mai jee rahi thi ek hi hasrat liye huwe dulha tumhe bana'ungi jab hoge tum jawan asghar ho tum kahan bikhre huwe ye chand sitare zameen pe hai zinda hi hai koi magar sab yahin pe hai ek teri laash hi nahi laashon ke darmiyan asghar ho tum kahan do char din bhi zinda nahi reh sakungi mai itna teri ghareebi pe matam karoongi mai ay laal mere haaton ki khulwade rassiyan asghar ho tum kahan maqtal se khaafile ko guzaara gaya tha jab pyaron ko apne dete thay rokar sadayein sab ye kehte kehte bandh gayi bano ki hichkiyan asghar ho tum kahan majlis wahin pe mir takallum bapa hui maa turbate sagheer par kehti thi jis ghadi jaata hai suye shaam ab lut kar ye kaarwan asghar ho tum kahan
