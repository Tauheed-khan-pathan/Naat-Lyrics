Karbala Jaane Walon

Manqabat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Mir Hassan Mir
karbala jaane waalon jaane waalon dar e abbas pe jab tum jaana ye areeza mere maula ko suna kar aana sab se pehle unhe kehna meri jaanib se salaam phir ye kehna ke tadapta hai watan mein wo ghulam kuch nahi laana faqat izn e ziarat laana ye areeza mere maula ko suna kar aana kehna hashim ke qamar se ye kare aur karam dekhne hai mujhe rauzon ke badalte parcham is baras maah e aza mujhko wahin dikhlana ye areeza mere maula ko suna kar aana kehna hazrat se wasail ko kushada karde mera is saal zara rizq zyada karde betiyon beton ko hamraah mujhe hai laana ye areeza mere maula ko suna kar aana karbala piason se jaate nahi mujhko hai khabar tum bulaate ho to mil jaata hai khud zaad e safar likha ho aana to lagta nahi ek bhi aana ye areeza mere maula ko suna kar aana chote hazrat se ye kehna ke hai ashoor khareeb ye bhi kehna ke kya is saal bhi tadpega ghareeb khoon ka dena hai wan aa ke mujhe nazrana ye areeza mere maula ko suna kar aana unse kehna nahi maangunga mai aazaish o chain neend jab aayegi so jaunga bain ul harmain kafsh ko takiya bana kar hai mujhe so jaana ye areeza mere maula ko suna kar aana arbaeen ki jo banane lagay fehrist aaqa naam mera bhi sakina ki tassaduq likhna mujhko bhi karbala paidal hai najaf se jaana ye areeza mere maula ko suna kar aana mai agar shaam gaya pehle to boloonga yehi aap ke ammu ne bheja hai sakina pani tum abal fazl ke sardaab ka pani laana ye areeza mere maula ko suna kar aana kaise zinda rahi bin aap ke asghar ki behen mar gayi par na khuli bachi ki gardan se rasan pursa masoom sakina ka hai dene aana ye areeza mere maula ko suna kar aana mir sajjad ko nauhe ka sila ye dena is baras karbobala aayega jab wo maula aap nazrane ke badle mein nazar aa jaana ye areeza mere maula ko suna kar aana
