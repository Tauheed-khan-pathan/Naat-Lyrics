Taboot E Hussain

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Nadeem Sarwar
wavaila wavaila aa raha hai taboote hussain e ghareeb momino uth'ta hai taboote ghareeb e zehra jisko nau laak layeeno ne dagha se maara pushte gardan se jise haye ghazab qatl kiya darmiyan zeen o zameen ke raha jiska laasha chauda sadiyon se jise ahle aza rote hai aise bekas ko hussain ibne ali kehte hai aa raha hai taboote hussain e ghareeb wavaila wavaila uska taboot hai jisne hai bada gham dekha apna ghar lut'te hue daswin moharam dekha apne ansaar rafeeqon ko labe dam dekha akbar o qasim o abbas ka matam dekha subho se shaam talak laashon pe laashe laaya uski ek laash koi bhi na uthane aaya aa raha hai taboote hussain e ghareeb wavaila wavaila jab bhi taboot ye uth'ta hai kahin wavaila shaam se iski behan aati hai karne girya saath ek bachi bhi aati hai sunane nauha apne baba ke azadaron ko deti hai dua daud ke baba ke taboot pe gir jaati hai taboot se rone ki sada aati hai aa raha hai taboote hussain e ghareeb wavaila wavaila iska ek beta tha athara baras ka akbar ayyuhal badr tamami tha yehi noor e nazar byaah ke din thay bahot khush thi behan aur madar haye afsos sina kaat gayi uska jigar iski majboor zaeefi ko sahara dene aaj aaya hai ye taboot ko kaandha dene aa raha hai taboote hussain e ghareeb wavaila wavaila jiska taboot hai ek bhai tha iska safdar log kehte thay usay karbobala ka hyder hoke bedast gira jab wo zameen ke upar bas usi din se na seedhi hui bekas ki kamar jab jahan bhi kahin taboot baramad ye hua badh ke karta hai alamdar ka parcham saaya aa raha hai taboote hussain e ghareeb wavaila wavaila zere taboot jo nanha sa ye gehwara hai isse zaahir hai ke baba ko bahot pyara hai kurta is bache ka bhi khoon may tar saara hai iske haaton pe layeeno ne isay maara hai saath taboot ke shaamil hai azadaron may sab se kamsin ye azadar hai ghamkharon may aa raha hai taboote hussain e ghareeb wavaila wavaila asr ka waqt tha ghash khake jo ghode se gira is nihatte pe jo hazaron ka jo lashkar toota hoke behosh ye phir zeen se ghode ki gira darmiyan zeen o zameen haye badan tha iska thi behan saamne jab seene pe jallad chada maa ke zaanu pe tha jab haye gala iska kata aa raha hai taboote hussain e ghareeb wavaila wavaila shor e matam may ye kaun aaya basad aaho buka ke log chillane lagay ya ali hyder maula saath ek bibi hai odhe hue kaala burqa kehti hai maa hoo teri pehlu shakista zehra kya adab sarwar o rehaan nazar aata hai maa padar ke liye taboot jhuka jaata hai aa raha hai taboote hussain e ghareeb wavaila wavaila
