Maula Shab-e-Barat Hai Tauba Qubool Ho

Dua • Shayar: Saif Qadri Allahabadi
Naat Khawan: Hafiz Tahir Qadri
tu kareem hi jo Thehra, to karam ke kya thikaane tu nawaazne pe aae, to nawaaz de zamaane maula ! shab-e-baraat hai, tauba qubool ho ham 'aasiyo.n pe 'afw-o-karam ka nuzool ho ye maGfirat ki raat hai, Gaafil na hoiye 'ayyaashiyo.n me.n aaj na ye shab guzaariye har jurm baKHsh dega KHuda aaj bil-yaqee.n kuchh qatre aansu KHauf-e-KHuda se bahaaiye maula ! shab-e-baraat hai, tauba qubool ho ham 'aasiyo.n pe 'afw-o-karam ka nuzool ho dar par KHuda ke aae.n ki tauba ki raat hai sajde me.n sar jhukaae.n ki tauba ki raat hai ro ro ke, gi.Dgi.Da ke mu'aafi talab kare.n baKHshega rab KHataae.n ki tauba ki raat hai maula ! shab-e-baraat hai, tauba qubool ho ham 'aasiyo.n pe 'afw-o-karam ka nuzool ho maula ! mai.n sharmsaar hu.n apne gunaah par mujh ko shab-e-baraat ke sadqe mu'aaf kar maula ! ye maanta hu.n gunahgaar hu.n magar qurbaan jaan-o-dil se hu.n tere habeeb par maula ! shab-e-baraat hai, tauba qubool ho ham 'aasiyo.n pe 'afw-o-karam ka nuzool ho abr-e-karam me.n aaj nahaane ki raat hai soya huaa naseeb jagaane ki raat hai pa.Dh ke namaaz, kar ke gunaaho.n se tauba aaj KHallaaq-e-do-jahaa.n ko manaane ki raat hai maula ! shab-e-baraat hai, tauba qubool ho ham 'aasiyo.n pe 'afw-o-karam ka nuzool ho ai Saif ! aaj rab ki 'inaayat hai josh par kar dega teri saari KHataae.n wo darguzar bhar dega tera daaman-e-ummeed aaj shab jo chaahiye KHuda-e-jahaa.n se suwaal kar maula ! shab-e-baraat hai, tauba qubool ho ham 'aasiyo.n pe 'afw-o-karam ka nuzool ho
