Jab Bhi Ho Karbobala

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Mir Hassan Mir

mansoob hai ab raat meri hoga ehsaan nibhadoge agar baat meri chain se qabr mein ghat jayegi har raat meri jeete ji unse na ho paayi mulaqaat meri sar chacha jaan ka meri qabr pe laana bhaiya meri
