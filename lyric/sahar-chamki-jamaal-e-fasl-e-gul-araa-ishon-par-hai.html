Sahar Chamki Jamaal e Fasl e Gul Araa-ishon Par Hai

Naat • Shayar: Hasan Raza Khan
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Sahar Chamki Jamaal e Fasl e Gul Araa-ishon Par Hai Naseem e Rooh Parwar Se Mashaam e Jaan Mu'attar Hai Qareeb e Taibah Bakhshe Hain Tasaw'wur Ne Mazay Kya, Kya Mera Dil Hai Madina Mein, Madina Dil Ke Andar Hai Malaa'ik Sar Jahaan Apna Jhijakte Darte Rakhte Hain Qadam Un Ke Gunahgaaron Ka Aisi Sar Zameen Par Hai Aray O Sonay Waale Dil! Aray O Sonay Waale Dil! Sahar Hai, Jaag Ghaafil, Dekh Tu 'Aalam Munawwar Hai Suhaani Tarz Ki Tal'at, Niraali Rang Ki Nik'hat Naseem e Subh Se Mahaka Huwa, Pur Noor Manzar Hai Ta'aal-Allah Ye Shaadaabi, Ye Rangeeni Ta'aal-Allah Bahaar e Hasht Jannat, Dasht e Taibah Par Nich'haawar Hai Hawa-en Aa Rahi Hain Koocha e Pur Noor e Jaanaan Ki Khili Jaati Hain Kaliyaan, Taazgi Dil Ko Muyassar Hai Munawwar Chashm e Zaa-ir Hai Jamaal e 'Arsh e Azam Se Nazar Mein Sabz Qub'ba Ki Tajal'li Jalwa Gustar Hai Ye Rif'at Dargah e 'Arsh Aastaan Ke Qurb Se Paayi Ke Har-Har Saans, Har-Har Gaam Par Me'raj e Digar Hai Muharram Ki Naween Taarikh, Baara Manzilen Kar Ke Wahaan Pahunche Wo Ghar Dekha Jo Ghar Allah Ka Ghar Hai Na Poocho Ham Kahaan Pahunche Awr In Aankhon Ne Kya Dekha Jahaan Pahunche Wahaan Pahunche, Jo Dekha Dil Ke Andar Hai Hazaaron Be-Nawaawon Ke Hain Jamghat' Aastaana Par Talab Dil Mein Sada e Ya Rasool'Allah Lab Par Hai Likha Hai Khaama e Rahmat Ne Dar Par Khat't e Qudrat Se Jisay Ye Aastaana Mil Gaya, Sab Kuch Muyassar Hai Khuda Hai Is Ka Maalik, Ye Khudaayi Bhar Ka Maalik Hai Khuda Hai Is Ka Maula, Ye Khudaayi Bhar Ka Sarwar Hai Zamaana Is Ke Qaabu Mein, Zamaane Waale Qaabu Mein Ye Har Daftar Ka Haakim Hai, Ye Har Haakim Ka Afsar Hai 'Ata Ke Saath Hai Mukhtar, Rahmat Ke Khazaanon Ka Khudaayi Par Hai Qaabu, Bas Khudaayi Is Se Baahar Hai Karam Ke Josh Hain, Bazl o Ne'am Ke Daur-Daure Hain 'Ata e Ba-Nawa Har Be-Nawa Se Sheer o Shak'kar Hai Koyi Lipt'a Hai Fart e Shauq Mein Rozay Ki Jaali Se Koyi Gardan Jhukaaye Ro'ab Se Ba Deeda e Tar Hai Koyi Mashghool e Arz e Haal Hai Yun Shaadmaan Ho Kar Ke Ye Sab Se Bar'i Sarkaar Hai Taqdeer Yaawar Hai Kameena Banda e Dar 'Arz Karta Hai Huzoori Mein Jo Mauroosi Yahaan Ka Madh Gustar Hai Sana-Gar Hai Teri Rahmat Ke Sadqe, Ye Teri Rahmat Ka Sadqa Tha Ke In Na Paak Aankhon Ko Ye Naz'zaara Muyassar Hai Zaleelon Ki To Kya Ginti, Salaateen e Zamaana Ko Teri Sarkaar Aali Hai, Tera Darbaar Bar-tar Hai Teri Daulat, Teri Sarwat, Teri Shaukat, Jalaalat Ka Na Hai Koyi Zameen Par, Awr Na Koyi Aasmaan Par Hai Mataaf o Kaa'ba Ka 'Aalam Dikhaaya Tu Ne Taibah Mein Tera Ghar Beech Mein, Chaaron Taraf Allah Ka Ghar Hai Tajal'li Par Teri Sadqe Hai Mehr o Maah Ki Taabish Pasinay Par Tere Qurbaan Rooh e Mushk o Ambar Hai Gham o Afsos Ka Daafi' Ishaara Pyaari Aankhon Ka Dil e Maayoos Ki Haami Nigaah e Banda Parwar Hai Jo Sab Ach'chon Mein Hai Ach'cha, Jo Har Behtar Se Behtar Hai Tere Sadqe Se Ach'cha Hai, Tere Sadqe Mein Behtar Hai Rakhun Main Haaziri Ki Sharm In 'Aamaal Par Kyunkar Mere Imkaan Se Baahar, Meri Qudrat Se Baahar Hai Agar Shaan e Karam Ko Laaj Ho Mere Bulaane Ki To Meri Haaziri Donon Jahaan Mein Meri Yaawar Hai Mujhe Kya Ho Gaya Hai, Kyun Main Aisi Baaten Karta Hun Yahaan Bhi Yaas o Mahroomi, Ye Kyun Kar Ho, Ye Kyun Kar Hai Bula Kar Apne Kut'tay Ko Na Den, Chamkaar Kar 'Tukr'a Phir Is Shaan e Karam Par Fahm Se Ye Baat Baahar Hai Tazab'zub Maghfirat Mein Kyun Rahe Is Dar Ke Zaa-ir Ko Ke Ye Dargah e Waala, Rahmat e Khaalis Ka Manzar Hai Mubaarak Ho Hasan Sab Aarazu-en Ho Gayeen Poori Ab Un Ke Sadqe Mein 'Aish e Abad Tujh Ko Muyassar Hai
