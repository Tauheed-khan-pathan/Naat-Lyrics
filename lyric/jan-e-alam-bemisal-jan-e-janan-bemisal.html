Naat • Shayar: Azmat Raza Bhagalpuri
Naat Khawan: Azmat Raza Bhagalpuri, Hasan Raza Noshahi
Jaan-e-'Aalam Be-Misaal, Jaan-e-Jaanaan Be-Misaal 'Arsh-e-Aa'zam Ka Wo Dulha Be-Misaal Hai Un Ki Baaten Be-Misaal, Un Ka Lehja Be-Misaal Mere Aaqa Ka Saraapa Be-Misaal Hai Jis Se Ziya Paate Hain Sitaare, Jis Se Chamakte Hain Ye Nazaare Jis Ka Shaidaai Sansaar, Tarse Karne Ko Deedaar Mere Aaqa Ka Wo Chehra Be-Misaal Hai Shabbiri Jazbaat Alag Hai, Ibn-e-'Ali Ki Baat Alag Hai Apna Lutwa Ke Ghar-Baar, Mazhab Kar Daala Gulzaar Shaah-e-Tayba Ka Nawaasa Be-Misaal Hai Hukm Huaa Jab Khwaja Piya Ka, Kooze Mein Saagar Hai Samaaya Duniya Hoti Hai Hairaan, Rab Se Paai Hai Wo Shaan Mere Khwaja Ka Katora Be-Misaal Hai Kaa'be Ka Mehmaan Huaa Wo, Peeron Mein Zeeshaan Huaa Wo Jis Ki Seerat Be-Misaal, Jis Ka Chehra Be-Misaal Aa'la Hazrat Ka Nawaasa Be-Misaal Hai Bhooki Reh Kar Bhi Muskaaye, Bachchon Ko Ji Bhar Ke Khilaaye Maan Ki, 'Azmat ! Aa'la Shaan, Is Ka Shaahid Hai Quraan Sar Pe Waalida Ka Saaya Be-Misaal Hai
