Hum Kabhi Na Bhulayenge

Manqabat • Shayar: Muhammad Asim Ul Qadri Moradabadi
Naat Khawan: Syed Hassan Ullah Hussaini
Ai mustfa ke nawaaso ! 'ali ke dildaaro ! Tumhein Khuda ke ye bande salaam kehte hain Hai yaad wo manzar karbal ka Behta hua khoon wo asghar ka Wo zakhmi badan, jalte khaime Nazaron se na hargiz jaayenge Ham kabhi na bhulaayenge Ham kabhi na bhulaayenge Zehra ke mehkte gulshan ka Har phool masal daala tu ne Pyaase honton ko zakhmi kiya Zaalim !
