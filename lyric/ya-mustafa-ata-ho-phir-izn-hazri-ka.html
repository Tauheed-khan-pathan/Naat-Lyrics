Ya Mustafa Ata Ho Phir Izn Hazri Ka

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Owais Raza Qadri
Ya Mustafa! Ata ho phir izn-e-haziri ka Kar loon nazara aa kar main aap ki gali ka Ik baar to dikha do Ramzan mein Madina Beshak bana lo, Aaqa! Mehman do ghadi ka Rota huwa main aaoon, daagh-e-jigar dikhaoon Afsana bhi sunaoon main apni bekasi ka Main sabz sabz gumbad ki dekh loon baharein Ab izn de do, Aaqa! Tum mujh ko haziri ka Maula! Gham-e-Madina, de de Gham-e-Madina Deewana tu bana de mujh ko tere Nabi ka Khoon-e-jigar se seencha hai baagh-e-Sunniyat ka Kar do karam, waseela us Madani Murshidi ka Ya Sayyid-e-Madina! Bas aap hi nibhana Go hoon bada kameena, par hoon to aap hi ka Ya Mustafa! Gunahon ki aadatein nikalo Jazba mujhe ata ho Sunnat ki pairavi ka Hoon door ab bulaein, deta hoon wasita main Mazloom-e-Karbala ki, Sarkar! Bekasi ka Allah! Hubb-e-duniya se tu mujhe bachana Saail hoon, Ya Khuda! Main ishq-e-Muhammadi ka Fakhr-o-ghuroor se tu, Maula! Mujhe bachana Ya Rab! Mujhe bana de paikar tu aajizi ka Imaan pe, Rabb-e-Rehmat! De de tu istiqamat Deta hoon wasita main tujh ko tere Nabi ka Ho har nafas Madina, dhadkan mein ho Madina Jab tak na saans toote, Sarkar! Zindagi ka Duniya ke tajdaro! Tum se mujhe gharaz kya! Mangta hoon, main to mangta Sarkar ki gali ka Allah! Is se pehle imaan pe maut de de Nuqsan mere sabab se ho Sunnat-e-Nabi ka Ulfat ki bheek de do, deta hoon wasita main Siddiq ka, Umar ka, Usman ka, Ali ka Kuchh nekiyan kama le, jald aakhirat bana le Koi nahin bharosa, ai bhai!
