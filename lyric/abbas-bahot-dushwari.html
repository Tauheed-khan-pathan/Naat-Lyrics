Abbas Bahot Dushwari

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Ameer Hasan Aamir
mera abbas bhaiya abbas abbas bahot dushwari hai zainab ne kaha ro kar bhaiya sar khulne ki tayyari hai abbas bahot dushwari hai badhta hai meri jaanib lashkar aa jaldi aa darr taari hai abbas bahot dushwari hai aakhir mai karun kya batla de koi raah to mujhko dikhla de aisa na ho mujh par dhaye sitam mai kaun hoo inko samjha de sunti hoo ke teri darya par is waqt alambardari hai abbas bahot dushwari hai kyun leta nahi hai meri khabar mai hogayi hoo ghar se be ghar darti hoo na chin jaye chadar hae dekh qayamat ka manzar kyun karta nahi imdaad meri kya baat hai jo be zaari hai abbas bahot dushwari hai kya is se bada bhi hoga sitam ab nargha e aada mein hai hum saansein hai galay mein atki hui lagta hai nikal jayega dam karti hai sakina yaad tujhe ghabrayi hui bechaari hai abbas bahot dushwari hai qaimon ko jalaya jaata hai be jurm sataya jaata hai aa dekh mere ghazi qaidi abid ko banaya jaata hai pehnaate hai zaalim zanjeerein aur tauq galay mein bhaari hai abbas bahot dushwari hai aise mein kare kya teri behan bandhti hai mere shaano mein rasan kis tarha rakhun dil par qaabu kis tarha karun ye zulm sahan ghairat se mera phat'ta hai jigar saanson mein bhi aah o zaari hai abbas bahot dushwari hai kis tarha jiye naazon ke palay hai ek rasan mein baara galay haste hai meri ghurbat pe layeen din ye bhi meri taqdeer mein thay zinda mujhe bhaiya rakhe huwe is waqt meri khuddaari hai abbas bahot dushwari hai kodon pe mai kode khaati hoo aur kuch bhi nahi keh paati hoo jab dekhti hoo naizon pe sar dil thaam ke bas reh jaati hoo hote hai musalsal zulm o sitam ghurbat ka safar bhi jaari hai abbas bahot dushwari hai ay tishna jigar ay tishna dahan ay sher e ghazanfar raahat e jaan itna to bata de zainab ko manzil hai kahan jaana hai kahan bithlaate hai zaalim naaqe par kis zulm ki ye tayyari hai abbas bahot dushwari hai asif jo sukhan zainab ke sune abbas lab e darya tadpe shabbir ka sar roya aamir aankhon ko na khola ghazi ne kehti rahi khwahar ro ro kar imdad ko aa laachari hai abbas bahot dushwari hai
