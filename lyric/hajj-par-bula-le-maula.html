Hajj Par Bula Le Maula

Dua • Shayar: Hafiz Shauq Fareedi
Naat Khawan: Hafiz Tahir Qadri

'ata kar de mujh ko bhi asbaab, maula ! mere haq me.n ab khol de dar ka rasta sharaf haaziri ka tu jaldi 'ata kar mujhe, maula ! hajj ki sa'aadat 'ata kar hajj par bula le, maula ! maula ! kaa'ba dikha de, maula ! dar-e-mustafa ki ziyaarat kara de tamanna ye bachpan ki meri miTa de sada ke liye kar de us dar ka naukar mujhe, maula ! hajj ki sa'aadat 'ata kar hajj par bula le, maula ! maula ! kaa'ba dikha de, maula ! tamanna-e-Shauq-e-Fareedi ho poori 'ata kaa'batullah ki phir ho huzoori nikal jaae dam shehr-e-tayba me.n ja kar mujhe, maula ! hajj ki sa'aadat 'ata kar hajj par bula le, maula ! maula ! kaa'ba dikha de, maula !
