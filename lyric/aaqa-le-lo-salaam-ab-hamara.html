Aaqa Lelo Salaam Ab Humara

Salaam • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Unknown
aaqa ! lelo salaam ab humaara aaqa ! lelo salaam ab humaara saba ! tu madine jaa-ke kehna KHudaara gham se hein toote hue, isiyaan mein doobe hue kardo karam ya nabi ! hein haath faile hue roze mahshar ummati ka, aap hi sahaara aaqa ! lelo salaam ab humaara saba ! tu madine jaa-ke kehna KHudaara chaand ke tukde hue, pedon ne sajde kiye suraj palat aa gaya, ye mo'jze aapke saari duniya par hai aaqa ! qabza tumhaara aaqa ! lelo salaam ab humaara saba ! tu madine jaa-ke kehna KHudaara noor-e-KHuda aap hein, shaah-e-huda aap hein aye sarware ambiya ! baher-e-sakha aap hein hai ye 'azmat rab ne tum par, quraan utaara aaqa ! lelo salaam ab humaara saba ! tu madine jaa-ke kehna KHudaara din raat roti hai ye, ummat tumhare liye ho jaaye nazr-e-karam , aaqa ! humaare liye taiba ki Galiyon ka humko , ho ab nazaara aaqa ! lelo salaam ab humaara saba ! tu madine jaa-ke kehna KHudaara
