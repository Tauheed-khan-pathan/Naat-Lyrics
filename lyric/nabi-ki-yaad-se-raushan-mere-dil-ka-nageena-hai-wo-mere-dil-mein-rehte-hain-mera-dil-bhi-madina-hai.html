Nabi Ki Yaad Se Raushan Mere Dil Ka Nageena Hai | Wo Mere Dil Mein Rehte Hain, Mera Dil Bhi Madina Hai

Naat • Shayar: Abdul Sattar Niazi
Naat Khawan: Milad Raza Qadri
