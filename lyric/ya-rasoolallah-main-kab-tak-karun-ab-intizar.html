Ya Rasoolallah Main Kab Tak Karun Ab Intizar

Naat • Shayar: Muhammad Shauqeen Nawaz Shauq Fareedi
Naat Khawan: Zohaib Ashrafi
Ya Rasoolallah ! Main Kab Tak Karun Ab Intizaar Jald Dikhla Dijiye Na Sabz-Gumbad Ki Bahaar Ya Rasoolallah ! Main Kab Tak Karun Ab Intizaar Ek Main Hoon Haaziri Ki Aas Seene Mein Liye Aur Koi Jaa Raha Hai Tere Dar Par Baar Baar Ya Rasoolallah ! Main Kab Tak Karun Ab Intizaar Baa-Khabar Ho Tum Mere Haalaat Se, Main Kya Kahun Kaifiyat Is Qalb-e-Muztar Ki Hai Tum Par Aashkaar Ya Rasoolallah ! Main Kab Tak Karun Ab Intizaar Haaziri Ki Aarzoo Mein Rota Hoon Subh-O-Masa Ab Bula Lo Jald Mujh Ko, Ai Shah-e-'Aali-Waqaar ! Ya Rasoolallah ! Main Kab Tak Karun Ab Intizaar Muflisi Ki Bediyan Badhne Nahin Dete Qadam Koi Soorat Kijiye Na, Do Jahaan Ke Taajdaar ! Ya Rasoolallah ! Main Kab Tak Karun Ab Intizaar Ai Madine Jaane Waalo ! 'Arz Kar Dena Salaam Aur Kehna Haaziri Ko Hai Deewana Be-Qaraar Ya Rasoolallah ! Main Kab Tak Karun Ab Intizaar Maut Aaye Tere Dar Par Aur Madfan Ho Baqee' Aarzoo Shauq-e-Fareedi Ki Hai, Ai 'Aali-Waqaar ! Ya Rasoolallah ! Main Kab Tak Karun Ab Intizaar
