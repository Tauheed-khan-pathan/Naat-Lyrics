Manqabat • Shayar: Makhdoom Jamali
Naat Khawan: Mahmood Raza Qadri, Hassaan Raza Qadri
ghaus-e-a'zam ! saamne aa jaaiye, aaiye be-sukooni ki dawa farmaaiye, aaiye ghaus-e-a'zam ko bana kar apna peer, dast-geer la-takhaf ki chhaaon mein aa jaaiye, aaiye ghaus-e-a'zam ! saamne aa jaaiye, aaiye be-sukooni ki dawa farmaaiye, aaiye de raha hai ik sada munakar-nakeer, daar-o-geer 'aashiqaan-e-ghaus mat ghabraaiye, aaiye ghaus-e-a'zam ! saamne aa jaaiye, aaiye be-sukooni ki dawa farmaaiye, aaiye shah ban ne ka agar armaan hai, yaan hai ghaus-e-a'zam ke gada ban jaaiye, aaiye ghaus-e-a'zam ! saamne aa jaaiye, aaiye be-sukooni ki dawa farmaaiye, aaiye jod kar rishta muhayyuddeen ke saath, pi ke saath saath bakhshish ki sanad bhi paaiye, aaiye ghaus-e-a'zam ! saamne aa jaaiye, aaiye be-sukooni ki dawa farmaaiye, aaiye ek dar un ka mila to sab mila, rab mila dar-b-dar ki thokrein kyun khaaiye, aaiye ghaus-e-a'zam ! saamne aa jaaiye, aaiye be-sukooni ki dawa farmaaiye, aaiye qaadri ban ne ka matalab hai yahi, haan yahi naam apna khuld mein likhwaaiye, aaiye ghaus-e-a'zam ! saamne aa jaaiye, aaiye be-sukooni ki dawa farmaaiye, aaiye qaadri may-khaana har-dam hai khula, baramla peejiye auron ko bhi pilwaaiye, aaiye ghaus-e-a'zam ! saamne aa jaaiye, aaiye be-sukooni ki dawa farmaaiye, aaiye mil gaya hai ghaus ka khan-e-karam, kya hai gham un ke tukde, un ka sadqa khaaiye, aaiye ghaus-e-a'zam ! saamne aa jaaiye, aaiye be-sukooni ki dawa farmaaiye, aaiye ai jamaali ! ghaus-e-a'zam hain to bas, pesh-o-pas sarakshaan-e-waqt ko batlaaiye, aaiye ghaus-e-a'zam ! saamne aa jaaiye, aaiye be-sukooni ki dawa farmaaiye, aaiye
