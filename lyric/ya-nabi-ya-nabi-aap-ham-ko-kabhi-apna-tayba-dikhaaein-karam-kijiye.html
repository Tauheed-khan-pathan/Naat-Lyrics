Ya Nabi Ya Nabi Aap Ham Ko Kabhi, Apna Tayba Dikhaaein Karam Kijiye

Naat • Shayar: Syed Shajar Ali Makanpuri
Naat Khawan: Sayed Shajar Ali Makanpuri, Sayed Abdul Qadir Qadri
Ya Nabi! Ya Nabi! aap ham ko kabhi Apna Tayba dikhaaein, karam kijiye Aa ke darbaar mein aap ke, ai Shaha! Na'at ham gungunaaein, karam kijiye Aakhri waqt mein jab bhi atka ho dam Aap aa jaaein baaleen pe, Shah-e-Umam! Dekh kar aap ke roo-e-anwar ko ham Marte dam muskuraaein, karam kijiye Ya Nabi! Ya Nabi! aap ham ko kabhi Apna Tayba dikhaaein, karam kijiye Haal ummat ka makhfi nahin aap par Ai Khuda ke dulaare! ai Khair-ul-Bashar! Hind se, Shaam se aur Baghdad se Aa rahi hain sadaaein, karam kijiye Ya Nabi! Ya Nabi! aap ham ko kabhi Apna Tayba dikhaaein, karam kijiye Jab bhi toofaan mein apna safeena phanse Dekh kar apni haalat jo duniya hanse Jab bhi ghere museebat, karam kijiye Gardishein jab sataaein, karam kijiye Ya Nabi! Ya Nabi! aap ham ko kabhi Apna Tayba dikhaaein, karam kijiye Jis ne naana ka wa'da wafa kar diya Jis ne ghar-baar deen par fida kar diya Wo Husain ham ko mahshar ke din, Ya Nabi! Jaam-e-Kausar pilaaein, karam kijiye Ya Nabi! Ya Nabi! aap ham ko kabhi Apna Tayba dikhaaein, karam kijiye Bach ke shaitaan se, dil ko Madina karein Khauf Rab ka bhi ye dil mein paida karein Aap ka 'ishq sab qaum ke naujawaan Apne dil mein basaaein, karam kijiye Ya Nabi! Ya Nabi! aap ham ko kabhi Apna Tayba dikhaaein, karam kijiye Na'at padhta hai ye aap ki barmala Ho Shajar par karam aap ka, ai Shaha! Jhoom uthe sun ke mehfil mein saare bashar Na'at aisi sunaaein, karam kijiye
