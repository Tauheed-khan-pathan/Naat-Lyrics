Hussain Haye Hussain

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Ali Yazdan
kahan se laaun bhara ghar hussain haye hussain pukari zainab e muztar hussain haye hussain tumhare baad to ab jab bhi shaam aati hai saron ko dar se patakne ki kaam aati hai baraste rehte hai aankhon se ta-sehar sab ki lahoo bhare hue manzar hussain haye hussain pukari zainab e muztar hussain haye hussain palat ke aun o mohamed bhi ghar nahi aate ab umme laila ko akbar nazar nahi aate tadapti rehti hai qasim ki yaad mein farwa rubab kehti hai asghar hussain haye hussain pukari zainab e muztar hussain haye hussain guzur gaye hai kayi din na sabr hai na kharaar tumhe bulaati hai raaton ko neend mein kayi baar kabhi kabhi yahan hum sab se chup ke kone mein sakina roti hai aksar hussain haye hussain pukari zainab e muztar hussain haye hussain kate jo pani ki khaatir kareeb nehr e furaat wohi to thay mere baazu wohi to thay mere haath mila huwa hai dil o jaan mein zakhm ki soorat hai jiska naam alam par hussain haye hussain pukari zainab e muztar hussain haye hussain badan tha jism ali ka lahoo rasool ka tha jigar hassan ka tha seene mein dil batool ka tha wo jism chod ke aayi hoo tapte sehra mein wo sar hai noke sina par hussain haye hussain pukari zainab e muztar hussain haye hussain jo hum pe toot gaya aasman e ranj o mehan qayaam qaid mein hota hai aur safar mein rasan aseeri bebasi o bekasi ke aalam mein mera salaam ho tum par hussain haye hussain pukari zainab e muztar hussain haye hussain
