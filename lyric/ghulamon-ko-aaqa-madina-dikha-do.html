Ghulamon Ko Aaqa Madina Dikha Do

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Jawed Raza
Ghulamon Ko Aaqa! Madina Dikha Do Tumhen Yaad Kar Ke Bahut Ro Rahe Hain Badi Hasraten Le Ke Baithe Hain Dil Mein Kisi Din Bhi, Aaqa! Madina Dikha Do Koi Mujh Se Poochhe Tere Dil Mein Kya Hai Mere Dil Mein, Logo! Madina Basa Hai Jise Dekhne Ki Tamanna Mein Roe Usi Dar Ko, Aaqa! Bula Kar Dikhao Ghulamon Ke Dil Mein Madina Basa Hai Har Ik Dil Mein Aaqa Ye Sadma Laga Hai Ghamon Ke Samandar Mein Ham Ro Rahe Hain Ki Pal Bhar Mein, Aaqa! Madina Dikha Do Badi Matlabi Duniya Hansti Hai Mujh Par Ki Kab Jaa Rahe Ho Tum Aaqa Ke Dar Par Isi Kashmakash Mein Ye Dil Ro Raha Hai Kisi Din Bhi, Aaqa! Madina Dikha Do Wo Sab Se Hain Aala, Are Duniya Walo! Wo Nabiyon Ke Sultan Hain, Duniya Walo! Mere Dil Ki Dhadkan Pe Hain Wo Base To Meri Dhadkanon Ko Madina Dikha Do Madine Se Aa Kar Batate Hain Haaji Bada Khush-Numa Hai Madina Madina Mera Dil Ye Sun Kar Bahut Ro Raha Hai Ki Aaqa Mujhe Bhi Madina Dikha Do Bahut Qaafile Main Ne Dekhe Hain Jaate Har Ik Lab Pe Muskaan Tayba Saja Hai Main Tayba Ki Galiyon Mein Kho Jaaun Jaa Kar Mere Aaqa! Tayba Ka Manzar Dikha Do Sabhi Jaane Wale Ye Kyun Ro Rahe Hain Khushi Ke Ye Aansu Liye Ro Rahe Hain Bahut Ro Raha Hai Ye Shamshaad, Aaqa! Ise Bhi Bula Kar Madina Dikha Do
