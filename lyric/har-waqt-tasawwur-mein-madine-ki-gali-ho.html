Har Waqt Tasawwur Mein Madine Ki Gali Ho

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Unknown

Dast-e-Shaha Batha se yehi chitthi mili ho Ai kaash! Madine mein mujhe maut yoon aajae Qadmaun mein Tere sar ho meri rooh chali ho
