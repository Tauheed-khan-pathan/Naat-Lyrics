Un Ki Jaali Wo Saman Aur Wo Rauza Un Ka

Naat • Shayar: Syed Shabahat Hussain
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Ali Faizi
Un Ki Jaali, Wo Saman Aur Wo Rauza Un Ka Kitna Pur-Noor Hai, Wallaah ! Wo Koocha Un Ka Dekh Kar Gumbad-e-Khazra Wo Dhadkna Dil Ka Yaad Aata Hai Mujhe Roz Madina Un Ka Hain Abu-Bakr-o-'Umar Aur Ghani, Maula 'Ali Beech Mein Chaaron Ke Baitha Hai Wo Aaqa Un Ka Rauza-e-Sayyida-Zahra Se Jo Dekha Main Ne Aaj Tak Qaid Hai Aankhon Mein Nazaara Un Ka Ho Gaya Noor Se Maa'moor Zamaana Saara Aisa Chamka Tha Shab-e-Noor Wo Chehra Un Ka Do Hi Lafzon Mein Badal Daali 'Umar Ki Duniya Kitna Meetha Tha Khuda Jaane Wo Lehza Un Ka Hashr Mein Aaqa Ko Munh Kaise Dikhaayega Zaleel Munh Chhupa Lega Sar-e-Hashr Kameena Un Ka Hoon Gunhgaar Magar Phir Bhi Bharosa Hai Mujhe Bakhshawa Lega Mujhe Hashr Mein Sajda Un Ka Sayyida Hi Ne To Paala Hai Shabaahat Ko Janaab Un Ke Hi Naam Se Mashhoor Hai Beta Un Ka
