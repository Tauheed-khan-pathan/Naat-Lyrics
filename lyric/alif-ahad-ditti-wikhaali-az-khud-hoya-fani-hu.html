Alif Ahad Ditti Wikhaali Az Khud Hoya Fani Hoo

Hamd • Shayar: Sultan Bahu
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Alif Ahad Ditti Wikhaali Az Khud Hoya Fani Hoo Qurb Visal Maqam Na Manzal Utth Jism Na Jaani Hoo Na Utth Ishq Mohabbat Kai Na Utth Kaun-Makani Hoo Ainon-Ain Thiyose Ahu Sirr Wahdat Subhani Hoo
