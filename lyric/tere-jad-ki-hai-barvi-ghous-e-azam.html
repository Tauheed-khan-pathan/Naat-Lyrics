Manqabat • Shayar: Jamil-ur-Rahman Qadri
Naat Khawan: Sayyid Kaifi Ali Razavi, Muhammad Sadiq Razavi, Muhammad Hassan Tahseeni Razavi

Koi Un Ke Rutbe Ko Kya Jaanta Hai Muhammad Ke Hain Ja-Nasheen Ghous-e-Aazam Tu Hai Noor-o-Aaeena-e-Mustafai Nahin Koi Tujh Sa Haseen, Ghous-e-Aazam! Huye Awliya Zee-Sharaf Garche Lakhon Magar Sab Se Hain Behtareen Ghous-e-Aazam Jahan Awliya Karte Hain Jabha-Sai Wo Baghdad Ki Hai Zameen, Ghous-e-Aazam! Ki Hum Bhi Ghisein Aastaan Par Jabeen, Ghous-e-Aazam! Hain Ghere Huye Chaar Jaanib Se Dushman Khuda-Ra Bacha Mera Deen, Ghous-e-Aazam! Chupa Le Mujhe Apne Daman Ke Neeche Ki Gham Ki Ghataayein Utheen, Ghous-e-Aazam!
