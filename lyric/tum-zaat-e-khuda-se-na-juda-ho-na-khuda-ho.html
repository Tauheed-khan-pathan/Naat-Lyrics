Tum Zaat-e-Khuda Se Na Juda Ho Na Khuda Ho

Naat • Shayar: Hasan Raza Khan
Naat Khawan: Owais Raza Qadri
Tum zaat-e-Khuda se na juda ho na Khuda ho Allah ko maaloom hai kya jaaniye kya ho Ye kyun kahun mujh ko ye ata ho ye ata ho Wo do ki hamesha mere ghar bhar ka bhala ho Jis baat mein mashhoor-e-jahan hai lab-e-Isa Ae jaan-e-jahan! Wo teri thokar se ada ho Toote hue dam, josh pe toofaan-e-maasi Daaman na mile un ka to kya jaaniye kya ho Yun jhuk ke mile hum se kameenon se wo jis ko Allah ne apne hi liye khaas kiya ho Mitti na ho barbaad pas-e-marg, Ilahi! Jab khaak ude meri Madine ki hawa ho Mangta to hai mangta koi shaahon mein dikha de Jis ko mere Sarkaar se tukda na mila ho Qudrat ne azal mein ye likha un ki jabeen par Jo un ki raza ho wahi Khaaliq ki raza ho Har waqt karam banda-nawaazi pe tula hai Kuchh kaam nahin is se bura ho ki bhala ho Sau jaa se gunahgaar ka ho rakht-e-amal chaak Parda na khule gar tere daaman se bandha ho Abraar niko-kaar Khuda ke hain Khuda ke Un ka hai wo un ka hai jo bad ho jo bura ho Ae nafs! Unhein ranj diya apni badi se Kya qahr kiya tu ne, are tera bura ho Allah yunhi umr guzar jaae gada ki Sar kham ho dar-e-paak par aur haath utha ho Shaabaash, Hasan!
