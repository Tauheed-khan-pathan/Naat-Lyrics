Ya Sayyadi Habibi Khairul Anam Aaqa | Apne Salamiyon Ka Le Lo Salam Aaqa

Salaam • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Aziz Naazaan
Ya sayydi habeebi khair-ul-anaam aaqa ! Apne salaamiyon ka le lo salaam, aaqa ! Aaye hain haath khaali, bhar do huzoor-e-'aali ! Maange hai aaj tum se mangaton ki khasta-haali Rehmat ki subh aaqa ! Rehmat ki shaam aaqa ! Ya sayydi habeebi khair-ul-anaam aaqa ! Apne salaamiyon ka le lo salaam, aaqa ! Tum se ziyaada pyaari jaan bhi nahin hamaari Donon jahaan mile hain dehleez par tumhaari Aaqaaiyaan hui hain tum par tamaam, aaqa ! Ya sayydi habeebi khair-ul-anaam aaqa ! Apne salaamiyon ka le lo salaam, aaqa ! Ehsaas mein jare hain, mujrim bane khare hain Sharminda bhi bohat hain, mushtaaq bhi bare hain Ham ko mu'aaf kar do, ham hain ghulaam, aaqa ! Ya sayydi habeebi khair-ul-anaam aaqa ! Apne salaamiyon ka le lo salaam, aaqa !
