Ay Ali Teri Tanhaiyan

Manqabat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Nadeem Sarwar
aaj tak koi samjha kahan ay ali teri tanhaiyan bas faqat hai teri raazdan ay ali teri tanhaiyan jagmagati na tauheed mein gar simat'ti na tauheed mein aakhirash phir simat'ti kahan ay ali teri tanhaiyan poochti hai salooni yehi hai koi ilm kash hai koi haye koi nahi tha wahan ay ali teri tanhaiyan kaisa koi salam o kalam jab nahi hai jawab e salam uff ye tauqeer e ilm al-amaan ay ali teri tanhaiyan ro rahi hai jo shaam o sahar fatema teri tanhaai par fatema par hai girya kuna ay ali teri tanhaiyan kis ko yaara kare guftagu goongti hai yahan sirf hoo bolti hai khuda ki zaban ay ali teri tanhaiyan ibtida inteha kibriya ambiya aulia ausiya sab hai chup bolti hai jahan ay ali teri tanhaiyan dam ba dam pae ba pae jaa baja keh rahi hai ye kun ki sada kaadh leti hai khud se jahan ay ali teri tanhaiyan zaat e shahid mein mash'hood mein yan wajood aur maujood mein kam se kam hai fasl e kamaan ay ali teri tanhaiyan husn ki deed ki ain tak jalwa e qaaba qausain tak haan bas ek parda e darmiyan ay ali teri tanhaiyan
