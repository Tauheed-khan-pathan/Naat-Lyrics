Urs Aaya Hai Waliyon Ke Sultan Ka Marhaba Marhaba | Batta Hai Sadqa Khwaja Ke Faizan Ka Marhaba Marhaba

Manqabat • Shayar: Muhammad Asim ul Qadri Moradabadi
Naat Khawan: Syed Hassan Ullah Hussaini
Ghareeb jaan ke har ik ne mujh ko taala hai Tum apne dar se na taalo mujhe, ghareeb nawaaz ! 'urs aaya hai waliyon ke sultaan ka, marehba marehba ! Batta hai sadqa Khwaja ke faizaan ka, marehba marehba ! Jagmga uttha phir deep eemaan ka, marehba marehba ! Kya hasin hai samaan un ke aiwaan ka, marehba marehba ! Khil utha chehra har ek muslmaan ka, marehba marehba ! 'urs aaya hai waliyon ke sultaan ka, marehba marehba ! Batta hai sadqa Khwaja ke faizaan ka, marehba marehba ! Wo 'ata-e-Nabi fazl-e-rehmaan hai, waah kya shaan hai Ibn-e-zehra hai, Hasnain ki jaan hai, waah kya shaan hai Hind ki sar-zamin ka wo sultaan hai, waah kya shaan hai Main hi kya us pe har ek qurbaan hai, waah kya shaan hai Phool hai wo 'ali ke gulistaan ka, marehba marehba ! 'urs aaya hai waliyon ke sultaan ka, marehba marehba ! Batta hai sadqa Khwaja ke faizaan ka, marehba marehba ! Ghusl aab-e-karam se karaaya gaya, marehba marehba ! Noori jore se tan jagmgaaya gaya, marehba marehba ! Sar pe taaj-e-vilaayat sajaaya gaya, marehba marehba ! Mere Khwaja ko doolha banaaya gaya, marehba marehba ! Aur sadqa mila shaah-e-jeelaan ka, marehba marehba ! 'urs aaya hai waliyon ke sultaan ka, marehba marehba ! Batta hai sadqa Khwaja ke faizaan ka, marehba marehba ! Ek kaase mein dariya jamaa' kar diya, kya se kya kar diya Mangta ko aap ne badshah kar diya, kya se kya kar diya Ek jogi ko 'abd-e-Khuda kar diya, kya se kya kar diya Jis ne jo maanga us ko 'ata kar diya, kya se kya kar diya Koi saani nahin aap ki shaan ka, marehba marehba ! 'urs aaya hai waliyon ke sultaan ka, marehba marehba ! Batta hai sadqa Khwaja ke faizaan ka, marehba marehba ! Dar pe aaye hain daaman pasaare hue, gham ke maare hue Dukh museebat balaaon se haare hue, gham ke maare hue Sab ne thukraaya ham be-sahaare hue, gham ke maare hue Waqt ghurbat mein apna guzaare hue, gham ke maare hue Daal do sadqa jholi mein 'usmaan ka, marehba marehba ! 'urs aaya hai waliyon ke sultaan ka, marehba marehba ! Batta hai sadqa Khwaja ke faizaan ka, marehba marehba !
