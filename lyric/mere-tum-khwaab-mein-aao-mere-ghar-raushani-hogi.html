Mere Tum Khwaab Mein Aao Mere Ghar Raushani Hogi

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Asad Raza Attari, Imran Shaikh Attari, Asif Attari, Haji Shahid Attari
Mere tum khwaab mein aao, mere ghar raushani hogi Meri qismat jaga jao, inaayat ye badi hogi Madine mujh ko aana hai, gham-e-furqat mitaana hai Kab, Aaqa-e-Madina! Dar pe meri haazri hogi Bana lo apna deewana, bana lo apna mastaana Khazaane mein tumhaare kya kami, pyaare Nabi! Hogi Gham-e-doori rulaata hai, Madina yaad aata hai Tasalli rakh, ai deewane! Teri bhi haazri hogi Mujhe gar deed ho jaae, to meri Eid ho jaae Tera deedaar jab hoga mujhe haasil khushi hogi Khazaan ka sakht pehra hai, ghamon ka ghup andhera hai Zara sa muskura do ge to dil mein raushani hogi Ilahi! Gumbad-e-Khazra ke saae mein shahaadat de Meri laash un ke qadmon mein na jaane kab padi hogi Agar wo chaand se chehre ko chamkaate hue aae Ghamon ki shaam bhi subh-e-bahaaraan ban gai hoti Mujhe jalwa dikha dena, mujhe kalma padha dena Ajal jis waqt sar par, Ya Nabi! Mere khadi hogi Andhera ghup andhera hai, Shaha! Wahshat ka dera hai Karam se qabr mein tum aaoge to raushani hogi Mere marqad mein jab, Attar! Wo tashreef laaenge Labon par Naat-e-Shah-e-Ambiya us dam saji hogi
