par jaa kar paighame aman phailana hai jab ilm ke deep jalaoge hum ghaibat se aajayenge hum dekhenge laazim hai ke hum bhi dekhenge tajeel ho ab ibn-e-zehra hai wird rehaan o sarwar ka dunya ko
