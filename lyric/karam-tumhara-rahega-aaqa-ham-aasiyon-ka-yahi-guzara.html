Karam Tumhara Rahega Aaqa, Ham Aasiyon Ka Yahi Guzara

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Ayan Raza Attari
Karam Tumhaara Rahega Aaqa, Ham Aasiyon Ka Yahi Guzaara Tumhaare Dar Pe Pade Rahenge, Tumhi Ghareebo Ka Ho Sahaara Karam Tumhaara Rahega Aaqa, Ham Aasiyon Ka Yahi Guzaara Tumhi Hamaare Ho Shaaf-e-Mehshar, Tumhi Hamaare Ghareeb-Parwar Na Bhoole Duniyaan Mein Jab Hamen Tum, To Saara Mehshar Hi Hai Tumhaara Karam Tumhaara Rahega Aaqa, Ham Aasiyon Ka Yahi Guzaara Haan ! Sadqa Aale-Ali Ka De Do, Tum Apne Har Ek Wali Ka De Do Haan ! De Do Sadqa-e-Zahra De Do, Haan ! Sab Se Aali Hai Ghar Tumhaara Karam Tumhaara Rahega Aaqa, Ham Aasiyon Ka Yahi Guzaara Karam Ye Rab Ne Kiya Hai Ham Par, Banaaya Is Ne Tumhaara Hai Dar Bataao Jaaun Kahaan Main Dar-Dar, Hai Kaun Aaqa Mera Sahaara Karam Tumhaara Rahega Aaqa, Ham Aasiyon Ka Yahi Guzaara Hai Mera Eemaan Ye Sab Se Aali, Main Apne Murshid Ka Hoon Suwaali Haan ! Naam-e-Attaar Hai Jahaan Mein, Wahi To Sab Se Tumhaara Pyaara Karam Tumhaara Rahega Aaqa, Ham Aasiyon Ka Yahi Guzaara Khada Hai Meezaan Pe Ye Ayaan, Kahaan Ho Aaqa Fansi Hai Ye Jaan Nahin Hai Husne-Amal, Haan !
