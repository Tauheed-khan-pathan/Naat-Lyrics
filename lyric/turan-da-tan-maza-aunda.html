Turan Da Tan Maza Aunda

Naat • Shayar: Maulana Manzoor Ahmad
Naat Khawan: Hafiz Umar Farooq Naqshbandi, Khadim Hussain Naqshbandi
Gali Hondi Madine Di, Turan Da Taan Maza Aunda O Baag Honde Khajooran De, Phiran Da Taan Maza Aunda Ae Seena Jaali Naal Honda, Sabaz Gumbad Di Chhaan Hondi Asaan Muddataan De Sikdiyaan Nun, Tharan Da Taan Maza Aunda Gali Hondi Madine Di, Turan Da Taan Maza Aunda O Baag Honde Khajooran De, Phiran Da Taan Maza Aunda Ae Seena Mimbar Naal Honda, Sabaz Gumbad Di Chhaan Hondi Asaan Muddataan De Sikdiyaan Nun, Tharan Da Taan Maza Aunda Gali Hondi Madine Di, Turan Da Taan Maza Aunda O Baag Honde Khajooran De, Phiran Da Taan Maza Aunda Maran Ton Main Nahin Dar Da, Ae Maut Ik Din Te Auni Ae Qabar Hondi Baqi' De Wich, Maran Da Taan Maza Aunda Gali Hondi Madine Di, Turan Da Taan Maza Aunda O Baag Honde Khajooran De, Phiran Da Taan Maza Aunda Au Kund Wallail Zulfaan Da, Hata Chhade Je Mukhde Ton Deedar-e-Mustafa, Yaaro ! Karan Da Taan Maza Aunda Gali Hondi Madine Di, Turan Da Taan Maza Aunda O Baag Honde Khajooran De, Phiran Da Taan Maza Aunda Kadi Je Deed Mil Jaandi, Aude Maazaag Nainaan Di Main Dub Da Roz Chup Kar Ke, Taran Da Taan Maza Aunda Gali Hondi Madine Di, Turan Da Taan Maza Aunda O Baag Honde Khajooran De, Phiran Da Taan Maza Aunda Je Vich Ramzaan De, Maula !
