Jaise Jaise Raat

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Nadeem Sarwar
jaise jaise raat ja rahi hai ek alam ke saaye mein qaime mehwe dua qaimon mein pyase pyason ki awaaz fiza mein shukre khuda dohra rahi hai jaise jaise raat ja rahi hai door watan se kuch pardesi seh ke aziyat tishnalabi ki dekh rahe hai jaanibe darya mauj qayamat dha rahi hai jaise jaise raat ja rahi hai aamne saamne do lashkar hai sabr idhar us jaa khanjar hai fauj idhar masroofe ibadat ek tayghein chamka rahi hai jaise jaise raat ja rahi hai ek behan ko fikr yehi hai raat ye kyon dhalti jaati hai is ki sehar ke peeche peeche subhe shahadat aa rahi hai jaise jaise raat ja rahi hai kehti hai bachon se maa'ein aao tumhe aaj saja'ein waqt yehi qurbani ka hai deen pe musibat aa rahi hai jaise jaise raat ja rahi hai wo jo shabihe payghambar hai zad pe sina ki uska jigar hai maut usi ki khaatir naiza maqtal mein lehra rahi hai jaise jaise raat ja rahi hai jhoola jhoolne wala bacha lori sunkar bhi na soya bache ko aadabe shahadat lori khud samjha rahi hai jaise jaise raat ja rahi hai saatveen se is ban mein ek maa baal khule bahaale pareshan jhaadti hai maqtal ki zameen ko roti hai thar'ra rahi hai jaise jaise raat ja rahi hai rakh do qalam rehaan o sarwar dard bhara hai subho ka manzar lehjaye akbar se jo ubhri saute azan wo aa rahi hai jaise jaise raat ja rahi hai
