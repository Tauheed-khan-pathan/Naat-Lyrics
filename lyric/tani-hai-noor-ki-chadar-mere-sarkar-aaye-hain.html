Tani Hai Noor Ki Chadar Mere Sarkar Aaye Hain

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Ahmadul Fattah
Tani hai noor ki chaadar, mere sarkaar aaye hain Machi hai dhoom sidra par, mere sarkaar aaye hain Musalsal ho rahi hai aasmaan se noor ki baarish Saraapa noor ke paikar, mere sarkaar aaye hain Mitega zulm duniya se, tanegi amn ki chaadar Nizaam-e-zindagi le kar, mere sarkaar aaye hain Kaha jungle mein hirni ne, mujhe tum se chhudaane ko Yahoodi! Dekh le mud kar, mere sarkaar aaye hain Zameen khush, aasmaan khush, chaand khush, taare bhi khush-khush hain Rachi hain shaadiyaan ghar-ghar, mere sarkaar aaye hain Kisi bhi baap ke haathon na hogi dafn ab zinda Kabhi bhi, koi bhi dukhtar, mere sarkaar aaye hain Hain aaye bazm-e-meelaadunnabi mein jitne deewaane Kahen ek baar sab mil kar, mere sarkaar aaye hain Milegi deen-o-duniya ki tumhen bhi barkaten, 'Aabid! Padho naat-e-nabi ghar-ghar, mere sarkaar aaye hain
