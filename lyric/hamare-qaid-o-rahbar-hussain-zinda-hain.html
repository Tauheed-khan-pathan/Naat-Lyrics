Hamare Qaid-o-Rahbar Hussain Zinda Hain

Manqabat • Shayar: Muhammad Ayyub Raza Amjadi
Naat Khawan: Sabir Raza Azhari Surat
Hamare Qaid-o-Rahbar Husain Zinda Hain Rasool-e-Paak Ke Mazhar Husain Zinda Hain Nabi Ke Deen Ki Khatir Shaheed Ho Kar Bhi Har Ek Sunni Ke Andar Husain Zinda Hain Dare Wo Jin Ka Jahaan Mein Nahi Koi Haami Husainiyon Ko Ho Kyun Dar, Husain Zinda Hain Tumhara Takht-e-Hukoomat Abhi Palat Denge Yazeediyo! Na Karo Shar, Husain Zinda Hain Jinaan Mein Qasim-o-Abbas Sair Karte Hain Wahin Pe Akbar-o-Asghar, Husain Zinda Hain Shaheedon Ko Kabhi Murda Gumaan Na Karna Khuda Bhi Kehta Hai Yaksar Husain Zinda Hain Unhein Jo Murda Samajhte Hain Wo Kare Maatam Mera Aqeeda Hai Dilbar Husain Zinda Hain Lagaya Gaus-o-Raza, Khwaja Ne Yahi Naara Hamare Maula-o-Sarwar Husain Zinda Hain Abhi To Baaqi Hai Karbal Ka Aakhiri Maidaan Bata Do Kufr Ko Ja Kar Husain Zinda Hain Yazeed-e-Waqt Ka Dil Kaanp Jaae Dehshat Se Ae Sunniyo! Kahao Mil Kar, Husain Zinda Hain Zameen-e-Karb-o-Bala Keh Rahi Hai Kya Tujh Se Sun, Ae Yazeed Ke Lashkar! Husain Zinda Hain Isi Se Jaan Lo Hai Kaun Faateh-e-Karbal Yazeed Mar Gaya Hai Par Husain Zinda Hain Mata-e-Ishq Ki Khairaat Maang Lo, Ayyub! Sarapa Ishq Ke Paikar Husain Zinda Hain
