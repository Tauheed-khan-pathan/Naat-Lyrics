Tamanna Hai Mere Maula ! Dayaar-e-Mustafa Dekhoon

Naat • Shayar: Muhammad Asim-ul-Qadri Muradabadi
Naat Khawan: Hafiz Tahir Qadri, Hafiz Ahsan Qadri
Tamanna hai, mere Maula! dayaar-e-Mustafa dekhoon Main Ka'be ka haseen manzar kabhi to, ya Khuda! dekhoon Sunoon labbaik ke naghme haram ki paak waadi mein Dar-e-Khair-ul-Wara par 'aashiqon ka jhoomna dekhoon Tamanna hai, mere Maula! dayaar-e-Mustafa dekhoon Main Ka'be ka haseen manzar kabhi to, ya Khuda! dekhoon Mazaar-e-Hazrat-e-Hamza ko bhi dekhein meri aankhein Dayaar-e-'Aaisha dekhoon, mazaar-e-Faatima dekhoon Tamanna hai, mere Maula! dayaar-e-Mustafa dekhoon Main Ka'be ka haseen manzar kabhi to, ya Khuda! dekhoon Tere Mahboob jis gumbad ke neeche jalwa-farma hain Hai jis par 'arsh bhi naazaan, main wo gumbad hara dekhoon Tamanna hai, mere Maula! dayaar-e-Mustafa dekhoon Main Ka'be ka haseen manzar kabhi to, ya Khuda! dekhoon Basar ho, ya Khuda! kuchh is tarah se zindagi meri Sahar mein Ka'ba dekhoon, shaam mein gumbad hara dekhoon Tamanna hai, mere Maula! dayaar-e-Mustafa dekhoon Main Ka'be ka haseen manzar kabhi to, ya Khuda! dekhoon Jahaan aaraam-farma hain sahaaba mere Aaqa ke Tamanna hai, mere Maula! wo manzar badr ka dekhoon Tamanna hai, mere Maula! dayaar-e-Mustafa dekhoon Main Ka'be ka haseen manzar kabhi to, ya Khuda! dekhoon Main choomoon gumbad-e-Khazra ki jis dam jhaaliyaan, 'Aasim! Khuda se hai du'aa us dam jamaal-e-Mustafa dekhoon Tamanna hai, mere Maula! dayaar-e-Mustafa dekhoon Main Ka'be ka haseen manzar kabhi to, ya Khuda! dekhoon
