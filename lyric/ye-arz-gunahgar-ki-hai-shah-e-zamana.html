Ye Arz Gunahgar Ki Hai Shah-e-Zamana

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Mehmood Attari
Ye arz gunahgar ki hai shah-e-zamana Jab aakhiri waqt aaye mujhe bhool na jana Sakrat ki jab sakhtiyan sarkar hon tari Lillah ! mujhe apane nazaron mein gumana Dar lagta hai eeman kahin ho jaye na barbad Sarkar ! bure khatme se mujh ko bachana Jab rooh meri tan se nikalne ki ghari ho Shaitan-e-laeen se mera eeman bachana Jab dam ho labon par, ai shahanshah-e-madina ! Tum jalwa dikhana mujhe kalma bhi parhana Aaqa ! mera jis waqt ki dam toot raha ho Us waqt mujhe chehra-e-pur-noor dikhana Sarkar ! mujhe naza'a mein mat chhorna tanha Tum aa ke mujhe soora-e-yasin sunana Jab gor-e-ghariban ko chale mera janaza Rahmat ki rida is pe khuda-ra tum urhana Jab qabr mein ahabab chalein mujh ko lita kar Ai pyare nabi ! gor ki wahshat se bachana Tay khair se tadfin ke hon sare marahil Ho qabr ka bhi lutf se aasan dabana Jis waqt nakirain karein aa ke suwalat Aaqa ! mujhe tum aa ke jawabat sikhana Sun rakkha hai hota hai bara sakht andhera Turbat mein meri noor ka fanoos jalana Jab qabr ki tanhai mein ghabraye mera dil Dene ko dilasa shah-e-abarar to aana Jab roz-e-qayamat rahe ik mil pe sooraj Kausar ka chhalakta mujhe ik jam pilana Ho arsa-e-mahshar mein mera chak na parda Lillah mujhe daman-e-rahmat mein chhupana Mahshar mein hisab aah ! main de hi na sakoonga Rahmat na hui, hoga jahannam mein thikana Farmayeinge jis waqt ghulamon ki shafa'at Main bhi hoon ghulam aap ka, mujh ko na bhulana Farma ke shafa'at meri, ai shafe-e-mahshar ! Dozakh se bacha kar mujhe jannat mein basana Ya shah-e-madina ! mah-e-ramzan ka sadqa Jannat mein parosi mujhe tum apana banana Allah ki rahmat se ye mayoos nahin hai Ho jayega attar ki bakhshish ka bahana
