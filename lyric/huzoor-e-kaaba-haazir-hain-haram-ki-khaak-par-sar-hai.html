Huzoor e Kaa'ba Haazir Hain, Haram Ki Khaak Par Sar Hai

Naat • Shayar: Hasan Raza Khan
Huzoor e Kaa'ba Haazir Hain, Haram Ki Khaak Par Sar Hai Bar'i Sarkaar Mein Pahunche, Muqad'dar Yaawari Par Hai Na Ham Aane Ke Laa-iq Thay, Na Qaabil Munh Dikhaane Ke Magar Un Ka Karam Zar'ra Nawaaz o Banda Parwar Hai Khabar Kya Hai Bhikaari Kaisi, Kaisi Ne'maten Paayen Ye Ooncha Ghar Hai Is Ki Bheek Andaaza Se Baahar Hai Tasad'duq Ho Rahe Hain Laakhon Banday, Gird Phir, Phir Kar Tawaaf e Khaana e Kaa'ba, 'Ajab Dil-Chasp Manzar Hai Khuda Ki Shaan, Ye Lab Awr Bosa Sang e Aswad Ka Hamaara Munh Awr Is Qaabil 'Ata e Rab'b e Akbar Ka Jo Haybat Se Rukay Mujrim, To Rahmat Ne Kaha Bar'h Kar Chale Aawo, Chale Aawo! Ye Ghar Rahmaan Ka Ghar Hai Maqaam e Hazrat e Khul'lat, Pidar Sa Mehrbaan Paaya Kaleja Se Lagaane Ko Hateem Aghosh e Maadar Hai Lagaata Hai Ghilaaf e Paak Koyi Chasm e Pur-Nam Se Lipat' Kar Multazam Se, Koyi Mahw e Wasl e Dilbar Hai Watan Awr Us Ka Tar'ka Sadqe Is Shaam e Ghareebi Par Ke Noor e Rukn e Shaami, Roo Kash e Subh e Munawwar Hai Huwe Imaan Taaza Bosa e Rukn e Yamaani Se Fida Ho Jaawun Yumn o Aymani Ka Paak Manzar Hai Ye Zam-Zam Is Liye Hai, Jis Liye Us Ko Piyay Koyi Isi Zam-Zam Mein Jannat Hai, Isi Zam-Zam Mein Kawthar Hai Shifa Kyunkar Na Paayen Neem Jaan, Zehr e Ma'aasi Se Ke Naz'zaara Iraqi Rukn Ka Tiryaaq e Akbar Hai Safa e Qalb Ke Jalwe 'Ayaan Hain Sa'ee Mas'aa Se Yahaan Ki Be-Qaraari Bhi, Sukoon e Jaan e Muztar Hai Huwa Hai Peer Ka Hajj, Peer Ne Jin Se Sharaf Paaya Unheen Ke Fazl Se Din Jumu'ah Ka, Har Din Se Behtar Hai Nahin Kuch Jumu'ah Par Mauqoof, Afzaal o Karam Un Ke Jo Wo Maqbool Farma Len, To Har Hajj, Hajj e Akbar Hai Hasan! Hajj Kar Liya, Kaa'ba Se Aankhon Ne Zia Paayi Chalo Dekhen Wo Basti Jis Ka Rasta Dil Ke Andar Hai
