Angootha Hata Diya Jaye Zulaikha Ungli Nahin Jaan Bhi Lutaegi Agar Huzoor Ka Chehra Dikha Diya Jaye Nazar Jise Nahin Aae Huzoor Ki Azmat Use Bareilly Ka Surma Laga Diya Jaye Ba-Roz-e-Hashr Jahannam Kahegi, Ai Maula!
