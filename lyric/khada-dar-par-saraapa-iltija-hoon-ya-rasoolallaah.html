Khada Dar Par Saraapa Iltija Hoon Ya Rasoolallaah

Naat • Shayar: Sajid Alvi UK
Naat Khawan: Azeem Attaari
Khada Dar Par Saraapa Iltija Hoon, Ya Rasoolallaah ! Ghulaam-e-Kamtri, Adna Gada Hoon, Ya Rasoolallaah ! Sahaara Dijiye Be-Dast-o-Pa Hoon, Ya Rasoolallaah ! Karam Farmaaiye, Be-Aasra Hoon, Ya Rasoolallaah ! Mera Haal-e-Zabun Mohtaaj Hai Chashm-e-'Inaayat Ka Pareshan Hoon, Giriftaar-e-Bala Hoon, Ya Rasoolallaah ! Mujhe Doori Madine Ki Bahut Be-Chain Rakhti Hai Gham-o-Ranj-o-Alam Mein Mubtila Hoon, Ya Rasoolallaah ! B-Naam-e-Ghaus-e-Aa'zam Mere Dil Mein Raushni Aaye Main Taareeki Mein Muddat Se Pada Hoon, Ya Rasoolallaah ! Bhikaari Aap Ka, Main Naam-Lewa Aap Ka, Aaqa ! Talbgaar-e-'Ata Hoon, Be-Nawa Hoon, Ya Rasoolallaah ! Main Saajid Naam Ka Hoon, Kaam Ka Saajid Bhi Ban Jaaun Tahi-Daaman Liye Kab Se Khada Hoon, Ya Rasoolallaah ! Sayyid Zabeeb Masood
