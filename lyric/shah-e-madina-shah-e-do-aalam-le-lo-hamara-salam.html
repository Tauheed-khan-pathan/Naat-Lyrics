Shah-e-Madina Shah-e-Do Aalam | Le Lo Hamara Salam

Salaam • Shayar: Kaisar Samastipuri
Naat Khawan: Chand Afzal Qadri
Shaah-e-madina shaah-e-do-'aalam ! Ai nabiyon ke imaam nabeeji ! Le lo hamaara salaam Noor-ul-'ala tum noor ho, aaqa ! Kehta hai ye quraaan, nabeeji ! Kehta hai ye quraaan 'arsh-e-'ula se farsh pe a ke Dikhlaai apni shaan, nabeeji ! Dikhlaai apni shaan Tum jo padhaare, jhuk gaye saare But-kade ke asnaam, nabeeji ! Le lo hamaara salaam Shaah-e-madina shaah-e-do-'aalam ! Ai nabiyon ke imaam nabeeji ! Le lo hamaara salaam Rutba tumhaara ala, nabeeji ! Shaan tumhaari niraali, nabeeji ! Shaan tumhaari niraali Donon jahaan ke shaah bhi tum ho Kaandhe kamliya kaali, nabeeji ! Kaandhe kamliya kaali Rab ke 'alaawa koi na jaane Kya hai tumhaara maqaam, nabeeji ! Le lo hamaara salaam Shaah-e-madina shaah-e-do-'aalam ! Ai nabiyon ke imaam nabeeji ! Le lo hamaara salaam Muflis aur majboor ke daata ! Bekas ke tum hi haami, nabeeji ! Bekas ke tum hi haami Rehmat saraapa tum ho, nabeeji ! Amn ke tum ho payaami, nabeeji ! Amn ke tum ho payaami Shaah-o-gada sab ban kar khare hain Aaqa ! Tumhaare ghulaam, nabeeji ! Le lo hamaara salaam Shaah-e-madina shaah-e-do-'aalam ! Ai nabiyon ke imaam nabeeji ! Le lo hamaara salaam Burraaq-o-rafraf paai sawaari Tum ne shab-e-me'raaj, nabeeji ! Tum ne shab-e-me'raaj Rab ne tumhaare sar pe, nabeeji ! Rakhkha shafaa'at ka taaj, nabeeji ! Rakhkha shafaa'at ka taaj Jinn-o-bashar aur hoor-o-malaaik Kehte hain ba-ehtiraam, nabeeji !
