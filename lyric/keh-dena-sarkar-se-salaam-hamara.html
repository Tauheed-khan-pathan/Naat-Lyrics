Keh dena Sarkar se Salam Hamara

Salaam • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Unknown
le ke jab aaye haji unke dar ka haseen nazara keh dena sarkar se salaam hamara, salaam hamara wo hein jaan-e-kaaba, wo hein shaah-e-do aalam dekh ke jinka jalwa jhoome arsh-e-aazam nabiyon mein koi bhi nahin hai unke jaisa nyaara keh dena sarkar se salaam hamara, salaam hamara unke dar pe jaa kar hosh na udne paaye dil ki har dhadkan pe rang-e-junoon na chhaye be adbi mahboob ke dar ki, rab ko nahin gawara keh dena sarkar se salaam hamara, salaam hamara unki rahmat ka dariya rehta hai har-soo gham ke andheron mein wahi hai roshni numaya ummat ke liye jo roye raaton mein, aisa nahin koi hamara keh dena sarkar se salaam hamara, salaam hamara hashr ke din jab parde uth jaaeinge unke sadqe se hi kaam ban jaaeinge unki shafa'at ka hai aasra, bas wahi hai sahara keh dena sarkar se salaam hamara, salaam hamara
