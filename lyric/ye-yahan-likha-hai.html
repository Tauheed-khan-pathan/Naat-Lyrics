Ye Yahan Likha Hai

Naat • Shayar: Bekhud Mandalvi
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Junaid Husaini and Javed Rahmani
Hum Nahin Ladne Wale, Na Hi Jhagadna Wale Jo Hawalon Se Sabit Hai Baat Wo Hi Karte Hain Haq Baat Pe Jeete Hain, Haq Baat Pe Marte Hain Tum Jo Kehte Ho Ke Meelad Manana Hai Ghalat Lao Phir Apne Hawalon Se Hi Qail Kar Do Kal Isi Baat Ko Le Kar Ke Kaha Tha Hum Ne Jashn-e-Aaqa Na Manao! Ye Kahan Likha Hai Tum Jo Kehte Ho Ke, Milad Kahan Likha Hai? Aao Hum Tum Ko Batayen, Ye Yahan Likha Hai! Jashn-e-Meelad Hai Haq Par, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Dekho Quran Utha Kar, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Un Ki Aamad Pe Lagaunga Main Jhande Lekin Kya Kisi Aur Ne Bhi Jhande Lagaye Isi Din! Aamina Kehti Hai, Jannat Se Wo Jhande Le Kar Aae Jibreel Zameen Par, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Jashn-e-Meelad Hai Haq Par, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Dekho Quran Utha Kar, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Wo Mujhe Kehta Hai Mehfil Na Sajai Jaye Aisi Kuchh Baat Batao Ke Wo Khud Bhi Aae Aao Mehfil Mein Ke Aate Hain Farishte Is Mein Dekho Farman-e-Payambar, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Jashn-e-Meelad Hai Haq Par, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Dekho Quran Utha Kar, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Kis Liye Karte Hain Hum Zikr-e-Rasool-e-Akram! Kya Koi Is Ka Hawala Bhi Hai Mere Humdam! Khoob Charcha Karo Nemat Jo Mili Hai Tum Ko Dekho Irshad-e-Moakhkhar, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Jashn-e-Meelad Hai Haq Par, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Dekho Quran Utha Kar, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Aql Ke Andhe Ko Do Koi Jawab Aisa Rab Ke Mehboob Ko Wo Kehta Hai Khud Ke Jaisa Apne Jaisa Tu Batata Hai Nabi Ko, Sun Le Un Sani Hai Na Hum-Sar, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Jashn-e-Meelad Hai Haq Par, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Dekho Quran Utha Kar, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Jo Sawal Hum Ne Ae Javed! Kiye The Un Se De Diye Un Ke Junaid! Hum Ne Hawale Sare Un Ko Samjha Diya Keh Kar, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Jashn-e-Meelad Hai Haq Par, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Dekho Quran Utha Kar, Ye Yahan Likha Hai Baat Poochhi Thi Yahi Saal-e-Guzishta Hum Ne Kya Koi Aae Jawab Aur Hawale Un Ke?
