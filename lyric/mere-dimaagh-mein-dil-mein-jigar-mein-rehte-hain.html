Mere Dimaagh Mein, Dil Mein, Jigar Mein Rehte Hain

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Hassan Raza Qadri
Mere Dimaagh Mein, Dil Mein, Jigar Mein Rehte Hain Ye Ghar Hai Aaqa Ka, Apne Hi Ghar Mein Rehte Hain Mere Dimaagh Mein, Dil Mein, Jigar Mein Rehte Hain Karam Hai Rahmat-e-Aalam Ka Saare Aalam Par Wo Noor Ho Ke Libaas-e-Bashar Mein Rehte Hain Ye Ghar Hai Aaqa Ka, Apne Hi Ghar Mein Rehte Hain Mere Dimaagh Mein, Dil Mein, Jigar Mein Rehte Hain Jo Ba-Adab Hain Adab Se Salaam Padhte Hain Jo Be-Adab Hain Agar Aur Magar Mein Rehte Hain Ye Ghar Hai Aaqa Ka, Apne Hi Ghar Mein Rehte Hain Mere Dimaagh Mein, Dil Mein, Jigar Mein Rehte Hain Ye Jaan Hosh-O-Khirad Rozaa-e-Nabi Ki Taraf Ghubaar Ban Ke Hamesha Safar Mein Rehte Hain Ye Ghar Hai Aaqa Ka, Apne Hi Ghar Mein Rehte Hain Mere Dimaagh Mein, Dil Mein, Jigar Mein Rehte Hain Ghubaar-e-Tayba Ki Azmat Na Poochiye Hamase Wo Misl-e-Surma Hamaari Nazar Mein Rehte Hain Ye Ghar Hai Aaqa Ka, Apne Hi Ghar Mein Rehte Hain Mere Dimaagh Mein, Dil Mein, Jigar Mein Rehte Hain Nadeem ! Tayba Aqeedat Ki Raajdhaani Hai Mere Huzoor Mere Chashm-e-Tar Mein Rehte Hain Ye Ghar Hai Aaqa Ka, Apne Hi Ghar Mein Rehte Hain Mere Dimaagh Mein, Dil Mein, Jigar Mein Rehte Hain
