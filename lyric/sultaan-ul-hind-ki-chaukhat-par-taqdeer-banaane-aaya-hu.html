Sultaan Ul Hind Ki Chaukhat Par Taqdeer Banaane Aaya Hu

Manqabat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Sultaan-ul-Hind Ki Chaukhat Par Taqdeer Banaane Aaya Hu Afsaana-e-Dard-e-Hijr-o-Alam Ro Ro Kar Sunaane Aaya Hu Haa! Chashma-e-Faiz-e-Rehmat Par Main Pyaas Bujhaane Aaya Hu Main Chisht Ke Mai-Khaane Wali Sehba Chhalkaane Aaya Hu Kya Bhool Hui Aakhir Mujh Se, Kyu Khwaab Mein Aana Chhod Diya Roothe Hue Apne Khwaaja Ko Main Aaj Manaane Aaya Hu Kya Pesh Karu, Kya Nazr Karu, Sarkaar! Kisi Qaabil Hi Nahi Kuchh Phool Aqeedat Ke Hain Jinhe Turbat Pe Chadhaane Aaya Hu Betaab Hai Dil, Bechain Hai Jaan, Raahat Ka Koi Saamaan Nahi Jalta Hai Mera Khirman Jis Se, Wo Aag Bujhaane Aaya Hu Sadqe Mein Janaab-e-Usmaan Ke, Mujh Par Bhi Karam Ki Ek Nazar Dhoraaji Se Ajmer Nagar Fariyaad Sunaane Aaya Hu Jo Bakht Ke Maare Aate Hain, Kaam Un Ke Yahaan Ban Jaate Hain Khwaabeeda-Muqaddar Hoon Main Bhi, Taqdeer Jagaane Aaya Hu Jab Raaz-o-Niyaaz Ka Aalam Ho, Allah Se Hoti Hon Baatein Us Waqt Na Bhoole Aap Mujhe, Ye Yaad Dilaane Aaya Hu Deedaar Ka Dil Mein Shauq Liye Kehta Hai Tumhaara Ye Sayyad Sultaan-ul-Hind Ke Jalwon Ko Aankhon Mein Basaane Aaya Hu
