Mustafa Aap Ke Jaisa Koi Aaya Hi Nahin

Naat • Shayar: Shamim Faizi
Naat Khawan: Shamim Faizi
Mustafa aap ke jaisa koi aaya hi nahin Aata bhi kaise, jab Allah ne banaya hi nahin Koi saani na hai Rab ka, na mere Aaqa ka Ek ka jism nahin, ek ka saaya hi nahin Qabr mein jab kaha Sarkaar ne, ye mera hai Phir farishton ne mujhe haath lagaya hi nahin Zulf Wallail hai, rukh Wadduha, maa-zaagh aankhein Is tarah Rab ne kisi ko bhi sajaya hi nahin Aap ne jab se nawaaza hai, Ya Rasoolallah! Main ne daaman kisi chaukhat pe bichhaya hi nahin Jis ne Sarkaar ke chehre ki ziyaarat ki hai Us ki nazron mein koi aur samaaya hi nahin Laut kar aa gaya Makke se, Madine na gaya Kaise jaata, tujhe Aaqa ne bulaya hi nahin Jab se darwaaze pe likha main ne Aala Hazrat Koi gustaakh-e-Nabi ghar mere aaya hi nahin Jab se darwaaze pe likha hai Ya Rasoolallah Koi gustaakh-e-Nabi ghar mere aaya hi nahin Jab talak pusht pe Shabbir rahe, ae Faizi!
