Nazdeek Aa Raha Hai Ramzan Ka Mahina

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Owais Raza Qadri, Muhammad Mushtaq Attari, Asad Raza Attari
Nazdeek aa raha hai Ramzan ka mahina Saahil se hajiyon ka phir aa laga safeena Aaqa! Na toot jaae ye dil ka aabgeena Bulwaiye Madine, dikhlaiye Madina Dil ro rahe hain jin ke aansoo chhalak rahe hain Un aashiqon ka sadqa, bulwaiye Madina Hijr-o-firaaq mein dil betaab ho rahe hain Ushshaaq ro rahe hain, lo chal diya safeena Betaab ho raha hai Tayba ki haziri ko Aashiq tadap raha hai aur phat raha hai seena Rauze ko dekhne ko aankhein taras rahi hain Dikhla do sabz-gumbad, Ya Sayyid-e-Madina Nazar-e-karam Khuda-ra mere siyaah dil par Ban jaaega ye dam bhar mein be-baha nageena Rangeeni-e-jahaan se dil toot jaae mera Ya Rab! Mujhe bana de tu aashiq-e-Madina Aansoo na tham rahe hon, dil khoon ugal raha ho Jis waqt tere dar par aaoon, Shah-e-Madina! Bas aarzoo yahi hai qadmon mein jaan nikle Pyare Nabi! Hamara madfan bane Madina Ai bekasoon ke hamdam! Duniya ke door hon gham Bas jaae dil mein Ka'ba, seena bane Madina Tableegh Sunnaton ki karta rahoon hamesha Marna bhi Sunnaton mein ho Sunnaton mein jeena Aaqa! Meri huzoori ki aarzoo ho poori Ho jaae door doori, ai Wali-e-Madina! Un ke diyaar mein tu kaise chale phirega? Attar! Teri jur'at, tu jaaega Madina!
