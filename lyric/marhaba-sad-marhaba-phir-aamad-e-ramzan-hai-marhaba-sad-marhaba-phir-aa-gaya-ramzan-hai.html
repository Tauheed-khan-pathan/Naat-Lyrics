Marhaba Sad Marhaba Phir Aamad-e-Ramzan Hai | Marhaba Sad Marhaba Phir Aa Gaya Ramzan Hai

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Maulana Bilal Raza Attari
Marhaba Sad Marhaba ! Phir Aamad-e-Ramzaan Hai Khil Uthe Murjhaaye Dil, Taaza Huaa Imaan Hai Ya Khuda ! Ham 'Aasiyon Par Ye Bada Ehsaan Hai Zindagi Mein Phir 'Ata Ham Ko Kiya Ramzaan Hai Tujh Pe Sadqe Jaaun, Ramzaan ! Tu 'Azeemushshaan Hai Tujh Mein Naazil Haq Ta'aala Ne Kiya Quraan Hai Abr-e-Rehmat Chha Gaya Hai Aur Samaan Hai Noor Noor Fazl-e-Rab Se Magfirat Ka Ho Gaya Saamaan Hai Har Ghadi Rehmat Bhari Hai, Har Taraf Hain Barkaten Maah-e-Ramzaan Rehmaton Aur Barkaton Ki Kaan Hai Aa Gaya Ramzaan, 'Ibaadat Par Kamar Ab Baandh Lo Faiz Le Lo Jald, Ye Din Tees Ka Mehmaan Hai 'Aasiyon Ki Magfirat Ka Le Kar Aaya Hai Payaam Jhoom Jaao, Mujrimo ! Ramzaan Mah-e-Gufraan Hai Bhaaiyo Behno ! Gunaahon Se Sabhi Tauba Karo Khuld Ke Dar Khul Gae Hain, Daakhila Aasaan Hai Kam Huaa Zor-e-Gunah Aur Masjiden Aabaad Hain Maah-e-Ramzaan-Ul-Mubaarak Ka Ye Sab Faizaan Hai Rozaadaaro ! Jhoom Jaao, Kyun-Ki Deedaar-e-Khuda Khuld Mein Hoga Tumhen, Ye Waa'd-e-Rahmaan Hai Do Jahaan Ki Ne'maten Milti Hain Rozaadaaron Ko Jo Nahin Rakhta Hai Roza Wo Bada Naadaan Hai Ya Ilahi ! Tu Madine Mein Kabhi Ramzaan Dikha Muddaton Se Dil Mein Ye 'Attar Ke Armaan Hai
