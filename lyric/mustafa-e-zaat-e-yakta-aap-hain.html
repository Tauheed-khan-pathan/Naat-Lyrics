Mustafa-e-Zaat-e-Yakta Aap Hain

Naat • Shayar: Akhtar Raza Khan
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Mustafa-e-zaat-e-yakta aap hain Yak ne jis ko yak banaaya aap hain Aap jaysa koi ho sakta nahin Apni har khoobi mein tanhaa aap hain Aab-o-gil mein noor ki pehli kiran Jaan-e-aadam jaan-e-hawwa aap hain Husn-e-awwal ki numood-e-awwalin Bazm-e-aakhir ka ujaala aap hain Laa makaan tak jis ki phaili roshni Woh charagh-e-aalam aara aap hain Hai namak jis ka khameer-e-husn mein Woh maleeh-e-husn-e-aara aap hain Zeyb-o-zeen-e-khaak-o-fakhr-e-khaakiyaan Zeenat-e-arsh-e-mualla aap hain Naazish-e-arsh-o-waqaar-e-arshiyaan Sahib-e-qausayn-o-adnaa aap hain Aap ki talat Khuda ka aayinah Jis mein chamkey haq ka jalwah aap hain Aap ki rooyet hai deedar-e-Khuda Jalwah gaah-e-haq taala aap hain Aap ko rabb ne kiya apna habeeb Saari khilqat ka khulaasah aap hain Aap ki khaatir banaaye do jahaan Apni khaatir jo banaaya aap hain Jaan tooi jaanaan qaraar-e-jaan tooi Jaan-e-jaan jaan-e-maseeha aap hain Paykar-e-har shey mein jaan ban kar nihaan Pardon pardon mein huwayda aap hain Aap se khud aap ka saail hoon main Jaan-e-jaan meri tamanna aap hain Aap ki talat ko dekha jaan di Qabr mein pahoncha to dekha aap hain Barr darat aamad gada bahr-e-sawaal Ho bhala Akhtar ka daata aap hain
