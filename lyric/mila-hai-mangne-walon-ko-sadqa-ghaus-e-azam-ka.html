Mila Hai Mangne Walon Ko Sadqa Ghaus-e-Azam Ka

Manqabat • Shayar: Jauhar Vaishalvi
Naat Khawan: Jauhar Vaishalvi
Mila hai maangne waalon ko sadqa Ghaus-e-Azam ka Kabhi khaali nahin lauta hai mangta Ghaus-e-Azam ka Mubaarak ho ruoosaan-e-sheher ki chaukhtein tum ko Hamaare waaste kaafi hai tukra Ghaus-e-Azam ka Mere aa'maal mujh ko le ke jaayenge jehannam tak Magar mujh ko bacha lega waseela Ghaus-e-Azam ka Mere sarkar-e-surkaanhi ki 'azmat poochhte kya ho Hai mera rehnuma beshak dulaara Ghaus-e-Azam ka Farishte dekh kar us ko lehad mein muskuraayenge Jo dil se ho gaya, jauhar !
