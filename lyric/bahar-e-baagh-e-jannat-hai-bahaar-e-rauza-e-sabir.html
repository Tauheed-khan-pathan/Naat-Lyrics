Bahar E Baagh E Jannat Hai Bahaar E Rauza E Sabir

Manqabat • Shayar: Bedam Shah Warsi
Bahar E Baagh E Jannat Hai Bahaar E Rauza E Sabir Jawar E Arsh E A’ala Hai Jawaar E Rauza E Sabir Yahan Be Parda Allah Aur Nabi Ki Deed Hoti Hai Hamen Makka Madina Hai Dayaar E Rauza E Sabir Zameen Kaliyar Ki, Rauze Ki Fazaa Par Naaz Karti Hai Falak Hota Hai Phir Phir Kar Nisaare Rauza E Sabir Yahan Per Murda Dil Aakar Hayaat E Taaza Paate Hain Bahaar E Jaavidan Hai Humkinar E Rauza E Sabir Rahe Rangeen Chaman Khoon E Shahidan E Mohabbat Ka Sada Phoole-Phale Yeh Laalah-Zaar E Rauza E Sabir Banaaun Ghaaza E Rukhsar E Imaan, Khaak Kaliyar Ki Meri Aankhon Ka Surma Ho Ghubar E Rauza E Sabir Tasavvur Se Nazar Mein Koundti Hain Bijliyan Bedam Ajab Purnoor Hai Naqsh O Nigaar E Rauza E Sabir
