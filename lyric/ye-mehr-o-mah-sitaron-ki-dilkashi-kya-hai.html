Ye Mehr-o-Mah Sitaron Ki Dilkashi Kya Hai

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Asad Iqbal Kalkattavi
Ye mehr-o-maah sitaaron ki dilkashi kya hai Nabi ke husn ke aage ye chaandni kya hai Nabi ki naat padhi ja rahi hai mehfil mein Zara bataaiye lab par ye khaamoshi kya hai Agar hai aana to aana Nabi ke rauze par Ae maut! Tujh ko bataaunga zindagi kya hai Nabi ke shehr mein jannat talaash karta hai Zara mujhe to bata us jagah kami kya hai Laga ke takht ko thokar kaha ye shaahon ne Teri gadaai ke aage shahanshahi kya hai Nabi ke ishq ne lafzon ko kar diya anmol Nabi ki naat hai warna ye shaa'iri kya hai Hain jis ke Maula Nabi, us ke hain Ali Maula Ab is mein aur samajhna hai kya, Ali kya hai Khamosh reh ke wo dushman ko maat dete the Isi se jaan lo me'yaar-e-Azhari kya hai Pehan ke baithe hain Gulzar jubba-e-Maula Isi se jaan lo Gulzar Wasti kya hai
