Jahan Baani Ata Kar Den

Naat • Shayar: Akhtar Raza Khan
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Jahan baani ata kar den bhari jannat hibaa kar den Nabi mukhtaar-e-kul hain jis ko jo chahen ata kar den Jahan mein un ki chalti hai woh dam mein kya se kya kar den Zameen ko aasmaan kar den surayya ko saraa kar den Fazaa mein urhney waaley yun na itraayen nidaa kar den Woh jab chaahen jisey chaahen usey farmaan rawaa kar den Meri mushkil ko yun aasaan mere mushkil kushaa kar den Har ik mouj-e-balaa ko mere maulaa nakhuda kar den Munawwar meri aankhon ko mere shamsu-duhaa kar den Ghamon ki dhoop mein wo saaya-e-zulf-e-dutaa kar den Ataa ho beykhudi mujhko khudi meri hawaa kar den Mujhey yun apni ulfat mein mere maulaa fanaa kar den Jahan mein aam payghaam-e-shah-e-Ahmad Raza kar den Palat kar peechey dekhen phir se tajdeed-e-wafaa kar den Nabi se jo ho begaanaa usey dil se juda kar den Pidar, maadar, biraadar, maal-o-jaan un par fidaa kar den Tabassum se gumaan guzrey shab-e-taareek par din ka Ziyaa-e-rukh se deewaaron ko roshan aayinaa kar den Kisi ko woh hansaatey hain kisi ko woh rulaatey hain Woh yunhi aazmatey hain woh ab to fayslah kar den Gil-e-Taybah mein mil jaaun gulon mein mil ke khil jaaun Hayaat-e-jaawedaani se mujhey yun aashna kar den Unhen manzoor hai jab tak ye dour-e-aazmaesh hai Na chaahen to abhi woh khatm dour-e-ibtilaa kar den Sag-e-aawaarah-e-sahra se uktaa si gayi dunya Bachaao ab zamaaney ka sagaan-e-mustafaa kar den Mujhey kya fikr ho Akhtar mere yaawar hain woh yaawar Balaaon ko jo meri khud giraftaar-e-balaa kar den
