Aisa Lagta Hai Madine Jald Wo Bulwaaenge

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Owais Raza Qadri, Mushtaq Qadri, Asad Raza Attari, Hafiz Ahsan Qadri
Aisa lagta hai Madine jald wo bulwaaenge Jaaenge jaa kar unhe.n zakhm-e-jigar dikhlaaenge Wo agar chaahenge to aisi nazar farmaaenge Khoob roenge, pachhaadon par pachhaadein khaaenge Khushk ashk-e-ishq se aankhein hain, dil bhi sakht hai Aap hi chaahenge to, Aaqa! Hamein tadpaaenge Maut ab to Gumbad-e-Khazra ke saae mein mile Kab tak, Aaqa! Dar-ba-dar ki thokrein ham khaaenge Ai khusha! Taqdeer se gar ham ko manzoori mili Rakh ke sar dehleez par Sarkaar ki mar jaaenge Rote rote gir padenge un ke qadmon mein wahaan Roz-e-Mehshar Shaafe-e-Mehshar nazar jab aaenge Khuld mein hoga hamaara daakhila is shaan se Ya Rasoolallah ka naa'ra lagaate jaaenge Hashr mein kaise sambhaaloonga main apne aap ko Aa mere, Attar! Aa, wo jab wahaan farmaaenge Haae! Ab ki baar bhi, Attar! Jo zinda bache Phir Madine se Karachi rote rote aaenge
