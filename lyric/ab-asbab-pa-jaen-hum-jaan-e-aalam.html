Ab Asbab Pa Jaen Hum Jaan-e-Aalam

Naat • Shayar: Owais Raza Qadri
Naat Khawan: Owais Raza Qadri
Ab Asbaab Pa Jayen Hum, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Pahunch Jayen Tere Haram, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Madine Ko Dekhe Hua Ek Arsa Mujhe Kaat Khaaye Ye Gham, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Tere Dar Pe Iftaariyaan Phir Main Paaun Dikha De Phir Apna Haram, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Mera Nafs-e-Sarakash Shab-o-Roz Mujh Par Bahot Dha Raha Hai Sitam, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Gunahon Ne Haye Mujhe Maar Dala Karam, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Karam, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Gunahon Ki Aatash Meri Kab Bujhegi Tu Barsa De Abr-e-Karam, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Karam Na Karoge, Khabar Gar Na Loge Kidhar Jayenge Aah Hum, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Tu Rakh De Mere Sar Pe Dast-e-Tasalli Mita De Mere Gham-Alam, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Sunaoon Kise ? Kaun Meri Sunega ? Meri Daastan-e-Alam, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Jise Sun Ke Seene Se Apne Laga Lo Karun Naat Aisi Raqam, Jaan-e-Aalam ! Ubaid-e-Raza Ka Bhi Dono Jahan Mein Tu Rakh Le Khuda-Ra Bharam, Jaan-e-Aalam !
