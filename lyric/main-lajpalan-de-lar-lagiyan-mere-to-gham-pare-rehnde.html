Main Lajpalan De Lar Lagiyan Mere To Gham Pare Rehnde

Naat • Shayar: Abdul Sattar Niazi
Naat Khawan: Nusrat Fateh Ali Khan, Owais Raza Qadri, Hafiz Tahir Qadri, Mehmood Attari
