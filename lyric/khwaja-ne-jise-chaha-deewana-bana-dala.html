Khwaja Ne Jise Chaha Deewana Bana Dala

Manqabat • Shayar: Unknown
Khwaja ne jise chaha deewana bana dala Jis Jis pe nazar dali mastana bana dala Ek Qais ko laila ne deewana bana dala Tum ne to jise chaha deewana bana dala Khwaja ki nigaho ka andaaz ajab dekha Farzaan ko ek pal me deewana bana dala Khwaja ne mere dil ko deewana bana dala Khud shamma bane mujhko parwana bana dala Khwaja ki muhabbat ne deewana kiya kaisar Deewane se phir mujhko farzana bana dala Paimanaye wehdat ka ek ruks e musalsal hai Ajmer ko Khwaja ne maikhana bana dala Unke dar e aqdas par jhukne ka ye sadka hai Khwaja ne mera aaisa afsana bana dala Ek khalk e khuda iske deedar ko aati hai Khwaja ne mera aaisa kashana bana dala Deewano ki mehfil me wo ja se huye jana Phir chahane walo ne janana bana dala Musrind ko ya khwaja kya jaam ki parwa ho Jisne teri akho ko paimana bana dala Sufi hai qalandar Hai Jogi Hai Deewana Hai Khwaja ne bhi masto ko kya kya na bana dala Karti hai inhe hoore har roz salam aakar Ajmer ko Khwaja ne jannat sa bana dala Nakamiye kismet ki choti si kahani thi Tum ne ise Bedam afsana bana dala
