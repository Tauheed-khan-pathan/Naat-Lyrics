Manqabat • Shayar: Syed Shajar Ali Makanpuri
Aaj hamne unhein pukaara hai Be-sahaaron ka jo sahaara hai Meri khush-naseebi ke aasaar nazar aate hain Saamne zinda shaah madaar nazar aate hain Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Wyaakul manwa, tarsat naina Ham ko daras dikhla do Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Har-su hai dukh-dard ka mela Dukh hain hazaaron aur main akela Jhar-jhar akhiyaan neer bahaave Door karo ye dard ka rela Toofaan mein hai nayya hamaari Is ko paar laga do Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Jeewan ki pathreeli raahein Paon mein chhaale, lab pe hain aahein Mil jaaye manzil raahi ko Shaah badee'uddeen jo chaahein Meel ke patthar dikhte nahin hain Raah hamein dikhla do Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Wyaakul manwa, tarsat naina Ham ko daras dikhla do Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Tore dar par ye deewaane Shama'-e-mohabbat ke parwaane Taak rahe hain tera rasta Tere 'aashiq aur mastaane 'urs ki rut hai, mukh se apne Parda aaj hata do Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Madaar mohe ! Mori bigri bana do Hindu, muslim, sikh, 'eesaai Sab hain yahan par bhaai-bhaai Man ki muraadein a kar dar par Jis ne jo maangi wo paai Simt-e-shajar hai dar ki jaani !
