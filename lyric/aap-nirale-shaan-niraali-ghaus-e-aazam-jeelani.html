Aap Nirale Shaan Niraali Ghaus-e-Aazam Jeelani

Manqabat • Shayar: Tahir Raza Rampuri
Naat Khawan: Tahir Raza Raamapuree
Aap nirale, shaan niraali, Ghaus-E-A'zam Jeelani ! Bhar do Meri jholi khaali, Ghaus-E-A'zam Jeelani ! Dar pe Tumhaare mangta khada Hai, Dil Mein Karam ki aas liye Door karo Meri bad-haali, Ghaus-E-A'zam Jeelani ! Maa Ne Tum ko apne shikam Mein aadha hafiz kar dala Aap ki maa bhi Allah waali, Ghaus-E-A'zam Jeelani ! Aap ki maa Ne apne shikam Mein aadha hafiz kar dala Aap ki maa bhi Allah waali, Ghaus-E-A'zam Jeelani ! Aap Ke waalid Ne anajaanaa seb Jo khaaya us Ke 'iwaz Baagh ki, ki barson rakh waali, Ghaus-E-A'zam Jeelani ! Qabr Mein jis dam Aap ka shaida khauf se tharra uttha tha Aap Ne Aa kar laaj bacha li, Ghaus-E-A'zam Jeelani ! Sab Kuch mil jaye Tahir Jo Tu mil jaye Tahir ko Tujh se Hai Tahir Tera sawali, Ghaus-E-A'zam Jeelani !
