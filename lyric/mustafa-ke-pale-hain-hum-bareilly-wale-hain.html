Mustafa Ke Pale Hain Hum Bareilly Wale Hain

Manqabat • Shayar: Muhammad Ayyub Raza Amjadi
Naat Khawan: Sabir Raza Azhari Surat, Hafiz Ghulam Mustafa Qadri
