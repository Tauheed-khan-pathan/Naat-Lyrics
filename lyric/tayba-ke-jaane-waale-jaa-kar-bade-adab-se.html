Tayba Ke Jaane Waale Jaa Kar Bade Adab Se

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Owais Raza Qadri
Tayba ke jaane waale! Jaa kar bade adab se Mera bhi qissa-e-gham kahna shah-e-arab se Kahna ki, shah-e-aali! Ik ranj-o-gham ka maara Dono jahaan mein is ka hain aap hi sahaara Haalaat-e-pur-alam se har dam guzar raha hai Aur kaanpte labon se fariyaad kar raha hai Baar-e-gunaah apna hai dosh par uthaye Koi nahi hai aisa jo poochhne ko aaye Bhatka huaa musaafir manzil ko dhoondta hai Taareekiyon mein maah-e-kaamil ko dhoondta hai Seene mein hai andhera, dil hai siyaah-khaana Ye sab meri haqeeqat sarkaar ko sunaana Kahna mere nabi se, mahroom hoon khushi se Sar par ik abr-e-gham hai, ashqon se aankh nam hai Paamaal-e-zindagi hoon, sarkaar! Ummati hoon Ummat ke rahnuma ho, kuchh arz-e-haal sun lo Fariyaad kar raha hoon, main dil-figaar kab se Mera bhi qissa-e-gham kahna shah-e-arab se Tayba ke jaane waale! Jaa kar bade adab se Mera bhi qissa-e-gham kahna shah-e-arab se Kahna ki kha raha hoon main thokren jahaan mein Tum hi batao, aaqa! Jaaoon bhala kahaan main Mahsoos kar raha hoon, duniya hai ek dhoka Matlab ke yaar hain sab, koi nahi kisi ka Kis ko main apna jaanoon, kis ka main loon sahaara Mujh ko to, mere aaqa! Hai aap ka sahaara Tum hi sunoge meri, tum hi karam karoge Dono jahaan mein tum hi mera bharam rakhoge Tum daad-khwaah-e-aalam, main ik naseeb-e-barham Tum be-kason ke waali, main be-nawa sawaali Tum aasiyon ka yaara, main gardishon ka maara Rahmat ho tum saraapa, main murtaqib khata ka Hoon sharmsaar apne aamaal ke sabab se Mera bhi qissa-e-gham kahna shah-e-arab se Tayba ke jaane waale! Jaa kar bade adab se Mera bhi qissa-e-gham kahna shah-e-arab se Ai aazim-e-madina! Jaa kar nabi se kahna Soz-e-gham-e-alam se ab jal raha hai seena Kahna ke dil mein laakhon armaan bhare hue hain Kahna ke hasraton ke nashtar chubhe hue hain Hai aarzoo ye dil ki, main bhi madine jaaoon Sultan-e-do-jahaan ko sab daag-e-dil dikhaaoon Kaatoon hazaar chakkar tayba ki har gali ke Yoonhi guzaar doon main ayyaam zindagi ke Phoolon pe jaan nisaaroon, kaanton pe dil ko waaroon Zarron ko doon salaami, dar ki karoon ghulaami Deewaar-o-dar ko choomoon, qadmon pe sar ko rakh doon Mahboob se lipat kar, rota rahoon baraabar Aalam ke dil mein hai ye hasrat na jaane kab se Mera bhi qissa-e-gham kahna shah-e-arab se Tayba ke jaane waale!
