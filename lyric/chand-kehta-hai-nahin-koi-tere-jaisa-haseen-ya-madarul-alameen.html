Chand Kehta Hai Nahin Koi Tere Jaisa Haseen | Ya Madarul Alameen

Manqabat • Shayar: Syed Shajar Ali Makanpuri
Naat Khawan: Syed Shajar Ali Makanpuri
Ya madaar-al-'aalmin ! Mere madaar-ul-'aalmin ! Ya madaar-al-'aalmin ! Mere madaar-ul-'aalmin ! Chaand kehta hai nahin koi tere jaisa hasin Chaand kehta hai nahin koi tere jaisa hasin Taabi'een hai, aal-e-khatmul-murslin hai tu, shaha ! Tu 'ali ka laa'l hai, zehra ka pyaara ba-Khuda Qalb par tere qadam rakkhe hain khatmul-murslin Ya madaar-al-'aalmin ! Mere madaar-ul-'aalmin ! Hain mile tujh se muhammad, hai mila tujh se Khuda Hind ki dharti pe hai eemaan ka gulshan khila Noor-e-haq se jagmgaai Hind ki ye sar-zamin Ya madaar-al-'aalmin ! Mere madaar-ul-'aalmin ! Mushkilon mein jab kaha hai ham ne, aaqa ! Dam madaar Ho gaya haan ho gaya haan ho gaya hai bera paar Daafe-e-har-ik-bala hai naam tera bil-yaqin Ya madaar-al-'aalmin ! Mere madaar-ul-'aalmin ! Mil gai dharti madaar-e-paak ki tu rashk kar Khaadim-e-sayyad-badee'uddeen hai tu, ai shajar ! Hai madeene ka hi tukra ye maknpur ki zamin Ya madaar-al-'aalmin ! Mere madaar-ul-'aalmin !
