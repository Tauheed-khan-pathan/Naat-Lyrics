Ye Chaand Rabiul-Awwal Ka Chamkata Hai Chamkayega

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Asad Iqbal
Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Eemaan-O-Aqeedat Ka Jalwa Dikhlaata Hai, Dikhlaayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Ek Parcham Arsh Ke Oopar Hai, Ek Kaa'ba Par, Ek Aqsa Par Jibreel Ke Haathon Parcham-e-Haq Lahraata Hai, Lahraayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Ye Sunni Ki Pahchaan Bhi Hai Aur Seene Mein Armaan Bhi Hai Deewaana Nabi Ka Dushman Se Takraata Hai, Takraayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Jo Ghaus Ka Tukada Khaata Hai Wo Sher Ko Aankh Dikhaata Hai Ye Ghaus Ka Kutta Sheron Pe Ghurraata Hai, Ghurraayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Jab Ashq-e-Nadaamat Palkon Se Daaman Pe Utrne Lagte Hain Sarkaar Ki Rahmat Ka Dariya Lahraata Hai, Lahraayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Har Shaakh Pe Un Ki Rahmat Hai, Har Phool Mein Un Ki Nikhat Hai Ye Phool Hai Shehar-e-Tayba Ka Mahkaata Hai, Mahkaayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Jo Aala Hazrat Waala Hai, Wo Waleeyon Ka Matwaala Hai Aur Pyaar Mohabbat Se Un Ko Samjhaata Hai, Samjhaayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Kehte Hain Ise Ikraam-e-Raza ! Kya Khoob Huwa Inaam-e-Raza ! Razwi Se Aankh Milaane Mein Gabhraata Hai, Gabhraayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Eemaan Ho Jis Ka Mustahkam Aur Dil Mein Base Sarkaar-e-Umam Wo Sheesha Ho Kar Patthar Se Takraata Hai, Takraayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Jis Cheez Se Shaah-e-Batha Ki Ulfat Mein Kami A Jaati Hai Us Shaya Ko Hiqaarat Se Aashiq Thukraata Hai, Thukraayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Jo Maslak-e-Aala-Hazrat Hai Wo Maslak-e-Ahal-e-Sunnat Hai Jo Asl Mein Maslak-e-Hanfeeyya Kahlaata Hai, Kahlaayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Wo Shaaf-e-Roz-e-Mahshar Hai, Wo Saaki-e-Jaam-e-Kausar Hai Sarkaar Ki Bakhshish Par Shaida Itraata Hai, Itraayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega Yaaseen Wahi, Taaha Bhi Wahi, Muzzammil Bhi, Muddassir Bhi Alqaab Ka Tughra Qur'aan Mein Muskaata Hai, Muskaayega Ye Chaand Rabeeul-Awwal Ka Chamkaata Hai, Chamkaayega e Sunni Muslmaanon !
