Tum Ne Shah-e-Jilan Mujhe Baghdad Bulaya

Manqabat • Shayar: Owais Raza Qadri
Naat Khawan: Owais Raza Qadri
Tum Ne, Shah-e-Jeelan ! Mujhe Baghdad Bulaya Yun Mere Muqaddar Ko, Shaha ! Tum Ne Jagaya Dekhun Tera Darbar, Meri Thi Ye Tamanna Muddat Ka, Mere Dil Ka Ye Armaan Bar Aaya Haan Haan Tumhe Maaloom Hai Kya Chahiye Mujh Ko Ya Gaus ! Karam Kar, Main Badi Door Se Aaya Tukdon Se Tere Dar Ke, Shaha ! Palti Hai Duniya Malik Tere Jad Ko Tere Allah Ne Banaya Ho Shukr Ada Kaise Tumhara, Shah-e-Jeelan ! Mujh Paapi Ko Darbar Ka Deedar Karaya Har Aarzoo Bar Aaye Ubaid-e-Razvi Ki Ye Arz Liye, Aaqa ! Karaachi Se Main Aaya Nazron Mein Sada Rakhna Ubaid-e-Razvi Ko Meeraan !
