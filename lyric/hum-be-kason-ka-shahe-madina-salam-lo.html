Hum Be Kason Ka Shahe Madina Salam Lo

Salaam • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Hum Be Kason Ka Shahe Madina Salam Lo Hum Aasiyon Ke Dil Ki Sada Hai Salam Lo Murshid Salam Lo, Mere Rehbar Salam Lo Aaqa Salam Lo, Mere Moula Salam Lo Wallah Sare Nabiyon Mein Sartaj Ho Tumhi Mahboob-e-Haq Ho Sahib-e-Meraj Ho Tumhi Pyare Rasool Arsh Ke Dulha Salam Lo Tum Se Hi Deen Mil Gaya, Eemaan Mil Gaya Rehmat Mili Karam Mila, Quran Mil Gaya Sab Kuch Tumhi Se Paya Hai, Aaqa Salam Lo Tum Bin Nahin Hai Koi Madadgaar Ya Nabi Toofan-e-Gham Se Kar Do Humein Paar Ya Nabi Majboor Be-Kason Ka Khudaara Salam Lo Hasnain Ka Tasadduq Kar Dijiye Ataa Deta Hoon Wasta Tumhein Banda Nawaz Ka Mangte Khade Hain Dar Pe, Aye Shaaha Salaam Lo Wasile Se Khair Ke, Saghar Ke Aye Khuda Sardar Bhi Khade Hain Dar-e-Aqdas Pe Aap Ke Hyder Ki Hai Tamanna, Ya Mohammed Salam Lo
