Thandi Thandi Hawa Madine Ki

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Asad Raza Attari
Thandi thandi hawa Madine ki Mehki mehki faza Madine ki Aarzoo hai, Khuda! Madine ki Mujh ko galiyaan dikha Madine ki Din hai kaisa munawwar-o-raushan Raat raunaq-fiza Madine ki Chhaanw to har jagah ki thandi par Dhoop bhi waah wa Madine ki Kaif-aawar hai mausim-e-sarma Garmiyaan marhaba Madine ki Phool to phool khaar bhi dil-kash Pattiyaan dil-kusha Madine ki Hai chaman to haseen kaanton ki Jhaadiyaan khush-numa Madine ki Sabz-gumbad ka husn kya kehna! Jaaliyaan dil-kusha Madine ki Har ghadi rehmatein barasti hain Hai mubarak hawa Madine ki Gham-zado! Aankh khol ke dekho Chha gayi hai ghata Madine ki Kyoon ho mayoos, ai mareezo! Tum Le lo khaak-e-shifa Madine ki Hai tamanna Baqi-e-Gharqad ki Bhaiyo! Do du'a Madine ki Ja ke, Attar! Phir Madine mein Rehmatein lootna Madine mein
