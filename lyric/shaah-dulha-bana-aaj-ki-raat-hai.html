Shaah Dulha Bana Aaj Ki Raat Hai

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Owais Raza Qadri, Mehmood Attari, Haji Bilal Raza Attari
Donon 'aalam hain noorun-'ala-noor kyoon Kaisi raunaq faza aaj ki raat hai Ye masarrat hai kis ki mulaaqaat ki 'Eid ka din hai ya aaj ki raat hai Shaah dulha bana aaj ki raat hai Shaah dulha bana aaj ki raat hai Baag-e-'aalam mein baad-e-bahaari chali Sarwar-e-Anbiya ki sawaari chali Ye sawaari soo-e-zaat-e-baari chali Abr-e-rahmat utha aaj ki raat hai Shaah dulha bana aaj ki raat hai Shaah dulha bana aaj ki raat hai Toor par rif'at-e-laa-makaani kahaan! Lan-taraani kahaan! ma-rraani kahaan! Jis ka saaya nahin, us ka saani kahaan!
