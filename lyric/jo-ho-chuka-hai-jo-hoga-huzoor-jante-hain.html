Jo Ho Chuka Hai, Jo Hoga, Huzoor Jante Hain Teri Ata Se, Khudaya ! Huzoor Jante Hain Wo Mominon Ki To Jaanon Se Bhi Qareeb Huwe Kahan Se Kis Ne Pukara, Huzoor Jante Hain Hiran Ye Kehne Lagi, Chhod De Mujhe, Sayyaad ! Main Laut Aaoongi Wallah, Huzoor Jante Hain Hiran Ne, Oont Ne, Chiriyon Ne Ki Yahi Faryad Ki Un Ke Gham Ka Madawa Huzoor Jante Hain Bula Rahe Hain Nabi, Jaa Ke Itna Bol Use Darakht Kaise Chalega Huzoor Jante Hain Kahan Marenge Abu-Jahal-o-Utba-o-Shaiba Ki Jang-e-Badr Ka Naqsha Huzoor Jante Hain Isi Liye To Sulaya Hai Apne Pehlu Mein Ki Yaar-e-Ghaar Ka Rutba Huzoor Jante Hain Umar Ne Tan Se Juda Kar Diya Tha Sar Jis Ka Wo Apna Hai Ki Paraya Huzoor Jante Hain Nabi Ka Faisla Na Maan Kar Wo Jaan Se Gaya Mizaaj Umar Ka Hai Kaisa, Huzoor Jante Hain Wohi Hain Paikar-e-Sharm-o-Haya-o-Zunnoorain Maqaam Un Ki Haya Ka Huzoor Jante Hain Wo Khud Shaheed Hain, Bete, Nawase, Pote Shaheed Ali Ki Shaan-e-Yagana Huzoor Jante Hain Hain Jis Ke Maula Huzoor, Us Ke Hain Ali Maula Abu-Turaab Ka Rutba Huzoor Jante Hain Main Un Ki Baat Karun Ye Kahan Meri Auqaat Ki Shaan-e-Fatema-Zahra Huzoor Jante Hain Jinaan Mein Kaun Hain Sardar Naujwaanon Ke ? Hasan-Hussain Ke Nana-Huzoor Jante Hain Nahi Hai Zaad-e-Safar Paas Jin Ghulamon Ke Unhe Bhi Dar Pe Bulana Huzoor Jante Hain Khuda Ko Dekha Nahi Aur Ek Maan Liya Ye Jante Thay Sahaba, Huzoor Jante Hain Khabar Bhi Hai ? Ki Khabar Sab Ki Hai Unhe Kab Se Ki Jab Ye Ab Tha Na Tab Tha, Huzoor Jante Hain Munaafiqon Ka Aqeeda, Wo Ghaib-Daan Nahi Sahaabiyon Ka Aqeeda, Huzoor Jante Hain Ay Ilm-e-Ghaib Ke Munkir ! Khuda Ko Dekha Hai ? Tujhe Bhi Kehna Parega, Huzoor Jante Hain Unhi Ke Haath Mein Hain Kunjiyan Khazanon Ki Ki Kis Ko Dena Hai Kitna, Huzoor Jante Hain Hai Un Ke Haath Mein Kya-Kya, Tujhe Khabar Na Mujhe Khuda Ne Kitna Nawaza, Huzoor Jante Hain Wo Kitna Faasla Tha Aur Kalaam Tha Kitna Aw-Adna Aur Fa-Awha Huzoor Jante Hain Mile Thay Raah Mein Nau Baar Kis Liye Musa Ki Deed-e-Haq Ka Bahaana Huzoor Jante Hain Khuda Hi Jaane, Ubaid !
