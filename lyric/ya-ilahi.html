Ya Ilahi Har Jagah Teri Ata Ka Saath Ho

Hamd • Shayar: Ala Hazrat
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Owais Raza Qadri
Ya Ilahi har jagah teri ata ka saath ho Jab pare mushkil, Shah-e-mushkil kusha ka saath ho Ya Ilahi bhool jaun naz'a ki takleef ko Shaadi-e-deedar-e-Husn-e-Mustafa ka saath ho Ya Ilahi gor-e-teera ki aaye jab sakht raat Unke pyare munh ki subh-e-janfaza ka saath ho Ya Ilahi jab pare mahshar mein shor-e-daru-gir Aman dene wale pyare peshwa ka saath ho Ya Ilahi jab zabanein bahar aayein pyas se Sahib-e-Kausar Shah-e-jood-o-ata ka saath ho Ya Ilahi sard mehri par ho jab khursheed-e-hashar Sayyid-e-be-saya ke zill-e-liwa ka saath ho Ya Ilahi garmi-e-mahshar se jab bhadkein badan Daman-e-Mahboob ki thandi hawa ka saath ho Ya Ilahi nama-e-amaal jab khulne lagein Aib-posh-e-khalq sattar ka saath ho Ya Ilahi jab bahein aankhein hisab-e-jurm mein Un tabassum-rez honton ki dua ka saath ho Ya Ilahi jab hisab-e-khanda-e-beja rulaye Chashm-e-giryan-e-murtaja ka saath ho Ya Ilahi rang laayein jab meri be-bakiyan Unki neechi neechi nazron ki haya ka saath ho Ya Ilahi jab chalun tareek rah-e-pul sirat Aftab-e-Hashmi Noor-ul-Huda ka saath ho Ya Ilahi jab sar-e-shamsheer par chalna pare Rabb-e-sallim kehne wale ghamzada ka saath ho Ya Ilahi jo dua-e-naik main tujh se karun Qudsiyon ke lab se ameen Rabbana ka saath ho Ya Ilahi jab Raza khwab-e-giraan se sar uthaye Daulat-e-bedar-e-ishq-e-Mustafa ka saath ho Ya Ilahi le chale jab dafan karne qabar mein Ghaus-e-Azam peshwa-e-awliya ka saath ho
