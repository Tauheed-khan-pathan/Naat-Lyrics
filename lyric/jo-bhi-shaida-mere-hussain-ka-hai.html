Jo Bhi Shaida Mere Hussain Ka Hai

Manqabat • Shayar: Azhar
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Sirf unke liye hi hai Allah Jo bhi shaida mere Hussain ka hai Zulm karna toh sirf Yazeed ka tha Sabr karna mere Hussain ka hai Saare lashkar pe pad gaya bhaari Sher beta mere Hussain ka hai Naaz karta hai aaj bhi Azhar Aisa sajda mere Hussain ka hai
