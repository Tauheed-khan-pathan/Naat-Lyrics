Ya Saiyyada Ya Zahra

Manqabat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Ya Saiyyada Ya Zahra Shehzadi e Rasool Jannat ki hai Malika Hasanain ki hai Maa wo Fatima Zahra Nabi ke ghar mai jo rehmat hai Fatima Zahra Khuda ki khas inayat hai Fatima Zahra Nabi aal hamara azim virsa hai Ye la zaval virasat hai Fatima Zahra Tumhare naam jo utri hai Ayat e Tathir Ye Be misal karamat hai Fatima Zahra Tumhare baalon ko dekha na chand suraj ne Haya ki chadar e izzat hai Fatima Zahra Zamana kehta hai jisko Hasan Husain ki Maa Rasool e paak ki itrat hai Fatima Zahra Nabi ke jism ka tukda hai zauja e haidar Ali ke paas amanat hai Fatima Zahra Riza v sabr ki malika nabi ki shehzadi Ibadaton ki hakikat hai Fatima Zahra Lahu Husain ka ab bhi gavahi deta hai Tumhare khun mai jur'at hai Fatima Zahra Dar e Batool UJAGAR kabhi na chhode ga Tumhare dar se hi izzat hai Fatima Zahra
