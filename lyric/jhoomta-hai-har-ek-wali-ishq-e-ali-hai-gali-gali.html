Jhoomta Hai Har Ek Wali | Ishq-e-Ali Hai Gali Gali

Manqabat • Shayar: Allama Nisar Ali Ujagar
Naat Khawan: Irfaan Qadri
Ya Ali! Ya Ali! Shaah-e-mardan, sher-e-yazdan, quwwat-e-parwardigar La-phata illa Ali, la-Saif illa zulfiqaar Jhoomta hai har ik wali, ishq-e-Ali hai gali gali Khilne lagi hai dil ki kali, ishq-e-Ali hai gali gali Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali! Lahamuka lahami se hai ayan, jismuka jismi ka hai bayan Ramz-e-Nabi hai ramz-e-Ali, ishq-e-Ali hai gali gali Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali! Hubb-e-Ali Imaan hua, bughz-e-Ali mardood hua Ali ka ishq muqaddar sawar deta hai Ali ka bughz to chehre bigaar deta hai Ali ke zikr ko adna na samajh, ae munkir! Ali ik haath se khaibar ukhaar deta hai Hubb-e-Ali Imaan hua, bughz-e-Ali mardood hua Har momin ka Maula Ali, ishq-e-Ali hai gali gali Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali! Maula Hasan aur Maula Husain, dono mere dil ka chain Teri nasl-e-paak mein hai bacha bacha Noor ka Tu hai ain-e-Noor, tera sab gharaana Noor ka Maula Hasan aur Maula Husain, dono mere dil ka chain Nasl-e-Muhammad in se chali, ishq-e-Ali hai gali gali Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali! Faateh-e-khaibar Ali Ali, goonjta lashkar Ali Ali Manzar fiza-e-dahar mein saara Ali ka hai Jis samt dekhta hoon, nazaara Ali ka hai Marhab do-neem hai sar-e-maqtal pada hua Uthne ka ab nahin ki ye maara Ali ka hai Faateh-e-khaibar Ali Ali, goonjta lashkar Ali Ali Mushkil, Ujagar! In se tali, ishq-e-Ali hai gali gali Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali Maula! Ali Ali!
