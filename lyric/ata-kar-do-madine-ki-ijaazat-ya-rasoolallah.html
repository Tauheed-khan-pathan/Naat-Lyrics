Ata Kar Do Madine Ki Ijaazat, Ya Rasoolallah

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Asad Raza Attari
'Ata kar do Madine ki ijaazat, Ya Rasoolallah! Karoon main sabz-gumbad ki ziyaarat, Ya Rasoolallah! 'Ata kar do Madine ki ijaazat, Ya Rasoolallah! Usi Ahmad Raza ka waasta, jo mere murshid hain 'Ata kar do mujhe apni mohabbat, Ya Rasoolallah! 'Ata kar do Madine ki ijaazat, Ya Rasoolallah! Sunehri jaaliyaan hon, aap hon aur mujh sa aasi ho Mile qadmon se seena, jaan-e-jaanaan! Ya Rasoolallah! 'Ata kar do Madine ki ijaazat, Ya Rasoolallah! Main hoon Sunni, rahoon Sunni, maroon Sunni Madine mein Baqi-e-Paak mein ban jaae turbat, Ya Rasoolallah! 'Ata kar do Madine ki ijaazat, Ya Rasoolallah! Shaha! Attar par har aan rehmat ki nazar rakhna Kare din raat ye Sunnat ki khidmat, Ya Rasoolallah!
