Asghar Ko Leke Godh

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Mir Hassan Mir
asghar ko leke godh mein kehti hai karbala ye wo ali hai jisko kabhi ghar nahi mila cheh maah ke ghareeb musafir ko saump ke kehne lage hussain ye qabr e sagheer se rakhna zara khayaal mere tishnakaam ka ye wo ali hai jisko kabhi ghar nahi mila maadar ye bain karti thi dil mein hai ye kasak sughra tumhari godh se aaghosh e qabr tak asghar ko maut ne kahin rukne nahi diya ye wo ali hai jisko kabhi ghar nahi mila maa sochti thi dhalta hai din ho rahi hai shaam jhoole mein laut aayega shayad wo tishnakaam lekin ghazab hua ke wo jhoola bhi jal gaya ye wo ali hai jisko kabhi ghar nahi mila galiyon mein shehre koofa ki bazare shaam mein shireen ke ghar kabhi kabhi darbare shaam mein marne ke baad bhi ali asghar safar mein tha ye wo ali hai jisko kabhi ghar nahi mila nanhe se sar ko yaas se bas takte rehte thay phir khoon ro ke sayyede sajjad kehte thay mai wo ali hoo jiska bhara ghar ujad gaya ye wo ali hai jisko kabhi ghar nahi mila jaane laga watan jo ghareebon ka kaarwan mud mud ke kehti jaati thi ek khaali godh maa rakhiyo sambhaal kar isay ay arze nainawa ye wo ali hai jisko kabhi ghar nahi mila darr hai ye phir se koi sina par utha na le lipta hua hai ab bhi wo seene se baap ke ro ro ke keh rahi hai takallum ye karbala ye wo ali hai jisko kabhi ghar nahi mila
