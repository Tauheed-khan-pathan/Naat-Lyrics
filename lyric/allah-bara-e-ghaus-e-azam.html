Allah Bara E Ghaus E Azam

Manqabat • Shayar: Hasan Raza Khan
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Allah Bara-e-Ghaus-e-Azam De Mujh Ko Wila-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Deedaar-e-Khuda Tujhe Mubaarak Ay Mahw-e-Liqaa-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Wo Kaun, Kareem Saahib-e-Jood Main Kaun, Gada-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Sookhi Huwi Kheytiyaan Hari Kar Ay Abr-e-Sakha-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Ummeeden Naseeb, Mushkilen Hal Qurbaan Ata-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Kya Tezi-e-Mahr-e-Hashr Se Khauf Hain Zer-e-Liwa-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Wo Aur Hain, Jinn Ko Kahiye Muhtaaj Ham To Hain Gada-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Hain Jaanib-e-Naala-e-Ghareebaan Gosh-e-Shanwa-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Kyun Ham Ko Sataaye Naar-e-Dozakh Kyun Radd Ho Dua-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Be-Gaanay Bhi Ho Gaye Yagaanay Dil-Kash Hai Ada-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Aankhon Mein Hai Noor Ki Tajalli Phayli Hai Zia-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Jo Dam Mein Ghani Kar De Gada Ko Wo Kya Hai, Ata-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Kyun Hashr Ke Din Ho Faash Parda Hain Zer-e-Qaba-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Aa-ina Roo-e-Khoobruyaan Naqsh-e-Kaf-e-Paa-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Ay Dil Na Dar In Balaaon Se Ab Wo Aayi Sada-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Ay Gham Jo Sataaye Ab Tu Jaanun Le Dekh Wo Aaye, Ghaus-e-Azam Taar-e-Nafs-e-Malaika Hai Har Taar-e-Qaba-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Sab Khol De Uqdahaa-e-Mushkil Ay Naakhun-e-Paa-e-Ghaus-e-Azam Kya Un Ki Sana Likhun Hasan Main Jaan Baad Fida-e-Ghaus-e-Azam
