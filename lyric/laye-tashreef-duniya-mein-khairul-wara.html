Laye Tashreef Duniya Mein Khairul Wara

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Maulana Munir Ahmad
tal'al-badru 'alaina min saniyyaatil-wadaa'i wajab-shshukru 'alaina ma d'a lillaahi daa'i laaye tashreef duniya mein khair-ul-wara haqq-o-baatil hua aamne-saamne kufr ab sharm se munh chhupaane lage aaye jab mustafa aamne-saamne chaand se khoob-tar hai rukh-e-mustafa un ko dekha to ye 'aaisha ne kaha pheeki pheeki si lagti thi us ki ziya chaand jab tak raha aamne-saamne laaye tashreef duniya mein khair-ul-wara haqq-o-baatil hua aamne-saamne kufr ab sharm se munh chhupaane lage aaye jab mustafa aamne-saamne ek din bu-jehl ne nabi se kaha ye bata aaj hai meri mutthi mein kya dast-e-bu-jehl mein kankri ne kiya wasf-e-shah-e-huda aamne-saamne laaye tashreef duniya mein khair-ul-wara haqq-o-baatil hua aamne-saamne kufr ab sharm se munh chhupaane lage aaye jab mustafa aamne-saamne kufr ne phir uthaaya ye fitna naya hai nabi to kare chaand tukde zara dekhte dekhte chaand shaq ho gaya jab nabi ne kaha aamne-saamne laaye tashreef duniya mein khair-ul-wara haqq-o-baatil hua aamne-saamne kufr ab sharm se munh chhupaane lage aaye jab mustafa aamne-saamne qatl ka jab 'umar ne iraada kiya jazba-e-shauq araqam ke ghar le gaya dekhte hi jamaal-e-rukh-e-mustafa dil se kalma padha aamne-saamne laaye tashreef duniya mein khair-ul-wara haqq-o-baatil hua aamne-saamne kufr ab sharm se munh chhupaane lage aaye jab mustafa aamne-saamne gum-shuda ek sooi ka hai waaqi'a jaane kab tak use dhoondteen 'aaisha raat ki teergi mein ujaala liye aa gaye mustafa aamne-saamne laaye tashreef duniya mein khair-ul-wara haqq-o-baatil hua aamne-saamne kufr ab sharm se munh chhupaane lage aaye jab mustafa aamne-saamne
