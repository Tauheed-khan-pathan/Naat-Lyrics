Mere Aaqa ! Mere Maula ! Mujhe Tayba Bula Leeje

Naat • Shayar: Muhammad Asim ul Qadri
Naat Khawan: Ghulam Mustafa Qadri
Mere Aaqa ! Mere Maula ! Mujhe Tayba Bula Leeje Meri Be-Chain Aankhon Ko Hara-Gumbad Dikha Deeje Mere Aaqa ! Mere Maula ! Mujhe Tayba Bula Leeje Madina Jab Koi Jaata Hai To Dil Mera Rota Hai Bula Leeje Mujhe Bhi Ab, Mere Aaqa ! Bula Leeje Mere Aaqa ! Mere Maula ! Mujhe Tayba Bula Leeje Tawaaf-e-Khaana-e-Kaa'ba Karun, Aisa Karam Kar Do Haram Ke, Ya Rasoolallah ! Minaare Bhi Dikha Deeje Mere Aaqa ! Mere Maula ! Mujhe Tayba Bula Leeje Gilaaf-e-Khaana-e-Kaa'ba Pakad Kar Roun Jee Bhar Kar Sunaaun Haal-e-Dil Apna, Wo Lamha Ab 'Ata Keeje Mere Aaqa ! Mere Maula ! Mujhe Tayba Bula Leeje Main Jaana Chaahta Hun, Muflisi Jaane Nahin Deti Khuda Ke Waaste, Aaqa ! Meri Gurbat Mita Deeje Mere Aaqa ! Mere Maula ! Mujhe Tayba Bula Leeje Na Jaane Kab Farishta Maut Ka Aa Jaae, Ai Aaqa ! Khuda-Ra, Maut Se Pahle Mujhe Rauza Dikha Deeje Mere Aaqa ! Mere Maula ! Mujhe Tayba Bula Leeje Kabootar Dekh Lun, Ai Kaash ! Main Shahr-e-Madina Ke Ai Aaqa ! Gulshan-e-Tayba Ke Bulbul Bhi Dikha Deeje Mere Aaqa ! Mere Maula ! Mujhe Tayba Bula Leeje Tamanna Hai Gulaam-e-Mustafa Ki Aur 'Aasim Ki Baqee'-e-Paak Mein Madafan Hamen, Aaqa ! 'Ata Keeje Mere Aaqa ! Mere Maula ! Mujhe Tayba Bula Leeje
