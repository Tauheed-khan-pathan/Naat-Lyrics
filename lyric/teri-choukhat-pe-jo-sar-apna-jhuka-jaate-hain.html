Teri Choukhat Pe Jo Sar Apna Jhuka Jaate Hain

Naat • Shayar: Akhtar Raza Khan
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Teri choukhat pe jo sar apna jhuka jaate hain Har bulandi ko wahi neecha dikha jaate hain Sarfaraazi-e-ajal un ko mila karti hai Nakhwat-e-sar jo tere dar pe mita jaate hain Doobey rehte hain teri yaad mein jo sham-o-sahar Doobton ko wahi saahil se laga jaate hain Ay maseeha tere beemar hain aise beemaar Jahaan bhar ka dukh dard mita jaate hain Marne waale rukh-e-zayba pe tere jaan-e-jahaan Aysh-e-jaawed ke asraar bataa jaate hain Aasmaan tujh se uthaaye na uthen ge sunn le Hijr ke sadme jo ushshaq utha jaate hain Zikr-e-sarkar bhi kya aag hai jis se sunni Baithe baiythe dil-e-najdi ko jala jaate hain Jin ko sheerni-e-milad se ghin aati hai Aankh ke andhey unhen kawwa khila jaate hain Dasht-e-Taybah mein nahin keel ka khatka Akhtar Naazuk andaam wahaan barhanaah paa jaate hain
