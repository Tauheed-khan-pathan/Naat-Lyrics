Sachchi Baat Sikhate Ye Hai

Naat • Shayar: Ala Hazrat
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Sachchi baat sikhate ye hai Sidhi rah chalate ye hai Halti naav jamate ye hai Tuti aas bandhate ye hai Jalti jaan bujhate ye hai Roti ankh hansate ye hai Rabb hai mu'ti ye hain qasim Rizk us ka hai khilate ye hai Us ki bakhshish in ka sadqa Deta wo hai dilate ye hai Inna aatayna kal kausar Sari kasrat paate ye hai Thanda thanda mitha mitha Pite ham hain pilate ye hai Naz'a-e-rooh mein aasani de Kalma yaad dilate ye hai Marqad mein bandon ko thapak kar Mithi nind sulate ye hai Maa jab ek lote ko chhode Aa aa keh ke bulate ye hai Sallim sallim ki dharas se Pul se paar chalate ye hai Apne karam se ham halkon ka Palla bhari banate ye hai Apni bani ham aap bigade Kaun banaye banate ye hai Faiz-e-jaleel khalil se puchho Aag mein baag lagate ye hai In ke haath mein har kunji hai Malik-e-kul kehlate ye hai Qasr-e-dana tak kis ki rasaai Jaate ye hai aate ye hai Keh do raza se khush ho khush reh Muzda raza ka sunate ye hai
