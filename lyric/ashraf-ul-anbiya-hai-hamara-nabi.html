Ashraf-ul-Anbiya Hai Hamara Nabi

Naat • Shayar: Akbar Shah Warsi
Ashraf-ul-Anbiya hai hamara Nabi Woh Nabi kaun? Ummat ka pyara Nabi Bulbulon ne jo dekhi malahat teri Gul ko sadqay mein tujh par utara Nabi Tum ho woh husn walay keh Allah ne Apna mehboob keh kar pukara Nabi Aur Nabiyon ko makhsoos thein ummatain Har ik aalam ka baadi hamara Nabi Aaj Yusuf bhi un ki ghulami mein hai Tum ne dekha Zulaikha hamara Nabi Hai shafaat ke sehray ki sar pe phaban Aaj dulha bana hai hamara Nabi Behr-e-isyaan ke girdab mein nao hai Duba duba mujhe do sahara Nabi Kooch-e-Akbar ka jis waqt duniya se ho Lab pe jari ho kalma tumhara Nabi
