Haqeeqat Ki Zuban Siddiq-e-Akbar | Sadaqat Ka Bayan Siddiq-e-Akbar

Manqabat • Shayar: Abdul Sattar Niazi
Naat Khawan: Zohaib Ashrafi, Ubaid Raza Qadri
Haqeeqat ki zubaan siddiq-e-akbar Sadaaqat ka bayan siddiq-e-akbar Wo haq ka tarjumaan siddiq-e-akbar Nabi ka raz-daan siddiq-e-akbar Kabhi na saath chhoda Mustafa ka Jahan aaqa wahan siddiq-e-akbar Mehekta phool gulzaar-e-nabi ka Bahar-e-be-khizaan siddiq-e-akbar Nabi ke naam par sab kuch fida hai Mohabbat ka nishaan siddiq-e-akbar Nabi ne 'azamtein wo un ko bakhshin Zameen par aasman siddiq-e-akbar Wo pehla yaar mahboob-e-khuda ka Waqar-e-dilbraan siddiq-e-akbar Niyazi ! main aur un ke wasf likkhoon Kahan main aur kahan siddiq-e-akbar
