Khudi Ka Sirr e Nihan La Ilaha Illallah

Hamd • Shayar: Allama Iqbal
KHudi ka sirr-e-nihaa.n la-ilaaha-illallah KHudi hai teG fasaa.n la-ilaaha-illallah Ye daur apne baraaheem ki talaash me.n hai Sanam-kada hai jahaa.n la-ilaaha-illallah Kiya hai tu ne mataa-e-Guroor ka sauda Fareb-e-sood-o-ziyaa.n la-ilaaha-illallah Ye maal-o-daulat-e-duniya ye rishta-o-paiwand Butaan-e-wahm-o-gumaa.n la-ilaaha-illallah KHirad hui hai zamaan o makaa.n ki zunnaari Na hai zamaa.n na makaa.n la-ilaaha-illallah Ye naGma fasl-e-gul-o-laala ka nahi.n paaband Bahaar ho ki KHizaa.n la-ilaaha-illallah Agarche but hai.n jamaa'at ki aasteeno.n me.n Mujhe hai hukm-e-azaa.n la-ilaaha-illallah
