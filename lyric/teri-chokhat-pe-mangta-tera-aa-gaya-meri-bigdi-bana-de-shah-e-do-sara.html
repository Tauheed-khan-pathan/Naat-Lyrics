Teri Chokhat Pe Mangta Tera Aa Gaya | Meri Bigdi Bana De Shah-e-do-sara

Naat • Shayar: Owais Raza Qadri
Naat Khawan: Owais Raza Qadri
Teri Chaukhat Pe Mangta Tera Aa Gaya Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Main Saraapa Khata, Tu Saraapa Ata Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Mujh Kameene Pe Tu Ne Ye Ehsaan Kiya Maah-e-Ramzan Mein Apna Mehmaan Kiya Ye Tera Hi Karam Hai Teri Hi Ata Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Main To Qabil Na Tha, Ay Shah-e-Do-Jahan ! Mujh Sa Aasi Kahan Aur Tera Dar Kahan Ye Tera Hi Karam Hai Teri Hi Ata Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Tere Rauze Ka Deedar Jis Ne Kiya Tujh Se Muzda Shafaat Ka Us Ne Liya Main Bhi To Ho Gaya Aaj Haazir, Shaha ! Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Door Ranj-o-Alam Saare Kar De, Shaha ! Mere Daman Ko Bhi Aaj Bhar De, Shaha ! Dar Pe Aaya Tere Bheek Lene Gada Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Tere Hi Sar Saja Hai Shafaat Ka Taaj Laaj Waale ! Tere Haath Hai Meri Laaj Bakhshawaana Mujhe Bhi Ba-Roz-e-Jaza Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Kab Piyunga Main Sharbat Teri Deed Ka Ab To Rakh Le Bharam Meri Ummeed Ka Ik Jhalak Hi Khuda-Ra Dikha De, Shaha ! Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Be-Sahara Hoon Main, Gham Ka Maara Hoon Main Laakh Paapi Sahi Par Tumhara Hoon Main Bahar-e-Gaus-ul-Wara Apne Seene Laga Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Jaan Duniya Ke Gham Mein Sulagti Rahi Aankh Meri Isi Gham Mein Roti Rahi Mujh Ko Bahar-e-Raza Apne Gham Mein Rula Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Ay Kas-e-Be-Kasaan ! Dar Tera Chhod Kar Jhirkiyaan Ghair Ki Khaane Jaaun Kidhar Tere Tukdon Pe Palta Rahoon Main Sada Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Jab Madine Se Rukhsat Main Hone Lagoon Hichkiyaan Baandh Kar, Kaash ! Rone Lagoon Mujh Ko Soz-e-Jigar Aisa Kar De Ata Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara ! Hai Muqaddar Mein Gar Lautna Apne Ghar Kaash ! Ho Jaye Aisa Ki Qalb-o-Jigar Chhod Jaye Idhar Ye Ubaid-e-Raza Meri Bigdi Bana De, Shah-e-Do-Sara !
