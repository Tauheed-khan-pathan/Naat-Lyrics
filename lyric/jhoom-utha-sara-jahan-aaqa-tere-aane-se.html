Jhoom Utha Sara Jahan Aaqa Tere Aane Se

Naat • Shayar: Allama Nisar Ali Ujagar
Naat Khawan: Hafiz Ghulam Mustafa Qadri, Rao Ali Hasnain
Jhoom utha saara jahan, saara jahan, saara jahan Aaqa! Tere aane se, Maula! Tere aane se Ya rasoolallah! Ya habiballah! Ya rasoolallah! Ya habiballah! Habada Ya Mustafa! Habada Ya Mustafa! Habada Ya Mustafa! Habada Ya Mustafa! Kya kashish hai, kaisa raushan aamina ka chand hai Is ke aage rang dekho chand ka bhi mand hai Jhoom utha saara jahan, saara jahan, saara jahan Aaqa! Tere aane se, Maula! Tere aane se Baarwein ke din teri azmat pe hon laakhon salaam Mustafa ki aamad-o-besat pe hon laakhon salaam Jhoom utha saara jahan, saara jahan, saara jahan Aaqa! Tere aane se, Maula! Tere aane se Mustafa ka Jashn dekho raushni ka baagh hai Raat ke maathe pe subh-e-zindagi ka baagh hai Jhoom utha saara jahan, saara jahan, saara jahan Aaqa! Tere aane se, Maula! Tere aane se Jashn-e-Milad-e-mohamed hum manaenge zaroor Jalne waalon ko qasam Rab ki jalaenge zaroor Jhoom utha saara jahan, saara jahan, saara jahan Aaqa! Tere aane se, Maula! Tere aane se Falsafa ye zindagi ka zaahir-o-baatin se hai Silsila khushiyan manane ka azal ke din se hai Jhoom utha saara jahan, saara jahan, saara jahan Aaqa! Tere aane se, Maula! Tere aane se Mustafa ki saltanat hai jis taraf bhi dekhiye Saara aalam milkiyat hai jis taraf bhi dekhiye Jhoom utha saara jahan, saara jahan, saara jahan Aaqa! Tere aane se, Maula! Tere aane se Aamad-e-shaah-e-huda se mil gayi raah-e-najaat Wahshaton mein ho gayi hai pur-sukoon insaan ki zaat Jhoom utha saara jahan, saara jahan, saara jahan Aaqa! Tere aane se, Maula! Tere aane se Ae Ujagar! Sab dinon se din Nabi ka la-jawab Saare mausam, sab ruton se din Nabi ka la-jawab Jhoom utha saara jahan, saara jahan, saara jahan Aaqa! Tere aane se, Maula! Tere aane se
