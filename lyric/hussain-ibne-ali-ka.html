Hussain Ibne Ali Ka

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Sajid Jafri
hussain ibne ali ka kalma padha diya tu ne la ilaah ko kata ke shaane alam utha kar basa diya tu ne la ilaah ko banayi zeenat jo nauhakhwan ki zameer goondha jo matami ka nabi mila ke ali mila ke mila diya tu ne la ilaah ko hussain ibne ali ka kalma labalab apne lahoo se bhar ke ye tishnagi se kasheed kar ke yehi ke abid baqa ka saaghar pila diya tu ne la ilaah ko hussain ibne ali ka kalma diye hai baazu de di hai saansein diya hai chehra de di hai aankhein khuda ye hoi ye jaa ke pooche ke kya diya tu ne la ilaah ko hussain ibne ali ka kalma subhana rabb e azeem hai tu hai rabb e aala kareem hai tu nashayb e maqtal se taa ba sidra saja diya tu ne la ilaah ko hussain ibne ali ka kalma hai sar ka nauha malak ka nauha zameen ka nauha falak ka nauha har ek zamane mein khud ujal ke basa diya tu ne la ilaah ko hussain ibne ali ka kalma khuda ki tasbeeh bhi yehi hai naved ka bhi yehi hai sajda lahoo ka apne libaas e dillat pinha diya tu ne la ilaah ko hussain ibne ali ka kalma
