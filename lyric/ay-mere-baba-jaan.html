Ay Mere Baba Jaan

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Ameer Hasan Aamir
maqtal ke sannate mein nauha karti hai naadan ay mere baba jaan ay mere baba jaan baaki nahi ab mere jeene ka koi imkaan ay mere baba jaan ay mere baba jaan jangal hainghar bhi nahi hai koi yawar bhi nahi hai ab to akbar bhi nahi hai sar par chadar bhi nahi hai yun hi ab qaidi ho kar jaana hai mujhko zindan ay mere baba jaan kab se khaati hoo tamache dono aariz hai neele pehnaye thay jo bunde wo bhi aada ne cheene beti ki haalat dekho zakhmi hai dono hi kaan ay mere baba jaan loota qaima bhi ghar bhi gehwara bhi bistar bhi maqna bhi aur chadar bhi hasli bhi aur gauhar bhi aada ne aag laga kar loote hai ghar ke saaman ay mere baba jaan ye akbar ki mayyat hai wo asghar ki turbat hai ye farwa ki hasrat hai wo zainab ki ghairat hai bikhre pade hai ran mein zehra ke dil ke armaan ay mere baba jaan tum thay ahmed ke nawase doori thi kya darya se zabha huwe kyun pyase batlaun kya dunya se tum ko kalmagoyo ne maara bula ke mehman ay mere baba jaan wo hai ammu ke baazu ye hai akbar ke gaysu qasim bikhre hai har soo mai hoo aur mere aansu kal tak thi is maqtal mein mere kunbe ki kya shaan ay mere baba jaan kufe mein ghabraungi kyun kar himmat laaungi balwe mein sharmaungi ghairat se mar jaungi rukh par matti hi mal do hone na paaye pehchaan ay mere baba jaan majalis mein jab aate hai ashk e gham barsaate hai matam mein kho jaate hai rote hai rulwaate hai nayyar aur aamir ki tum mushkil kar dena aasaan ay mere baba jaan
