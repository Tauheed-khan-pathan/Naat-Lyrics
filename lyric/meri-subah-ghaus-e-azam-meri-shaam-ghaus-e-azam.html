Meri Subah Ghaus-e-Azam, Meri Shaam Ghaus-e-Azam

Manqabat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Syed Abdul Wasi Qadri
Meri subah Ghaus-e-Azam, meri shaam Ghaus-e-Azam Meri zindagi ka pal pal tere naam, Ghaus-e-Azam! Tere naam se bane hain baday kaam, Ghaus-e-Azam! Mere kitne kaam ka hai tera naam, Ghaus-e-Azam! Tu Nabi ka laadla hai, tu Ali ka hai dulara Teri zaat par buzurgi hai tamaam, Ghaus-e-Azam! Mushkil bhare safar mein main na tha kabhi akela Mere saath chal raha tha tera naam, Ghaus-e-Azam! Teri zaat se hai jisko dil-o-jaan se aqeedat Na bigarh sakega uska koi kaam, Ghaus-e-Azam! Hum sab pe daal do ab nazar-e-karam Khuda-ra Mehfil mein keh rahe hain yeh ghulaam, Ghaus-e-Azam! Meri subah Ghaus-e-Azam, meri shaam Ghaus-e-Azam Meri zindagi ka pal pal tere naam, Ghaus-e-Azam!
