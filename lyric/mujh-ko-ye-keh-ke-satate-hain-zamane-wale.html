Mujh Ko Ye Keh Ke Satate Hain Zamane Wale

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Unknown
Mujh ko ye keh ke sataate hain zamaane waale Kab bulaaenge tujhe sab ko bulaane waale Raasta rok na paaenge zamaane waale Jab madine mein bulaaenge bulaane waale Jab chalun shehr-e-madina se to sarkaar kahen Laut aa ja, o mere shehr se jaane waale Mujh ko is baat ka har raat yaqeen rehta hai Aap hain aaj mere khwaab mein aane waale Aap ko dekh ke mehshar mein kahenge 'aasi Aa gae ham ko jahannam se bachaane waale Kaash! Mehshar mein ye keh keh ke bulaaen aaqa Aa idhar aa, mere naalain uthaane waale Deen ki raah mein ghar-baar luta dete hain Aise hote hain Muhammad ke gharaane waale Ab koi khauf nahin mujh ko, zamaane waalo! Mere khwaja hain meri laaj bachaane waale Hashr tak phoole-phale maslak-e-aala-hazrat Mit gae khud hi zamaane se mitaane waale Un se keh do ki wo aaieene mein chehra dekhen Apne jaisa mere aaqa ko bataane waale
