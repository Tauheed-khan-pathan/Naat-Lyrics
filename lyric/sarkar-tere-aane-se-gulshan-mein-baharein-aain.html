Sarkar Tere Aane Se Gulshan Mein Baharein Aain

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Haji Mushtaq Attari, Syed Hassan Ullah Hussaini
Rooh-e-Arz-O-Sama Ban Ke Aaya Sab Rasool-e-Khuda Ban Ke Aaye Wo Habeeb-e-Khuda Ban Ke Aaya Sarkaar ! Tere Aane Se Gulshan Mein Bahaaren Aain Tere Noor Se, Pyaare Aaqa ! 'Aalam Mein Raushani Chhaai Sarkaar Tere Aane Se Sab Chhat Gae Gam Ke Saaye, Sarkaar Mere Jab Aaye Ab Chhaa Gai Har-Soo Khushiyan, Masroor Hai Saari Khudaai Sarkaar ! Tere Aane Se Gulshan Mein Bahaaren Aain Tere Noor Se, Pyaare Aaqa ! 'Aalam Mein Raushani Chhaai Sarkaar Tere Aane Se Noor Waale Mustafa Aa Gae, Chhaa Gae Noor Waale Mustafa Aa Gae, Chhaa Gae Jab Jalwa-Figan Hue Aaqa, Sajde Mein Jhuka Tha Kaa'ba Ai Aamina ! Kaisi Teri Qismat Ye Khuda Ne Banaai Sarkaar ! Tere Aane Se Gulshan Mein Bahaaren Aain Tere Noor Se, Pyaare Aaqa ! 'Aalam Mein Raushani Chhaai Sarkaar Tere Aane Se Noor Waale Mustafa Aa Gae, Chhaa Gae Noor Waale Mustafa Aa Gae, Chhaa Gae Jalwon Se Tumhaare, Aaqa ! Phaili Hai Noor Ki Kirnen Khurshid-O-Qamar Ne, Aaqa ! Tum Se Hi Ziya Hai Paai Sarkaar ! Tere Aane Se Gulshan Mein Bahaaren Aain Tere Noor Se, Pyaare Aaqa !
