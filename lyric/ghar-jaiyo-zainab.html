Ghar Jaiyo Zainab

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Raza Abbas Zaidi
sheh ne kaha jab shaam se ghar jaaiyo zainab sughra ko meri seene se liptaiyo zainab maange jo kaneezi mein sakina ko sitamgar tu minnate kar kar usay chudwaiyo zainab sheh ne kaha jab shaam se ghar jaaiyo zainab mil jaaye agar pani to sab pyason se pehle pani meri sakina ko pilwaiyo zainab sheh ne kaha jab shaam se ghar jaaiyo zainab dekhe jo nishan rassi ke sughra dam e giryan baazu mein rasan thi na ye farmiyo zainab sheh ne kaha jab shaam se ghar jaaiyo zainab mil jaaye agar chadarein to sab bewon se pehle chadar tu meri kubra ko pinwaiyo zainab sheh ne kaha jab shaam se ghar jaaiyo zainab mil jaaye agar doodh to do koozon mein bhar ke fateha mere asghar ki tu dilwaiyo zainab sheh ne kaha jab shaam se ghar jaaiyo zainab
