Bajuz Khwaja tasawwur mein koi ab aa nahi sakta Khayal ghair mere dil pe hargiz chaa nahi sakta Mila hai mil raha hai aur milega to usi dar se Mere Khwaja teri chaukhat se khali jaa nahi sakta Guzarte hain jo sadme rozo shab mere dil par Samjh lije ise khud aap main samjha nahi sakta Tumhi mere Masiha mere Maawa mere Malja ho Tumhara chchod kar dar aur dar par jaa nahi sakta Bhala bulwa ke mehmanon se karta hai koi parda Samajh mein ye nirala sa tareeka aa nahi sakta Gada mashoor hoon khwaja ka kehte hain mujhe Sayyid Kisi ke aage apna haath main faila nahi sakta
