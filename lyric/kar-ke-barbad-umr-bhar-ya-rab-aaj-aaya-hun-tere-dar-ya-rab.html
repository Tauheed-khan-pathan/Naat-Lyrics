Kar Ke Barbad Umr Bhar Ya Rab | Aaj Aaya Hun Tere Dar Ya Rab

Hamd • Shayar: Owais Raza Qadri

Kar Ke Barbaad Umr-Bhar, Ya Rab ! Aaj Aaya Hoon Tere Dar, Ya Rab ! Rakht-e-Aamaal Sar-Ba-Sar, Ya Rab ! Hai Gunahon Se Tar-Ba-Tar, Ya Rab ! Khauf Aata Hai, Kaanp Jaata Hoon Apne Aamaal Dekh Kar, Ya Rab ! Roz-o-Shab, Aah ! Zahar-e-Isyaan Se Kat Raha Hai Mera Jigar, Ya Rab ! Nafs-o-Shaitaan Le Ure Mere Dil Ka Sukh-Chain Loot Kar, Ya Rab ! Mujh Ko Jhulsa Raha Hai Andar Se Mere Isyaan Ka Ye Saqar, Ya Rab ! Kaun Bakhshega Juz Tere, Maula ! Meri Tauba Qubool Kar, Ya Rab ! Mere Saare Gunah Dho Degi Teri Rehmat Ki Ik Nazar, Ya Rab !
