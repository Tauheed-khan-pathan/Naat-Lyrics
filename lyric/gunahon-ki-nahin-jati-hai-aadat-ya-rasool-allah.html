Gunahon Ki Nahin Jati Hai Aadat Ya Rasool Allah

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Haji Mushtaq Attari
Gunahon ki nahin jati hai aadat, ya rasoolallah ! Tumhin ab kuchh karo, mah-e-risalat ya rasoolallah ! Gunahon se mujhe ho jaye nafrat, ya rasoolallah ! Nikal jaye buri har ek khaslat, ya rasoolallah ! Gunah lamha-ba-lamha haye ! ab barhte hi jate hain Nahin par is pe haye kuchh nadamat, ya rasoolallah ! Gunah kar kar ke haye ! ho gaya dil sakht patthar se Karoon kis se kahan ja kar shikayat, ya rasoolallah ! Main bachna chahta hoon haye ! phir bhi bach nahin pata Gunahon ki pari hai aisi aadat, ya rasoolallah ! Kamar aa'mal-e-bad ne haye ! meri tor kar rakh di Tabahi se bacha lo, jan-e-rahmat ya rasoolallah ! Mere munh ki siyahi se andheri rat sharmaye Mera chehra ho taban, noor-e-izzat ya rasoolallah ! Ba-waqt-e-naza'a, aaqa ! ho na jaoon main kahin barbad Mera eeman rakh lena salamat, ya rasoolallah ! Tere rab ki qasam main laiq-e-nar-e-jahannam hoon Bacha sakti hai bas teri shafa'at, ya rasoolallah ! Yahan jaise hamari aib-poshi aap karte hain Wahan bhi aap rakh lijega izzat, ya rasoolallah ! Fasad-e-nafs-e-zalim se bacha lo az pa-e-shaikhain Karo shaitan se meri hifazat, ya rasoolallah ! Sahi jati nahin hain sakhtiyan sakrat ki, sarkar ! Sar-e-balin ab aao, jan-e-rahmat ya rasoolallah ! Mera ye khwab ho jaye, shaha ! sharminda-e-ta'bir Madine mein piyoon jam-e-shahadat, ya rasoolallah ! Mere dil se hawas duniya ki daulat ki nikal jaye Ata kar do mujhe bas apani ulfat, ya rasoolallah ! Mere aansoo na hon barbad duniya ki mohabbat mein Rulaye bas mujhe teri mohabbat, ya rasoolallah ! Phansa jata hai duniya ki mohabbat mein dil-e-attar Karo attar se ye door aafat, ya rasoolallah !
