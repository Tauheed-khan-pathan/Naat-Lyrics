Ghamzada Hai Dil Mera Aye Rab Ke Mehman Al Wada

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Mehmood Attari
Gham-zada hai dil mera, ai rab ke mehman ! al-wada Aah ! ab tu chal diya, ai mah-e-ramzan ! al-wada Al-firaqu wal-firaq, ai mah-e-ghufran ! al-wada Al-wada'u wal-wada, ai mah-e-subhan ! al-wada Teri aamad ki khushi mein ho gaye dil bagh bagh Teri rukhsat ke sabab hain sab pareshan, al-wada Tere aane se jahan mein aa gai thin raunkein Sab saman soona hai ab, maula ke mehman ! al-wada Mujrimon ko tere daman mein aman milti rahi Fazl-e-rab se maghfirat thi tujh mein aasan, al-wada Waqt-e-iftar-o-sahar ki raunqein jati rahin Kya baraste the karam ke abr-e-baran, al-wada Chhor kar rota hua tu gham-zadon ko chal diya Teri furqat mein tarapte hain musalman, al-wada Char janib hijr-e-ramzan mein udasi chha gai Kah rahe hain khoob ro ro kar musalman, al-wada Mah-e-ramzan ! ja raha hai haye ham ko chhor kar Char din ka rah gaya hai aah ! mehman, al-wada Mah-e-ramzan ! tere shaidai sabhi tujh par fida Tujh pe sadqe, tujh pe wari, tujh pe qurban, al-wada Pesh-e-haq karna shafa'at tu hamari ha mein Mah-e-ramzan ! ho karam ham par ho ehasan, al-wada Sal-e-aainda bhi phir, attar ! ramzan aayega Kya mere dam ka bharosa, mah-e-ramzan ! al-wada
