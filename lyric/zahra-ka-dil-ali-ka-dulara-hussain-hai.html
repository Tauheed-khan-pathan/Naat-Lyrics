Zahra Ka Dil Ali Ka Dulara Hussain Hai

Manqabat • Shayar: Hafizullah Qasmi
Naat Khawan: Sandali Ahmad
Zahra Ka Dil, Ali Ka Dulara Husain Hai Pyaare Nabi Ki Aankhon Ka Taara Husain Hai Har Jannati Jawaan Ki Qayadat Hai Us Ke Haath Allah Ki Nazar Mein Yun Pyaara Husain Hai Kaandhe Pe Jo Rasool Ke Hota Raha Sawaar Qismat Ka Ek Shokh Sitara Husain Hai Maidaan-e-Karbala Se Hai Goonji Yahi Sada Islam Ka Sutoon-o-Sahara Husain Hai Sar Ko Kata Ke, Momino! Saabit Ye Kar Diya Imaan Aur Yaqeen Ka Dhaara Husain Hai Islam Ke Liye Thi Shahadat Husain Ki Saugaat-e-Nanajaan Pe Waara Husain Hai Uqba Ka Tajwar Hi Nahi Hai Ali Ka Laal Duniya Ke Har Jawaan Mein Bhi Nyaara Husain Hai Jis Ne Yazeediyon Se Sulh-e-Kul Na Ki Kabhi Batil Ke Sar Pe Haq Ka Sharara Husain Hai
