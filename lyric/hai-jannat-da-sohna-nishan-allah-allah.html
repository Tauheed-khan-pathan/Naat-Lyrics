Hai Jannat Da Sohna Nishan, Allah Allah

Naat • Shayar: Abdul Sattar Niazi
Hai Jannat Da Sohna Nishan, Allah Allah Muhammad Da Sohna Garaan, Allah Allah Ohde Bol Misri De Sheere Ton Sheerin Oh Sohne Di Sheerin Zabaan, Allah Allah Hai Mushkil-Kusha Atte Rehmat Saraapa Mere Kamli Wale Da Naam, Allah Allah Adha Maalik-e-Kull Ae, Te Khatm-e-Rusul Ae Hai Dohan Jahaanan Di Jaan, Allah Allah Jehdi Thaan Te Wasda Ae, Ummat Da Waali Hai Arshaan Ton Afzal Oh Thaan, Allah Allah Jithe Mildi Taskeen, Tuttiyaan Dilaan Nu Oh Gumbad-e-Khizri Di Chhaan, Allah Allah Padhe Jis Diyaan Jagg Naataan, Niazi Zabaan Mera, Ohda Bayaan, Allah Allah
