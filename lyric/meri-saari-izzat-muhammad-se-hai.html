Meri Saari Izzat Muhammad Se Hai

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Meri Saari izzat Muhammad Se hai Ye daulat ye shohrat Muhammad se hai Ye mera sukhan ye meri khamoshi Qalam ki ye rif’at Muhammad se hai Mujhe chahte hai sabhi toot kar Ye nazre inayat Muhammad se hai Hai thandak nigaho me aur noor bhi Dilo me hararat Muhammad se hai Na kyun saara aalam ho un par fida Ke aalam me rehmat Muhammad se hai Abu Bakr Siddique ka martaba Aur inki sadaqat Muhammad se hai Woh Farooq e Aazam Umar ba safa Aur unki adaalat Muhammad se hai Hussain Ibne Haider ki har ek ada Aur unki shahadat Muhammad se hai Woh meraj o meelad ki sa’ate Woh khalwat ye jalwat Muhammad se hai Woh Roza Namaz ho ke Haj o zakaat Ye zauke ibaadat Muhammad se hai Tilawat sakhawat duroodo salaam Sabhi me halawat Muhammad se hai Woh Gaus o Qutub hon ke Abdaal hon Sabhi ki karamat Muhammad se hai Sana unki shewa hai Rizwan ka Ise khaas nisbat Muhammad se hai
