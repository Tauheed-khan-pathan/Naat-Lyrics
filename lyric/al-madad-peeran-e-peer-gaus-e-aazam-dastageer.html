Al Madad Peeran-e-Peer Gaus-e-Aazam Dastageer

Manqabat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Unknown
al-madad peeraan-e-peer, gaus-e-aa'zam dastageer! al-madad peeraan-e-peer, ghaus-e-aa'zam dastageer! rutba ye wilaayat mein, kya ghaus ne paaya hai allah ne waliyon ka sultaan banaaya hai hai dast-e-'ali sar par, hasnain ka saaya hai mere ghaus ne thokar mein murdon ko jilaaya hai al-madad peeraan-e-peer, ghaus-e-aa'zam dastageer! laakhon ne usi dar se taqdeer banaayi hai bagdaad sanwariya ki har baat niraali hai doobi hui kashti bhi dariya se nikaali hai ye naam adab se lo ye naam jhalaali hai al-madad peeraan-e-peer, ghaus-e-aa'zam dastageer! har fikr se tum ho kar aazaad chale jaao le kar ke labon par tum fariyaad chale jaao milna hai agar tumko waliyon ke shahenshaah se khwaaja se ijaazat lo baghdaad chale jaao al-madad peeraan-e-peer, ghaus-e-aa'zam dastageer! ya ghaus! karam kardo ya ghaus! karam kardo ye deen ki daulat se daaman sab ka bhar do bas itni guzaarish hai bas ek nazar kar do baghdaad ki galiyon mein chhota sa mujhe ghar do al-madad peeraan-e-peer, ghaus-e-aa'zam dastageer! hum ghaus ki mahefil ko ta-hashr sajaaeinge hum ghaus ka langar bhi ta-'umr pakaaeinge basti mein garibon ko, bhukon ko khilaeinge phir ghaus agar chaahein, baghdaad bhi jaaeinge al-madad peeraan-e-peer, ghaus-e-aa'zam dastageer! andaaz bayaan unka, hum kar nahin paaeinge ajmer se ho kar hum baghdaad ko jaaeinge jab aae bala sar par, hum nko pukaareinge tum dil se sada to do, wo haath badaaeinge al-madad peeraan-e-peer, ghaus-e-aa'zam dastageer! jubbe ko churaane ki, niyyat se jo aaya tha sumsaan andhere ka, jo laabh uthaaya tha ya ghaus! du'aa kar ke, maalik se nida kar ke tumne hi use rab ka mahboob banaaya tha al-madad peeraan-e-peer, ghaus-e-aa'zam dastageer! bachchi bhi salaamat hai, bachcha bhi salaamat hai har ek baraati ka kumba bhi salaamat hai farmaan tumhaara yun dariya ki taraf pahuncha dulhan bhi salaamat hai, dulha bhi salaamat hai al-madad peeraan-e-peer, ghaus-e-aa'zam dastageer! ek baar bulaaya tha, ek baar bulaaeinge ek roz yaqinan hum, baghdaad ko jaaeinge bachpan se meri maa ne mujhko ye sikhaaya hai jab aae koi aafat, ya ghaus! pukaara hai al-madad peeraan-e-peer, ghaus-e-aa'zam dastageer! milne ko sham' se ye parwaana tadapta hai qismat ke andheron mein deewaana bhatakta hai ise ishq-e-haqeeqi mein itna to asar aaye jab band karun aankhein baghdaad nazar aaye
