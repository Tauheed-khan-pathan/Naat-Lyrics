Ik Baar Madine Mein Ho Jae Mera Jaana

Naat • Shayar: Abdul Sattar Niazi
Naat Khawan: Milad Raza Qadri
Ik baar Madine mein ho jaye mera jaana Phir aur na kuch maange Sarkar ka deewana Ik baar Madine mein ho jaye mera jaana Pal pal mera dil tadpe, din-raat kare zari Kab aaoon Madine mein, kab aaye meri baari Kab jaa ke main dekhunga darbar wo shaahaana Ik baar Madine mein ho jaye mera jaana Is aas pe jeeta hoon ik roz bulayenge Aur gumbade-khazra ka deedar karayenge Phir pesh karunga main ashkon bhara nazrana Ik baar Madine mein ho jaye mera jaana Bechain nigahon ko deedar ata kar do Daaman mera khushiyon se ya shahe-umam ! bhar do Aabaad Khuda rakhe aaqa ! tera maykhana Ik baar Madine mein ho jaye mera jaana Itni si tamanna hai, ho jaye agar poori Jaa dekhun Madina main, ho jaye jo manzoori Bin deed kiye shaha ! mar jaye na deewana Ik baar Madine mein ho jaye mera jaana
