Din Dhal Gaya

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Nadeem Sarwar

abbas abbas na laute din dhal gaya shaam aa gayi abbas abbas na laute dar aaye qayame pisar-e-sheh may sitamgar zakhmi kiya durro se tan e abide mustar khayncha sitam afgaaro ne bemaar ka bistar dekha tumhari hai na hai baap ka seena zid karti hai jaan khoti hai ro ro ke hazeena aisa to kabhi dekha na tha bhai khareena kyon aayi na darya pe tumhe yaad sakina kya cheez wahan bha gayi abbas abbas na
