Wo Waqt Yaqeenan

Manqabat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Mir Hassan Mir
wo waqt yaqeenan aayega kabhi ay haqeeqat e muntazar nazar aa libas e majaaz mein ke hazaron sajde tadap rahe hai meri jabeen e niaaz mein wo waqt yaqeenan aayega jab hujjate daur ke haathon mein ghazi ka alam lehrayega wo waqt yaqeenan aayega jis din sajjad ke ashkon ki tadaad batayi jayegi aur un ashkon ke badle mein talwaar chalayi jayegi us sar se jism juda hoga talwaar se jo takrayega alam lehraye ga wo waqt yaqeenan aayega har ek mazloom ki aas hai ye hujjat ka shams tulu hoga phir shaam jigar se utregi sajjad ka khatm ruku hoga phir noor e aman yun phailega har zulm ko rondha jayega alam lehraye ga wo waqt yaqeenan aayega daadi ka haq wo maangega apni talwaar ke saath kabhi jo haath ridayein loot’te hai kat jayenge wo haath sabhi tatheer ke ghar ki surat phir koi ghar na loota jayega alam lehraye ga wo waqt yaqeenan aayega abaad baqayya tha jis jaa wo rauza kaise toota hai kis kis ne yahan kaise kaise sadaat ke ghar ko loota hai talwaar bataati jayegi wo khoon behta jayega alam lehraye ga wo waqt yaqeenan aayega baaghi ki beti jisne bhi choti bibi ko bola tha namoos e nabi ko raahon mein durron se jisne maara tha un sab ko bana kar qaidi wo bazaar e shaam mein layega alam lehraye ga wo waqt yaqeenan aayega phir ahlebait madine mein abaad karenge ghar apna aur lenge bani hashim mil kar kuch pardedaron ka badla abbas ki chup bhi tootegi baysheer bhi taygh uthayega alam lehraye ga wo waqt yaqeenan aayega gohar mulkon ki sarhad se insan taqseem nahi hoga naam nehaad mazahib mein quran taqseem nahi hoga wo aadil sari dunya ko ek adl ka shehr banayega alam lehraye ga wo waqt yaqeenan aayega
