Khoob Aankhon Ne Tere Dar Pe Ye Manzar Dekhe

Naat • Shayar: Abdul Sattar Niazi
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Azam Qadri
Khoob aankhon ne tere dar pe ye manzar dekhe Mangte phirte zamaane ke sikandar dekhe Khoob aankhon ne tere dar pe ye manzar dekhe Aap sa, rahmat-e-aalam ! koi dekha hi nahi Yun to Duniya mein hazaaron hi payambar dekhe Khoob aankhon ne tere dar pe ye manzar dekhe 'aasiyo ! aao chalein sarawar-e-deen ke dar par Josh par un ke karam ke hain samandar dekhe Khoob aankhon ne tere dar pe ye manzar dekhe Dekhna chaahe jo andaz shahanshaahi ke Shah-e-batha ke ghulaamon ko wo aa kar dekhe Khoob aankhon ne tere dar pe ye manzar dekhe Dushman-e-jaan ko nawaza hai du'aaein de kar Rukh-e-rahmat ke na badle huwe tewar dekhe Khoob aankhon ne tere dar pe ye manzar dekhe Phir bula mujh ko Madine mein, Madine waale !
