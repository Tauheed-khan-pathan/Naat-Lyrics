Deen-e-Haq Pe Syeda Ke Laal Ka Ehsaan Hai

Manqabat • Shayar: Allama Nisar Ali Ujagar
Naat Khawan: Zohaib Ashrafi
Khwaja-e-Ajmer ka hum ne suna farmaan hai Deen-e-haq pe sayyida ke laal ka ehsaan hai Padh rahe ho jo namazein, kar rahe ho hajj ada Ronak-e-Ramzan aur manzar hai jo khairaat ka Sadqa-e-shabbir hai, Islam ka e'laan hai Deen-e-haq pe sayyida ke laal ka ehsaan hai Khwaja-e-Ajmer ka hum ne suna farmaan hai Deen-e-haq pe sayyida ke laal ka ehsaan hai Dekh lo tareekh-e-aalam, kis ka ye kirdaar hai Ghar lutaya, sar kataaya, kaisa ye salar hai! Sab shaheedon mein Husain-ibn-e-Ali sultaan hai Deen-e-haq pe sayyida ke laal ka ehsaan hai Khwaja-e-Ajmer ka hum ne suna farmaan hai Deen-e-haq pe sayyida ke laal ka ehsaan hai Malika-e-khuld-e-barein se auraton ka hai waqaar Maadar-e-shabbir! Teri parwarish ki hai bahaar Aaj jo mazboot mera mazhab-o-Imaan hai Deen-e-haq pe sayyida ke laal ka ehsaan hai Khwaja-e-Ajmer ka hum ne suna farmaan hai Deen-e-haq pe sayyida ke laal ka ehsaan hai Neze pe ibn-e-Ali jo mard-e-aalishan hai Kat gaya hai sar magar parhata raha Quran hai Soch ke har ik yazeedi aaj bhi hairaan hai Deen-e-haq pe sayyida ke laal ka ehsaan hai Khwaja-e-Ajmer ka hum ne suna farmaan hai Deen-e-haq pe sayyida ke laal ka ehsaan hai Faisla kar lo, Ujagar! Saath ho kis ke khade Hain husaini saf-ba-saf aur phir yazeedi aa gaye Karbala ka saj gaya lo aaj bhi maidan hai Deen-e-haq pe sayyida ke laal ka ehsaan hai Khwaja-e-Ajmer ka hum ne suna farmaan hai Deen-e-haq pe sayyida ke laal ka ehsaan hai
