Sare La Makan Se Talab Hui

Naat • Shayar: Ambar Warsi
Naat Khawan: Ghulam Farid Sabri, Hafiz Ahmed Raza Qadri, Rao Ali Hasnain, Aayat Arif
Sar-e-la-makan se talab hui Soo-e-muntaha wo chale Nabi Koi had hai un ke urooj ki Balaghal-ula bi-kamalihi Balaghal-ula bi-kamalihi Kashafad-duja bi-jamalihi Hasunat jameeu khisalihi Sallu alaihi wa aalihi Wahi la-makan ke makeen hue Sar-e-arsh takht-nasheen hue Wo Nabi hain jis ke hain ye makan Wo Khuda hai jis ka makan nahi Sar-e-arsh par hai teri guzar Dil-e-farsh par hai teri nazar Malkoot-mulk me koi shai Nahi wo jo tujh pe ayan nahi Wo Khuda ke noor ko dekh kar Bhi jahan walon me aa gaye Sar-e-arsh jaana kamal tha Ki wahan se aana kamal hai Rukh-e-Mustafa ki ye roshni Ye tajalliyon ki hamahami Ki har ek cheez chamak uthi Kashafad-duja bi-jamalihi Wo sarapa rehmat-e-kibriya Ki har ik pe un ka karam hua Ye kalam-e-pak hai barmala Hasunat jameeu khisalihi Ye kamal-e-khulq-e-Muhammadi Ki har ik pe chashm-e-karam rahi Sar-e-hashr naara-e-ummati Hasunat jameeu khisalihi Wahi haq-nigar, wahi haq-numa Rukh-e-Mustafa hai wo aaina Ki Khuda-e-pak ne khud kaha Sallu alaihi wa aalihi Mera deen, Ambar-e-Warsi! Ba-Khuda hai ishq-e-Muhammadi Mera zikr-o-fikr hai bas yahi Sallu alaihi wa aalihi Ai mazhar-e-noor-e-Khuda!, Balaghal-ula bi-kamalihi, Maula Ali mushkil-kusha, Kashafad-duja bi-jamalihi, Hasnain jaan-e-Fatima, Hasunat jameeu khisalihi, Yaani Muhammad Mustafa Sallu alaihi wa aalihi
