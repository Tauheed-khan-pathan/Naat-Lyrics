Mah-e-Taiba Nayyar-e-Batha Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam

Naat • Shayar: Mustafa Raza Khan
Naat Khawan: Hafiz Dr. Nisar Ahmed Marfani, Muhammad Adil Attari
Mah-e-Tayba Nayyar-e-Batha! Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam Tere Dam Se Aalam Chamka, Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam Tu Hai Mazhar-e-Rabb-e-Ajmal, Zill Hain Tere Saare Mursal Kaun Hai Hum-Sar Tera, Shaha! Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam Tu Hai Naib-e-Rabb-e-Akbar, Pyare Har Dam Tere Dar Par Ahl-e-Haajat Ka Hai Mela, Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam Har Shay Mein Hai Tera Jalwa, Tujh Se Raushan Din-o-Duniya Baanta Tu Ne Noor Ka Baada, Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam Tu Chahe Wo Jo Rab Chahe, Rab Chahe Wo Jo Tu Chahe Chaha Tera Rab Ka Chaha, Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam Jitne Salateen Pehle Aaye, Sikke Un Ke Ho Gaye Khote Jaari Rahega Sikka Tera, Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam Tere Ghar Ka Bachcha Bachcha Saara Gharana, Sayyid-e-Wala! Noori Moorat Noor Ka Putla, Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam Main Hoon Bebas Tu Hi Bas Hai, Main Hoon Bekas Tu Hi Kas Hai Kas Le Kamar Aur Bahr-e-Madad Aa, Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam Haal Hamara Jaisa Zaboon Hai Aur Wo Kaisa Aur Wo Kyun Hai Sab Hai Tum Par Raushan, Shaha! Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam Ant-al-Qasim Rabbuka Mu'ti, Tum Hi Ne Sab Ko Ne'mat Di De Do Mujh Ko Mera Hissa, Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam Hazir-e-Dar Hai Noori-e-Muztar, Aap Ka Ye Maurusi Sana-Gar Ibn-e-Raza Hai Khwahan Raza Ka, Sallallahu Alaika Wa Sallam
