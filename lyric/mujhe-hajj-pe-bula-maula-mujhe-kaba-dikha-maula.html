Mujhe Hajj Pe Bula Maula Mujhe Kaba Dikha Maula

Hamd • Shayar: Sohail Kalim Farooqi
Naat Khawan: Hafiz Ahmed Raza Qadri
kaa'be pe padi jab pehli nazar kya cheez hai duniya bhool gaya yoon hosh-o-khirad maflooj hue dil zauq-e-tamaasha bhool gaya pahuncha jo haram ki chaukhat tak ik abr-e-karam ne gher liya baaqi n raha phir hosh mujhe kya maanga aur kya bhool gaya jis waqt du'a ko haath uthe yaad aa n saka jo socha tha izhaar-e-'aqeedat ki dhun mein izhaar-e-tamanna bhool gaya mujhe haj pe bula, maula! mujhe kaa'ba dikha, maula! tu maalik hai mera, maula! main banda hoon tera, maula! mujhe haj pe bula, maula! mujhe kaa'ba dikha, maula! tha jis dam haath mein mere ghilaaf-e-khaana-e-kaa'ba the aansu aankh se jaari, thi lab pe ye sada, maula! mujhe haj pe bula, maula! mujhe kaa'ba dikha, maula! tawaaf-e-ka'batullah kar ke phir do nafl ada kar ke maqaam-e-multazam par ja ke maangoonga du'a, maula! mujhe haj pe bula, maula! mujhe kaa'ba dikha, maula! maqaam-e-ibraahim aayega to sar ko jhuka kar main nawaafil padh ke us ja par karoonga shukr ada, maula! mujhe haj pe bula, maula! mujhe kaa'ba dikha, maula! safa-marwa pe jab mere labon par hongi takbeerein mere sar par teri rehmat ki barsegi ghata, maula! mujhe haj pe bula, maula! mujhe kaa'ba dikha, maula! wo nau zilehaj ki hogi shaam jab jaaunga muzdalifa jahaan haaji ada karte hain maghrib aur 'isha, maula! mujhe haj pe bula, maula! mujhe kaa'ba dikha, maula! ye faarooqi ka maslak hai, 'aqeedat hai, 'aqeeda hai madeene ja ke bakhshi jaayegi har ik khata, maula! mujhe haj pe bula, maula! mujhe kaa'ba dikha, maula!
