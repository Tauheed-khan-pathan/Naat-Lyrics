Rasool-e-Pak Ke Shaida Mere Farooq-e-Azam Hain

Manqabat • Shayar: Muhammad Shauqeen Nawaz Shauq Fareedi
Naat Khawan: Hafiz Tahir Qadri, Hafiz Ahsan Qadri
salaaman ya ameer-al-momineen ! salaaman ya khaleefatul-muslimeen ! salaaman ya 'umar al-faarooq ! salaaman ya 'umar al-faarooq ! ameerul-momineen faarooq-e-a'zam ! ameerul-momineen faarooq-e-a'zam ! rasool-e-paak ke shaida mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain hai jin ka 'arsh par charcha, mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain rasool-e-paak ke shaida mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain baaqi hai tere naam ki tauqeer abhi tak phaili hai tere 'adl ki tanweer abhi tak ai hazarat-e-faarooq wazeer-e-sheh-e-kaunain ! hai qaafila-e-'ishq ka tu meer abhi tak darte hain tere naam se shaitaan ke chele hai tere ghazab mein wahi taaseer abhi tak gustaakh-e-nabi aaj talak kaanp rahe hain gardan nahin bhooli teri shamsheer abhi tak zaalim ke kaleje hain tere 'adl se larzaan hai zulm ke seene mein tera teer abhi tak islaam ki tehzeeb ko ghere hain yazeedi karbal mein hain is waqt ke shabbeer abhi tak tum ne jo lagaaya tha kabhi badr-o-uhud mein hai goonjta wo naa'ra-e-takbeer abhi tak naa'ra-e-takbeer ! allaahu akabar ! allaahu akabar ! assalaam assalaam imaam-e-'aadil ! assalaam assalaam imaam-e-'aadil ! aiwaan-e-adaalat pe t'assub ka hai qabza insaaf ke daftar mein hai taakheer abhi tak eesaar-o-shujaa'at ke gauhr pyaare 'umar hain wo naam hai e'zaaz ki taa'beer abhi tak jannat ki bashaarat unhein sarkaar ne bakhshi har samt chamakti hai wo tabsheer abhi tak hai zaat-e-'umar aa'dal-e-ashaab, fareedi ! beshak wo hain ik meer-e-jahaangeer abhi tak wo jin par bu-bakar ki bhi sadaaqat naaz karti thi wo jin par mere 'usmaan ki sakhaawat naaz karti thi 'ali sher-e-khuda ki jin pe quwwat naaz karti thi wo fakhr-e-sayyid-e-batha mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain hai jin ka 'arsh par charcha, mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain rasool-e-paak ke shaida mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain sahaabi ho 'umar jaisa, khaleefa ho 'umar jaisa jahaan pe hukmaraani ka tareeqa ho 'umar jaisa zamaana ab n dekhega koi bhi hukmaraan aisa jo 'aadil ho 'umar jaisa, jo sachcha ho 'umar jaisa ameerul-momineen faarooq-e-a'zam ! ameerul-momineen faarooq-e-a'zam ! jalaali the, nabi ke 'ishq mein sarshaar rehte the jidhar se bhi guzarate the shayaateen bhaag jaate the munaafiq naam sunte the to dar se kaanp jaate the tha aisa dabadba jin ka, mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain hai jin ka 'arsh par charcha, mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain rasool-e-paak ke shaida mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain samajh aata nahin kaise bataaun martaba un ka bulaate the jinhein faarooq keh kar sayyid-e-batha jinhein siddeeq-e-akabar ne banaaya ja-nasheen apna mila jin ko naseeb aisa, mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain hai jin ka 'arsh par charcha, mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain rasool-e-paak ke shaida mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain jo, ai shauq-e-fareedi ! mustafa ke dil ki hain chaaht shehnshaah-e-adaalat hain, khuda ki khaas hain ne'mat nabi ke 'aashiqon ke waaste allah ki hain rehmat muraad-e-sarawar-e-batha mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain hai jin ka 'arsh par charcha, mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain rasool-e-paak ke shaida mere faarooq-e-a'zam hain ameerul-momineen faarooq-e-a'zam ! ameerul-momineen faarooq-e-a'zam !
