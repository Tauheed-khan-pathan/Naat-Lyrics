Hazir Hai Dar-e-Daulat Pe Gada Sarkar Tawajjoh Farmaein

Naat • Shayar: Hafeez Taib
Naat Khawan: Owais Raza Qadri, Muhammad Asif Attari, Muhammad Arif Attari
Haazir Hai Dar-e-Daulat Pe Gada, Sarkaar! Tawajjoh Farmaaein Mohtaaj-e-Nazar Hai Haal Mera, Sarkaar! Tawajjoh Farmaaein Main Kar Ke Sitam Apni Jaan Par, Qur'aan Se "Jaaooka" Sun Kar Aaya Hoon Bahut Sharminda Sa, Sarkaar! Tawajjoh Farmaaein Main Kab Aane Ke Qaabil Tha, Rahmat Ne Yahaan Tak Pahunchaaya Sarkaar Pe Tan-Man-Jaan Fida, Sarkaar! Tawajjoh Farmaaein Aansoo Aansoo Hai Fariyaadi, Aur 'Arz-e-Karam Hichki Hichki Dhadkan Dhadkan Deti Hai Sadaa, Sarkaar! Tawajjoh Farmaaein Ik Main Hi Nahin, Poori Ummat, Saari Duniya, Saari Khalqat Takti Hai Rasta Rahmat Ka, Sarkaar! Tawajjoh Farmaaein
