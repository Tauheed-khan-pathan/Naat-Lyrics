Marhaba Ya Mustafa (Naseeb Chamke Hain Farshiyon Ke)

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Milad Raza Qadri
Marhaba ya mustafa! Marhaba ya mustafa! Naseeb chamke hain farshiyon ke Ki arsh ke chaand aa rahe hain Jhalak se jin ki falak hai raushan Wo shams tashreef laa rahe hain Marhaba ya mustafa! Marhaba ya mustafa! Nisaar teri chahal-pahal pe Hazaaron eiden rabi-ul-awwal Siwaae iblis ke jahaan mein Sabhi to khushiyaan mana rahe hain Marhaba ya mustafa! Marhaba ya mustafa! Shab-e-wilaadat mein sab musalmaan Na kyun karen jaan-o-maal qurbaan Abu Lahab jaise sakht qaafir Khushi mein jab faiz paa rahe hain Marhaba ya mustafa! Marhaba ya mustafa! Zamaane bhar ka ye qaaida hai Ki jis ka khaana usi ka gaana To nematen jin ki khaa rahe hain Unhin ke ham geet gaa rahe hain Marhaba ya mustafa! Marhaba ya mustafa! Jo qabr mein un ko apni paaun Pakad ke daaman machal hi jaaun Jo dil mein rah ke chhupe the mujh se Wo aaj jalwa dikha rahe hain Marhaba ya mustafa! Marhaba ya mustafa! Main tere sadqe zameen-e-tayba! Fida na ho tujh pe kyun zamaana Ki jin ki khaatir bane zamaane Wo tujh mein aaraam paa rahe hain Marhaba ya mustafa! Marhaba ya mustafa!
