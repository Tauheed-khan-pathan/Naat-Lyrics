Chamakne Lagi Hai Dulhan Ban Ke Dargaah, Ki Ashraf Mere Aaj Dulha Bane Hain

Manqabat • Shayar: Sayed Jami Ashraf
Naat Khawan: Ghulam Mustafa Qadri
