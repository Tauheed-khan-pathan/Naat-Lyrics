Haye Sham Ka Bazaar

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Nadeem Sarwar
haye shaam ka bazaar sajjad se rone ka sabab pooch raha hai jo shaam me guzri hai wo sab puch raha hai haye shaam ka bazaar woh lakhon tamashayi be parda phupi behne kaise na lahoo roye bemaar ki phir aakhein is gham ki aziyyat ne usay maar diya hai haye shaam ka bazaar kis tarha karaar aaye bemaar ko ay logon sar naizon pe pyron ke aate hai nazar jisko bejurm musafir ki ye kaisi saza hai haye shaam ka bazaar aankhon se hai khoon jaari ghamkhwar hai bemaari zanjeer hai pairon mein aur tauq bhi hai bhaari jalti hai zameen saari aur garm hawa hai haye shaam ka bazaar jageer nahi maangi zanjeer nahi badli saaye mein nahi baitha poshaak nahi badli maa behno ki khaatir wo rida maang raha hai haye shaam ka bazaar zindan ke masayeb bhi har waqt rulaate hai ashkon ko magar apne zainab se chupate hai mar jaaye na zainab ye kahin soch raha hai haye shaam ka bazaar zindan se reha hoke bhi zindan mein hai sajjad ek nanhi si turbat jo wahan karti hai faryaad mar kar bhi rahun qaid mein kya meri khata hai haye shaam ka bazaar ye zakhm bhi sajjad ko jeene nahi deta zakhmon se sakina ke jo chipka raha kurta wo khoon bhara kurta hi kafan uska bana hai haye shaam ka bazaar bazaar mein darbar mein zindan mein roye chaalis baras maut ke armaan mein roye rehaan mera maula tadapta hi raha hai haye shaam ka bazaar
