Qadira Sarwara Rahnuma Dast-geer Gaus-e-aa'zam Imaam-e-mubeen Be-Nazeer Tere Zer-e-qadam Auliya Asfiya Sarwar-e-Sarwaraa Peer-E-Roshan-Zameer Zikr Se Tere Tal Jaae Sab Mushkile Naam Se Tere Paae Rihaai Aseer Tu Jise Chaahe De, Jis Qadar Chaahe De Teri Bakhshish Niraali, 'Ata Be-Nazeer Kuchh Nahi Chaahte Tere Dar Ke Siwa Kaise Sultaan Hain Tere Dar Ke Faqeer Tu Ne Ahmad Raza Ko Yu Aa'la Kiya Ki Zamaane Me Milti Nahi Hai Nazeer Tu Hai Aa.eena-E-Seerat-E-Mustafa Tu 'Aleem-o-Khabeer-O-Basheer-O-Nazeer Teri Nisbat Hai Me'yaar-e-'izz-o-sharaf Kaise Adna Rahe Jis Ka Aa'la Ho Peer Ye Wazeefa Hai Har Dukh Ka Dramaa, Waqar !
