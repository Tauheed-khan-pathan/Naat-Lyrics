Dil Tadapne Laga Aankh Rone Lagi

Naat • Shayar: Saeed Akhtar Jokhanpuri
Naat Khawan: Saeed Akhtar Jokhanpuri
Dil Tadapne Laga, Aankh Rone Lagi Mustafa Jaan-e-Rehmat Ki Yaad Aa Gayi Jab Ghata Chhai, Barsaat Hone Lagi Mustafa Jaan-e-Rehmat Ki Yaad Aa Gayi Uljhano Ne Zamane Ki Uljha Diya Mere Apno Ne Bhi Mujh Ko Thukra Diya Duniya Ta'no Ke Nashtar Chubhone Lagi Mustafa Jaan-e-Rehmat Ki Yaad Aa Gayi Kaun Hamdard Hai Mera, Jaaun Kahan Sochta Tha Sunaaun Kise Daastaan Zindagi Gham Ke Moti Pirone Lagi Mustafa Jaan-e-Rehmat Ki Yaad Aa Gayi Fikr Ka Saara Paani Utarne Laga Gham Ke Dariyaon Se Main Ubharne Laga Kashti-e-Zindagi Jab Dubone Lagi Mustafa Jaan-e-Rehmat Ki Yaad Aa Gayi Ye Mohabbat Hai, Is Mein Tadapna Bhi Hai Der Hai Par Madine Ko Jaana Bhi Hai Thak Ke Jab Muflisi Ro Ke Sone Lagi Mustafa Jaan-e-Rehmat Ki Yaad Aa Gayi Ai Sa'eed!
