Khuda Nigehdaar Ya Ali

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Mir Hassan Mir
tha chehra zakhmi shikasta pehlu ajab qayamat ki wo ghadi thi nabi ki beti dame wasiyat ali se jab ro ke keh rahi thi khuda nigehdaar ya ali khuda nigehbaan ya ali ay mere maula chod ke tanha jaati hai ab duktare nabi khuda nigehdaar ya ali khuda nigehbaan ya ali gham tumhara sayyeda ke saath jayega qabr may tumhara gham mujhe rulayega pursa dene bhi tumhe koi na aayega shehre madina ghuth ke hai jeena haye ali bekasi teri khuda nigehdaar ya ali khuda nigehbaan ya ali ek wo din tha mai dulhan ban ke aayi thi aap ke ghar ne meri izzat badhayi thi milke mere saath ye basti basayi thi shaam se pehle aap ke ghar se fatema khuda ke ghar chali khuda nigehdaar ya ali khuda nigehbaan ya ali dushmane abu turab hai yahan tamam tum pe aanch aayegi phir bhi na ay imam fatema ne kar diya hai aisa intezam chand se bete sadqe tumhare ab yehi hai jholi may meri khuda nigehdaar ya ali khuda nigehbaan ya ali baba aaye thay abhi dobara aayenge keh rahe thay kalma go na ab satayenge fatema tumhe hum aaj le ke jayenge waada kiya tha baba ne jiska aa gayi hai ab wohi ghadi khuda nigehdaar ya ali khuda nigehbaan ya ali yaas se hai dekhte mujhe wo baar baar kar liya hussain aur hassan ko maine pyar maut kar rahi hai fatema ka intezar maa ko tadapte dekhe na bache saanse chal rahi hai aakhri khuda nigehdaar ya ali khuda nigehbaan ya ali ummate nabi ne mera haal ye kiya jalta dar batool par gira diya gaya taziyana bhi laga hai haath bhi utha pehlu shikasta baazu shikasta fatema to zakhm ban gayi khuda nigehdaar ya ali khuda nigehbaan ya ali zakhm dekh kar mere abhi se hai nidhaal maa ke gham may hogaya hai unka ghair haal baad mere betiyon ka rakna tum khayaal zainab o kulsum hai abhi masoom raahe shaam door hai abhi khuda nigehdaar ya ali khuda nigehbaan ya ali fatema pe hogayi sitam ki inteha aap se chupayi maine har jafa ghurbaton ka hai meri gawah mujataba kaise bataun kaise sunaun fatema kahan kahan giri khuda nigehdaar ya ali khuda nigehbaan ya ali dukhtare nabi ko qabr may utaar kar roye murtaza ali takallum is khadar ho gayi tamaam qabr aansuon se tar uthke qabr se jab ali chale thi yehi sada batool ki khuda nigehdaar ya ali khuda nigehbaan ya ali
