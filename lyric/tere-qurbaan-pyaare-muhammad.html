Tere Qurbaan Pyaare Muhammad

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Asghar Ali Bangash
Jis Ne Dekhe Nain Matwaale Tere Mast-o-Bekhud Ho N Ho To Kya Kare Ek Nazar Jo Dekh Le, Kehta Phire Tere Qurbaan, Pyaare Muhammad ! Gir Raha Hoon Mujhe Bhi Sambhaalo Apne Pyaare Nawaason Ka Sadqa Ya Nabi ! Meri Jholi Mein Daalo Tere Qurbaan, Pyaare Muhammad ! Gir Raha Hoon Mujhe Bhi Sambhaalo Koi Apna Nahin Roz-e-Mehshar Hai Yahan Nafs-o-Nafsi Ka 'Aalam Ham Gunhgaar Hain, Ya Muhammad ! Apni Kamli Mein Ham Ko Chhupa Lo Tere Qurbaan, Pyaare Muhammad ! Gir Raha Hoon Mujhe Bhi Sambhaalo Balghal-'Ula Bi-Kamaalihi Kashaf-Dduja Bi-Jamaalihi ह़sunat Jamee'u Khisaalihi स़llu 'Alaihi W Aalihi Aye Khatm-e-Rusool ! Makki-Madni ! Kaunain Mein Tum Sa Koi Nahin Aye Khatm-e-Rusool ! Tere Qurbaan, Pyaare Muhammad ! Gir Raha Hoon Mujhe Bhi Sambhaalo Noor Se Noor Milne Chala Hai Mustafa Se Khuda Kah Raha Hai Ye Hai Me'raaj Ki Raat, Pyaare ! Apne Chehre Se Parda Hata Lo Tere Qurbaan, Pyaare Muhammad ! Gir Raha Hoon Mujhe Bhi Sambhaalo Door Ho Jaayenge Sab Andhere Noor Hi Noor Aankhon Mein Hoga Dekhna Hai Agar Un Ka Jalwa Apne Dil Ko Madina Bana Lo Tere Qurbaan, Pyaare Muhammad ! Gir Raha Hoon Mujhe Bhi Sambhaalo Salle 'Ala Nabiyyena Salle 'Ala Muhammadin Tere Qurbaan, Pyaare Muhammad ! Gir Raha Hoon Mujhe Bhi Sambhaalo Ehsan Abbaas Khaan
