Mainun Shauq Madine Jaawan Da

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Haaji Abdul Habeeb Attaari
Mainun Shauq Madine Jaawan Da Mere Dil Wich Hasrat Hor Nayin Hun, Aaqa ! Teri Marzi e Saada Te Koi Zor Nayin Mera Roop Wi Nayin, Mera Rang Wi Nayin Mainun Yaar Manaawan Da Dhang Wi Nayin Mera 'Ishq Wi Roomi Warga Nayin Meri Jaami Wargi Tor Nayin Meri 'Aql Wi Nayin, Meri Shakl Wi Nayin Mainun Yaar Manaawan Da Dhang Wi Nayin Mera 'Ishq Wi Roomi Warga Nayin Meri Jaami Wargi Tor Nayin Mera 'Ishq Ye Kehnda, Hausla Rakh Aaqa Da Bulaawa Aawega Par Ki Kariye Be-Sabri Da Mere Dil Wichon Jaanda Chor Nayin Tere Nan De Tazkire 'Arshan Te Tere Husn De Charche Farshan Te Koi Than Nayin Aisi Jis Than Te Tere 'Ishq Da, Aaqa ! Shor Nayin Tere Zikr De Charche Farshan Te Tere 'Ishq De Charche 'Arshan Te Koi Aisi Ja Nayin Jis Ja Te Mere Aaqa ! Tera Shor Nayin Teri Qabr Te Haafiz Lakh Waari Payi Duniya Baale Lakh Deewe Je Dil Wich 'Ishq Nabi Da Nayin Teri Raushan Honi Gor Nayin Muhammad Talha Qaadri Qaari Shaahid Mehmood Qaadri
