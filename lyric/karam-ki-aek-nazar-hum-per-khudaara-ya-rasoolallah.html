Karam Ki Aek Nazar Hum Per Khudaara Ya Rasoolallah

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Karam Ki Aik Nazar Hum Par, Khudara Ya Rasoolallah Tum Hi Ho Be Saharaon Ka Sahara Ya Rasoolallah Tum Hi Ho Be Saharaon Ka Sahara Ya Rasoolallah Ghareebon Ke Hain Daata, Be Kason Ke Aap Waali Hain Mohabbat Aap Ki Hai Dil Mein, Lekin Haath Khaali Hain Idhar Bhi Apni Rehmat Ka Ishaara, Ya Rasoolallah Pukara Hai Madad Ko Ya Nabi, Hum Gham Ke Maaron Ne Tumhare Dar Pe Ja Kar Bheek Maangi Baadshahon Ne Hamein Bhi Aasra Hai Bas Tumhara, Ya Rasoolallah Bane Bigri Hamari, Hum Bhi Hain Qismat Ke Maaron Mein Tumhein Ummat Hai Pyaari Aur Tum Allah Ke Pyaaron Mein Bacha Lo Tootne Se Dil Hamara, Ya Rasoolallah Mera Imaan Hai Aashiq Ko Jab Bhi Gham Satate Hain Shehenshah-e-Jahaan Is Ki Madad Ko Pahunch Jaate Hain Kisi Ne Jab Kahin Se Bhi Pukara, Ya Rasoolallah Tarpata Hai Mera Dil Aur Barasti Hain Bahut Aankhen Madine Ke Nazaaron Ko Tarasti Hain Bahut Aankhen Bula Lo Ab Madine Mein, Khudara Ya Rasoolallah
