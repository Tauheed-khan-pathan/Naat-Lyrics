Behre deedar mushtaq hai har nazar Dono aalam ke sarkar aa jayiye Chandni raat hai aur pichla pahar Dono aalam ke sarkar aa jayiye Ab toh aa jayiye ab toh aa jayiye Sidratul muntaha arsho baagh-e-iram Har jaga par chuke hain nishane qadam Ab to ik baar apne ghulamon ke ghar Dono aalam ke sarkar aa jayiye Ab to aa jayiye ab to aa jayiye Mere gulshan ko ik baar mehkaiye Apne jalwon ki baarish mein nahalaiye Deeda-e-shauq ko kijiye parwar Dono aalam ke sarkar aa jayiye Ab toh aa jayiye ab toh aa jayiye Saamne jalwagar paikar-e-noor ho Munkiron ka bhi sarkar shak door ho Karke tabdeel ik din libaas-e-bashar Dono aalam ke sarkar aa jayiye Ab toh aa jayiye ab toh aa jayiye Shaam-e-ummeed ka ab sawera hua Suu-e-taiba nigahon mein dera hua Bichh gaye raah mein farsh qalb-o-jigar Dono aalam ke sarkar aa jayiye Ab toh aa jayiye ab toh aa jayiye Aakhari waqt hai ek bimaar ka Dil machal ne laga shauq-e-deedar ka Bujh na jaaye kahin ye charagh-e-sahar Dono aalam ke sarkar aa jayiye Ab toh aa jayiye ab toh aa jayiye Shaam-e-ghurbat hai aur, sheher khamosh hai Ek arshad akela kafan posh hai Khauf ki hai ghadi waqt hai purkhatar Dono aalam ke sarkar aa jayiye Ab toh aa jayiye ab toh aa jayiye
