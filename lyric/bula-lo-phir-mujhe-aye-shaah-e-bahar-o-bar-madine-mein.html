Bula Lo Phir Mujhe Aye Shaah-e-Bahar-o-Bar Madine Mein

Naat • Shayar: Ilyas Attar Qadri
Naat Khawan: Owais Raza Qaadri
Bula Lo Phir Mujhe, Aye Shaah-e-Bahar-o-Bar ! Madine Mein Main Phir Rota Huwa Aaun Tere Dar Par Madine Mein Main Pahunchun Ku-e-Jaanan Mein Gireban-Chaak, Seena-Chaak Gira De Kaash ! Mujh Ko Shauq Tadpa Kar Madine Mein Madine Jaane Waalo ! Jaayo Jaayo, Fi-Amaanillaah Kabhi To Apna Bhi Lag Jayega Bistar Madine Mein Salaam-e-Shauq Kehna, Haajiyo ! Mera Bhi Ro Ro Kar Tumhe Aaye Nazar Jab Rauz-e-Anwar Madine Mein Payaam-e-Shauq Lete Jaayo Mera, Qaafile Waalo ! Sunaana Daastaan-e-Gham Meri Ro Kar Madine Mein Mera Gham Bhi To Dekho, Main Pada Hoon Door Tayba Se Sukun Paayega Bas Mera Dil-e-Muztar Madine Mein N Ho Maayoos, Deewaano ! Pukaare Jaayo Tum Un Ko Bulaayenge Tumhe Bhi Ek Din Sarwar Madine Mein Bula Lo Ham Ghareebon Ko Bula Lo, Ya Rasoolallaah ! P-e-Shabbeer-o-Shabbar-Fatima-Haidar Madine Mein Khudaaya ! Waasita Deta Hoon Mere Ghaus-e-Aa'zam Ka Dikha De Sabz-Gumbad Ka Hasin Manzar Madine Mein Waseela Tujh Ko Bu-Bakr-o-'Umar, 'Usmaan-o-Haidar Ka Ilaahi ! Tu 'Ata Kar De Hamen Bhi Ghar Madine Mein Madine Jab Main Pahunchu Kaash ! Aisa Kaif Taari Ho Ki Rote Rote Gir Jaaun Main Ghash Kha Kar Madine Mein Nikaab-e-Rukh Ulat Jaye, Tera Jalwa Nazar Aaye Jab Aaye Kaash ! Tera Saail-e-Be-Par Madine Mein Jo Teri Deed Ho Jaye To Meri 'Yid Ho Jaye Gham Apna De Mujhe 'Yidi Mein Bulwa Kar Madine Mein Madine Jun Hi Pahuncha Ashk Jaari Ho Gaye Mere Dam-e-Rukhsat Bhi Roya Hichkiyan Bhar Kar Madine Mein Madine Ki Judaayi 'Aashiqon Par Shaaq Hoti Hai Wo Rote Hain Tadap Kar, Hichkiyan Bhar Kar Madine Mein Kahin Bhi Soz Hai Duniya Ke Gulzaaron Mein Baaghon Mein ? Faza Pur-Kaif Hai Lo Dekh Lo A Kar Madine Mein Wahan Ik Saans Mil Jaye Yehi Hai Zeest Ka Haasil Wo Qismat Ka Dhani Hai Jo Gaya Dam Bhar Madine Mein Madina Jannatul-Firdaus Se Bhi Aula-o-Aa'la Rasool-e-Paak Ka Hai Rauza-e-Anwar Madine Mein Chalo Chaukhat Pe Un Ki Rakh Ke Sar Qurbaan Ho Jaayen Hayaat-e-Jaawedaani Paayenge Mar Kar Madine Mein Madina Mera Seena Ho, Mera Seena Madina Ho Madina Dil Ke Andar Ho, Dil-e-Muztar Madine Mein Mujhe Neki Ki Daa'wat Ke Liye Rakhkho Jahan Bhi Kaash ! Main Khwaabon Mein Pahunchta Hi Rahun Aksar Madine Mein N Daulat De, N Sarwat De, Mujhe Bas Ye 'Saaadat De Tere Qadmon Mein Mar Jaaun Main Ro Ro Kar Madine Mein 'Ata Kar Do, 'Ata Kar Do Baqee'-e-Paak Mein Madfan Meri Ban Jaye Turbat, Ya Shah-e-Kausar !
