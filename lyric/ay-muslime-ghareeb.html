Ay Muslime Ghareeb

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Mir Hassan Mir
ay muslim e ghareeb kar rahi thi laasha e muslim pe ye zahra buka ya ali kufe may phir aayi hai rone sayyeda aap par jab ibne muljim ne kiya sajde may waar haath pehlu par rakhe yan aayi thi mai pehli baar aaj phir us haal may zahra ko hai aana pada kar rahi thi laasha e muslim pe ye zahra buka kufe waalon ne isay hai mil kar hai maara ya ali laasha e muslim hai aise paara paara ya ali jism hai saara mere bache ka zakhmon se bhara kar rahi thi laasha e muslim pe ye zahra buka kis tarha maine sambhala kis tarha thaama isay kya bataun mai khadi thi haath phailaye huwe daarul imara se jab tha laasha e muslim gira kar rahi thi laasha e muslim pe ye zahra buka kuch hi din may phir yahan aaungi mai roti hui iske bachon par chalegi jabke haaris ki churi mai sambhalungi unhe aa kar ruqayya ki jagah kar rahi thi laasha e muslim pe ye zahra buka dushmani aale nabi se is tarha dikhlayege yun mohamed aur ibrahim maare jayenge naam ka bhi paas rakhenge na kuch ahle jafa kar rahi thi laasha e muslim pe ye zahra buka kar rahe hai zulm ki tafseer ye chehro ke neel kat rahi hai aaj jo dar dar ye aulaad e aqeel hai yaqeenan ye mere bachon se ultaf ki saza kar rahi thi laasha e muslim pe ye zahra buka bayn thay akbar zaban e sayyeda par bas yehi saath zainab ke yahan phir mujhko aana hai abhi shaam tak le jayega kufe se gham ka silsila kar rahi thi laasha e muslim pe ye zahra buka
