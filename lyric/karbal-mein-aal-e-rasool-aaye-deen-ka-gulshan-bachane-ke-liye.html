Karbal Mein Aal-e-Rasool | Aaye Deen Ka Gulshan Bachane Ke Liye

Manqabat • Shayar: Ehsan Shakir Azmi
Naat Khawan: Ehsan Shakir Azmi
Karbal mein aal-e-rasool, baagh-e-sayyida ke phool Aaye deen ka gulshan bachaane ke liye Haidar ke lakht-e-jigar, kar ke tayba se safar Aaye deen ka gulshan bachaane ke liye Ek taraf the sirf behattar Ek taraf laakhon ka lashkar Aisi haibat thi taari, behattar laakhon pe bhaari Ik sabaq de diya hai zamaane ke liye Shabbeer peete hain jaam-e-shahaadat Ya karte hain rab ki 'ibaadat Sar pe 'azmat ka hai taaj, ran mein parhte hain namaaz Apne naana ki ummat bachaane ke liye Pyaas ka 'aalam, hont jo khole Jhoole se 'ali asghar bole Baaba ! Ab kar do qurbaan, meri nanhi si ye jaan Dil hai betaab ab teer khaane ke liye Maidaan mein 'ali akbar aaye Saare yazeedi ke dil tharraaye Ran mein garje jaise sher, kitne dushman ho gaye dher Dhunde dushman jageh munh chhupaane ke liye Yaum-e-jaza tak yaad karenge Shaakir ! Ham fariyaad karenge Mere Maula ! Kar ehsaan, ham bhi ho jaaen qurbaan Apne pyaare Nabi ke gharaane ke liye
