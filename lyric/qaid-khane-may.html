Qaid Khane May

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Nadeem Sarwar
haye sadaat haye zindan qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai dukhtar e fatema ghairat se muee jaati hai aasman door zameen sakht kidhar jaaun mai yehi behtar hai ke dunya se guzar jaaun mai bibiyon mil ke dua maango ke mar jaaun mai maut aati hi nahi jaan khichi jaati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai aamad e hind ka ghul itrat e shabbir mein hai shor e matam haram e saahib e tatheer mein hai kehti hai jaaun kahan paaon to zanjeer mein hai paaon uth'ta hai to zanjir lipat jaati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai tha idhar bibi sakina se ye zainab ka bayan hind darbar se jab aayegi suye zindan sabr tum ko hai dikhana na uthe shor o fughan haye ye din bhi aseeri tumhe dikhlaati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai dar e zindan pe hua itne mein amboh e kamaal zan e haakim ka hai zindan mein nuzool o ijlaal qaidiyon uth ke dua de ke karo istikbaal zauja e haakim e dauran hai jo ye aati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai hind ne dekh ke raandon ki taraf di ye sada kis ka yusuf hai ye zindan mein giraftar e bala bibiyan kis ki hai rakhti jo nahi sar pe rida un ke chehron se to kuch aur jhalak aati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai dekhti kya hai ke ek sher hai aahan mein niha laaghar o khastatan o faaqa kash o tashna dahan na to chalne ki saqat hai na koi rishta e jaan gustakhano se larazne ki sada aati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai aayi nazdeek jo sajjad ke hind e dilgeer boli wo kaunsi isyan ki mili ye taazeer bole dasween ki moharam ki jo pehni zanjeer aaj taqdeer bhi qaidi hamein kehlaati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai hind ne poocha maraz kya hai kaha bepadari ghar ko daryaaft kiya kehne lage darbadari boli leta hai khabar kaun kaha bekhabari jo khabar geer thay unko to lehad bhaati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai baba saahib ke gale par jo na khanjar phirta inke sar khulte na mai kaanton pe dar dar phirta aur madine ki taraf sab ko mai le kar phirta yaad hazrat ye watan ki hamein tadpati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai hind ne naam madine ka jo abid se suna khaak par baith gayi haal ye taaqeer hua boli kya zainab o kulsum se nisbat he bhala aise chehron se to khushboo e wafa aati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai boli zainab ke na le zainab o kusoom ke naam wo nabi zaadiyan hai qaid mein unka kya kaam hind boli inhi baaton mein hai sab unka kalaam kyun meri rooh tumhe dekh ke ghabraati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai jo bhi surat he wo masoom nazar aati hai khudrat e khaaliq e qayyum nazar aati hai koi zainab koi kulsum nazar aati hai un se taqdeer ye kis haal mein milwaati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai gird abid ke phiri phir wo ba haal e taagheer bole wo kaun ye chillayi kaneez e shabbir assalaam ay rasan o tauq o salaasil ke aseer nauha karti hui qadmon mein giri jaati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai bas dabeer ab ghame shabbir se larza hai jigar hind bhi mehwe aza aaj hui nange sar qaid khane mein hua seena zani se mehshar yehi majlis hai jo khud marsiya kehlati hai qaid khane mein talatum hai ke hind aati hai
