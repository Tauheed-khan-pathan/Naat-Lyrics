Dama Dam Mast Qalandar

Manqabat • Shayar: Unknown
Ho lal meri pat rakhiyo bala jhoole lalan Sindhri da sehvan da sakhi shahbaz qalandar Dama dam mast qalandar Ali dam dam de andar Char chiragh tere baran hamesha Panjva mein baran aai bala jhoole lalan Hind Sind pira teri naubat baje Naal baje ghadial bala jhoole lalan Har dam pira teri khair hove Nam-e-Ali beda paar laga jhoole lalan
