Ya Nabi Hum Ko Madina Kab Bulaya Jayega

Naat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Nadeem Raza Faizi
Dil Ko Dard-e-Hijr Mein Kab Tak Rulaaya Jaayega Ya Nabi ! Ham Ko Madina Kab Bulaaya Jaayega Aao Kirdaar-e-Nabi Ka Baab Phir Kholen Zara Aaj Phir Chulha Gareebon Ka Jalaaya Jaayega Faatima ! Ghar Par Tere Aaye Hain Jibreel-e-Ameen Lagta Hai Hasnain Ko Jhoola Jhulaaya Jaayega Ek Sajde Mein Shahaadat Kyun Hui Shabbir Ki Doosra Sajda Qayaamat Mein Nibhaaya Jaayega Ek Asgar Hi Nahin Haq Ki Hifaazat Ke Liye Saikdon Asgar Ko Hans Hans Ke Lutaaya Jaayega Dhaal Ban Kar Karta Hai Aulaad Ki Jo Parwarish Baap Ke Ehsaan Ko Kaise Bhulaaya Jaayega Baap Ki Le Le Jagah Jo Baap Ke Jaane Ke Baa'd Us Bade Bhaai Ko Palkon Par Bithaaya Jaayega Kaun Haathon Se Khilaayega Niwaala Ab Mujhe Maan ! Gusl-e-Kaa'ba Ke Liye Tum Ko Bulaaya Jaayega Aa'la Hazrat Ke Mishan Ka Jo Banega Pehre-Daar Deen-O-Millat Ka Habeeb Us Ko Banaaya Jaayega Dushman-e-Ahmad-Raza Bhaagega Maidaan Chhod Kar Jab Bareilly Waale Ka Naa'ra Lagaaya Jaayega
