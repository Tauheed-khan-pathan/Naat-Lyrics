Kis Se Faryaad Karun

Noha • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Hassan Abbas
khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada kis se faryaad karun dekhti reh gayi mai ghar mera barbaad hua kis se faryaad karun maine athara baras naazon se paala tha jise aaj roti hoon usay khaak zulfon mein hai aur khoon mein seena hai bhara kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada haye wo asghar e masoom bhi maidan ko gaya teer gardan pa laga mujhse dekha nahi jaata hai wo khaali jhoola kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada ran ko jaa kar jo alamdar na wapas aaya mera dil toot gaya mera bhai meri chaadar ka muhafiz na raha kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada khanjar e zulm jo mazloom ki gardan pa chala thi qayamat barpa qatl shabbir huwe hogayi zainab tanha kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada kaisi shaadi hui dulha jo bana ibne hassan aur kubra thi dulhan tukde dil hogaya dulha ka jo laasha aaya kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada garm reti pa mere bhai ka laasha tha pada sar tha naize pa chadha daalti jism pa hoti jo mere sar pa rida kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada aaiye baba ke ab aap ka ghar jalta hai sab ka dam ghut’ta hai kis ko awaaz doon ab kaun sahara dega kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada yehi kufa hai jahan ab bhi hai baba ka makan dar badar hum hai yahan sab hamay bhool gaye aisa zamana badla kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada daakhila shaam mein ek hashr bana mere liye sar hamare thay khule be-ridayi ke wo lamhaat ke dam ghut’ta tha kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada hum rasan basta thay aur takht pe tha baani e shar tasht mein bhai ka sar maine kin aankhon se wo dard ka manzar dekha kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada shimr ne aise sakina ka gala baandha hai uska dam rukta hai mujhse dekha nahi jaata hai tadapna uska kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada taziyano se jo abid ka badan hai zakhmi mera dil hai chalni aisi zanjeer ke gardan se lahoo behta tha kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada qaid mein haye sakina ne jo dam tod diya yaad aaye bhaiya khoon bhare kurte mein abid ne usay dafn kiya kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada zulm ki haar hui qaid se kunba choota gham magar saath raha ghar ki barbadi ka manzar meri aankhon mein raha kis se faryaad karun khwahare shaah tadap kar yehi deti thi sada jabke loote huwe asbaab ko maine dekha pairahan bhai ka
