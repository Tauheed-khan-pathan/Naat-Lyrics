Kaun Kehta Hai Ke Zeenat Khuld Ki Ach'chi Nahin

Naat • Shayar: Hasan Raza Khan
Kaun Kehta Hai Ke Zeenat Khuld Ki Ach'chi Nahin Lekin Ay Dil! Furqat e Koo e Nabi Ach'chi Nahin Rahm Kar Sarkaar Main Pursish Hai Aison Ki Bahut Ay Dil Ach'cha Hai Agar Haalat Meri Ach'chi Nahin Teera Dil Ko Jalwa e Maah e 'Arab Darkaar Hai Chawdhween Ke Chaand Teri Chaandni Ach'chi Nahin Kuch Khabar Hai Main Bura Hun Kaise Ach'che Ka Bura Mujh Buray Par Zaahido, Taa'na Zanni Ach'chi Nahin Us Gali Se Door Reh Kar, Kya Maren Ham Kya Jiyen Aah! Aisi Maut, Aisi Zindagi Ach'chi Nahin Un Ke Dar Ki Bheek Ch'hor'ay Sarwari Ke Waaste Un Ke Dar Ki Bheek Ach'chi Sarwari Ach'chi Nahin Khaak Un Ke Aastaane Ki Manga De Chaara-Gar Fikr Kya Haalat Agar Bimaar Ki Ach'chi Nahin Saaya e Diwaar e Jaanaan Mein Ho Bistar Khaak Par Aarazu e Taaj o Takht e Khusrawi Ach'chi Nahin Dard e 'Isyaan Ki Taraq'qi Se Huwa Hun Jaan Balab Mujh Ko Ach'cha Kijiye, Haalat Meri Ach'chi Nahin Zar'ra e Taibah Ki Tal'at Ke Maqaabil, Ay Qamar! Ghat'ti Bar'hti Chaar Din Ki Chaandni Ach'chi Nahin Mausam e Gul Kyun Dikhaaye Jaate Hain Ye Sabz Baagh Dasht e Taibah Jaayenge Ham, Rahzani Ach'chi Nahin Bekason Par Mehrbaan Hai Rahmat e Bekas Panaah Kaun Kehta Hai Hamaari Bekasi Ach'chi Nahin Banda e Sarkaar Ho, Phir Kar Khuda Ki Bandagi Warna Ay Banda, Khuda Ki Bandagi Ach'chi Nahin Roo Siyaah Hun Munh Ujaala Kar De Ay Taibah Ke Chaand Is Andhere Paakh Ki, Ye Teergi Ach'chi Nahin Khaar-haa e Dasht e Taibah Chubh Gaye Dil Mein Mere 'Aariz e Gul Ki Bahaar e 'Aarizi Ach'chi Nahin Subh e Mahshar Chaunk Ay Dil! Jalwa e Mahboob Dekh Noor Ka Tar'ka Hai Pyaare, Kaahili Ach'chi Nahin Un Ke Dar Par Maut Aa Jaaye, To Jee Jaawun Hasan Un Ke Dar Se Door Reh Kar Zindagi Ach'chi Nahin
