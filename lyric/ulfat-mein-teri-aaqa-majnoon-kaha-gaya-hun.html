Ulfat Mein Teri, Aaqa ! Majnoon Kaha Gaya Hun

Naat • Shayar: Maulana Waliullah Wali Qasmi Bastavi
Naat Khawan: Hafiz Sa'dullah Madani, Shabbir Ahmad Muzaffarpuri
Ulfat mein teri, aaqa ! majnoon kaha gaya hoon Deewaana tere dar ka, kehta suna gaya hoon Ulfat mein teri, aaqa ! majnoon kaha gaya hoon Jab se mili ghulaami, main surkh-ru hua hoon Main tera naam le kar aa'lam pe chhaa gaya hoon Ulfat mein teri, aaqa ! majnoon kaha gaya hoon Muddat se sah raha tha main to gham-e-judaai Rab ka karam ke tere roze pe aa gaya hoon Ulfat mein teri, aaqa ! majnoon kaha gaya hoon Main dil ko rakh ke, aaqa ! roze ki jaaliyon par Teri inaayaton ka qarza chuka gaya hoon Ulfat mein teri, aaqa ! majnoon kaha gaya hoon Mujh ko raah-e-wafa mein jo bhi mila hai raahi Deewaangi ka us ko rasta bata gaya hoon Ulfat mein teri, aaqa ! majnoon kaha gaya hoon Husn-e-nazar se jis ne roze ko tere dekha Aankhon mein us ki ban ke surma sama gaya hoon Ulfat mein teri, aaqa ! majnoon kaha gaya hoon Darbaar-e-mustafa ki mitti mili jo mujh ko Main to, wali ! usi ko tosha bana gaya hoon Ulfat mein teri, aaqa ! majnoon kaha gaya hoon
