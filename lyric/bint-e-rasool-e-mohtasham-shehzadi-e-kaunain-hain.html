Bint-e-Rasool-e-Mohtasham Shehzadi-e-Kaunain Hain

Manqabat • Shayar: Unknown
Naat Khawan: Muhammad Ashfaq Attari
Sayyida Zaahira! Tayyiba Taahira! Shehzadi-e-Kaunain hain! Shehzadi-e-Kaunain hain! Do jahaan ka chain hain! Shehzadi-e-Kaunain hain! Bint-e-Rasool-e-Mohtasham Shehzadi-e-Kaunain hain Zebaaish-e-Baag-e-Iram Shehzadi-e-Kaunain hain Waalid Nabi, Shauhar 'Ali, Bete Hasan aur phir Husain Majmoo'a-e-Khair-o-Ni'am Shehzadi-e-Kaunain hain Jin ke Hareem-e-Faiz se Khaali nahi lauta koi Wo Markaz-e-Jood-o-Karam Shehzadi-e-Kaunain hain Naam-e-Mubaarak Faatima ya'ni Jahannam se Najaat Paate gae jin ke Qadam Shehzadi-e-Kaunain hain Jin ko Rasool-e-Paak ne hai Bid'atum-minni kaha Koi nahi wo Laa-Jaram Shehzadi-e-Kaunain hain Nasl-e-'Ali az Faatima Nasl-e-Khitaamul-Mursaleen Sad Marhaba Kaan-e-Karam Shehzadi-e-Kaunain hain Kehte hain Jinn-o-Ins ye Nazrein Jhukaae Hashr mein Makhdooma-e-Har Khushk-o-Nam Shehzadi-e-Kaunain hain Mehshar mein mere paas to Bakhshish ka Saamaan kuchh nahi Daanish!
