Shah-e-Kaunain Ke Mangte Hain Ali Wale Hain

Manqabat • Shayar: Tafseer Raza Amjadi
Naat Khawan: Syed Abdul Qadir Al-Qadri
shah-e-mardaan, sher-e-yazdaan, quwwat-e-parawaradigaar la-fata illa 'ali, la-saif illa zulfiqaar 'ali maula 'ali ! 'ali maula 'ali ! shah-e-kaunain ke mangte hain, 'ali waale hain sadqa hasnain ka khaate hain, 'ali waale hain shah-e-kaunain ke mangte hain, 'ali waale hain padh ke ham naad-e-'ali ahl-e-jahaan ko har-dam apna deewaana banaate hain, 'ali waale hain shah-e-kaunain ke mangte hain, 'ali waale hain apni pehchaan bataate nahin ham chaaron taraf apna kirdaar bataate hain, 'ali waale hain bolo 'ali 'ali ! maula 'ali 'ali !
