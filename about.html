About Naat Lyrics

Naat Lyrics is a free website for reading Naat, Hamd, Salaam, Noha and Manqabat lyrics in Roman Urdu, Hindi and Urdu, all in one place.

The site holds over 370 lyrics and keeps growing. Every lyric opens on its own page, and you can search any line or word to find the kalaam you are looking for.

You can read each lyric in Roman, Hindi or Urdu, change the font size, and pick a font style you like. The Shuara (Poets) section brings together the kalaam of the great naat poets, from Ala Hazrat to the poets of today.

Poet names are shown where they are known. Where a poet is not known, the lyric is marked "Unknown". Lyric texts are presented as published; the site does not change a poet's kalaam.

Reading is free, with no account and no login needed.
